# Heparin Induced Thrombocytopenia
#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Overview
An autoimmune complication of exposure to heparin that causes the formation of antibodies against the heparin-PF4 complex. These IgG antibodies bind to and activate platelets, leading to widespread clot formation. Thrombocytopenia arises through this mechanism and also through consumption by splenic macrophages. 

About one-third to a half of patients with HITwill develop a venous, arterial, or microvascular thrombosis. 

Although the risk of HIT is much lower with LMWH vs. UFH (~0.1% cf. 1%), it is not non-existent, and still needs to be considered if there is an unexplained thrombosis or the platelet count drops.

#### 4T Score
The 4T score helps to stratify the risk of HIT:
- Thrombocytopenia - platelet nadir >20/nL is most predictive
- Timing - highest likelihood if within 5-10d of heparin exposure
  - Caveat: can be within 1d if prior heparin exposure in the last thirty days
- Thrombosis
- No alternative cause of thrombocytopenia

#### Vaccine-Induced Thrombocytopenia 
Closely related to HIT in that antibodies develop against PF4, with subsequent downstream activation of platelets via FcγRIIa. THis differs from HIT however in that there is no prior exposure to heparin, and the pattern of platelt reactivity does ot remonstrate heparin-dependence.
