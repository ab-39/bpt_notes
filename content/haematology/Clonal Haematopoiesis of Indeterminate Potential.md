### Overview
The term _clonal haematopoiesis of indeterminate potential_ refers to the presence of a population of cells derived from a mutated multipotent stem/progenitor cell harbouring a selective growth advantage in the absence of unexplained cytopenias, haematological canceres, or other clonal disorders. This can be thought of as an MGUS-analogy for [[myeloproliferative neoplasms]]

By definition, CHIP harbours somatic mutations of myeloid malignancy-associated genes. CHIP is associated with an increased risk of arterial and venous thrombosis, particularly the _JAK2, DNMT3A, TET2, and ASXL1_ mutations.
