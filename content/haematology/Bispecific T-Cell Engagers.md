# Bispecific T-Cell Engagers
#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Overview
BiTEs are new immune therapies for the treatment of haematologic malignancies that utilise antibodies with two separate antibody-binding domains:
- One targeted against CD3 (i.e. T-cells)
- One targeted against the antibody of choice for treatment:
  - CD19 -> blinatumomab -> used in multiple B-cell malignancies
  - CD20 -> glofitimab -> used in relapsed lymphomas (see the [_GOLDILOX_ trial](https://trials.cancervic.org.au/details/vctl_nct05833763))
    - Also epcoritimab, mosunetuzumab, etc. 
  - BCMA -> teclistamab -> multiple myeloma
