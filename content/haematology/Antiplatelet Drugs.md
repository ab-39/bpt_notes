### Overview 
Platelets have multiple receptors which serve various functions:
- GP VI - bind (subendothelial) collagen
- PARs - bind thrombin
- GP Ib-V-IX - bind vWF
- GP IIb-IIIa - bind fibrin
![](Antiplatelet%20Drugs/image%202.png)
The final common pahway of activation of most platelet receptors is an increase in intracellular calcium, leading to increased expression of phosphatidylserine and microvesicle release. Phosphatidylserine is critical for the binding of the coagulation factors.
