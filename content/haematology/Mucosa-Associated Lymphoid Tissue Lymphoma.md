# Mucosa-Associated Lymphoid Tissue Lymphoma
#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Overview
- Extra-nodal marginal zone B-cell lymphomas of the mucosa-associated lymphoid tissue
- Making up almost 50% of primary gastric lymphoma
- Generally tends to follow some chronic inflammatory insult, e.g.:
  - _H. pylori_ -> gastric MALT
  - _C. psittaci_ -> conjunctival MALT
  - Sjögren syndrome -> salivary gland MALT
  - Hashimoto disease -> thyroidal MALT
- Tends to be localised and reversible with treatment of underlying condition
- Commonly affects orbit -> local radiotherapy
