### Overview
Risk stratification is the name of the game:
|                  | Low                                            | Moderate                                                     | High                                                         |
|------------------|------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Mechanical valve | Bileaflet AV without AF<br>No other stroke RFs | Bileaflet AV + stroke RFs (CHADS)                            | MV prosthesis<br>Ball/disc prosthesis<br>Recent stroke/TIA   |
| AF               | CHADS2: 0-2                                    | 3-4                                                          | 5+, or:<br>Recent stroke/TIA<br>RHD                          |
| VTE              | VTE >1ya<br>No other RFs                       | VTE 3-12/12 ago<br>Non-severe thrombophilia<br>Recurrent VTE<br>Active cancer | VTE <3/12 ago<br>Severe thrombophilia (e.g. APLS, protein C/S deficiency) |

In general:
- Low-risk -> no bridging indicated
- Moderate -> consider bridging
- High-risk -> bridging indicated
