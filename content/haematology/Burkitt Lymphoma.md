# Burkitt Lymphoma
#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Overview
- An extremely aggressive and chemosensitive non-Hodgkin lymphoma
- Three epidemiological categories:
  - Endemic - in equatorial Africa, almost 100% EBV-associated
  - Sporadic - less commonly EBV-associated
  - Immunodeficiency-associated - particularly AIDS, ~40% EBV-associated
- Histology: ‘starry sky’
  ![](Burkitt%20Lymphoma/image%202.png)
- High-risk of tumour lysis
- High rates of extranodal disease - particularly acute leukaemia
- Cytogenetics:
  - t(8;14) -> activation of _myc_ oncogene on Chr 8 
  - Alternatively t(2;8) or t(8;22) -> _myc_ activation by light chain promoters 
- Treatment:
  - Intensive multi-agent chemotherapy -> high rates of remission and cure
  - e.g. R-CODOX-M-IVAC, GMALL
