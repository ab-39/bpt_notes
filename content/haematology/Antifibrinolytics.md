# Antifibrinolytics
#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Overview
The main antifibrinolytic drug available is **_tranexamic acid_**. This drug works by binding to the lysine binding site on plasminogen, preventing its binding to fibrin, and consequently preventing the degradation of fibrin. Other drugs in this class include aminocaproic acid and aprotinin.
