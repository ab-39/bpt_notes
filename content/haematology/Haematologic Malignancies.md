#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Overview
Lymphomas are neoplasms of mature lymphoid cells that arise in the lymphoid tissue, while leukaemias are neoplasms arising in the blood or bone marrow that can comprise either immature (acute) or mature (chronic) cells. The following is a vastly oversimplified summary of leukaemia and lymphoma. 
- - -
### Lymphoma
Lymphomas can be divided in many ways, but one conceptual framework is as follows:
1. Hodgkin vs Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma
If NHL:
2. B-NHL vs T-NHL
If B-NHL:
3. Aggressive B-NHL (e.g. DLBCL)
4. Indolent B-NHL (e.g. follicular NHL)
- - -
### Leukaemia
Leukaemias are classified according to lineage of the cells, and the proportion of immature cells present:
- Acute myeloid leukaemia
- Acute lymphoblastic leukaemia
- Chronic myeloid leukaemia
- Chronic lymphocytic leukaemia
