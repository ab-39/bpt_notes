# Autoimmune Haemolytic Anaemia
#bpt/haematology #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Warm AIHA
_Warm_ autoimmune haemolytic anaemia is a type of [[haemolysis]] mediated by IgG antibodies directed at RBC membrane antigens. Binding to these antigens marks that section of the membrane for phagocytosis by macrophages, leading to the characteristic finding of _spherocytes_ in the peripheral smear, as well as polychromatic macrocytes (= reticulocytes). Severe cases may show hyposplenic changes (e.g. Howell-Jollie bodies) and nucleated RBCs.
![](Autoimmune%20Haemolytic%20Anaemia/image.png)
The key differential here is inherited spherocytosis, and the key discriminatory test is the direct antiglobulin test, which is only positive in AIHA.
- - -
### Cold AIHA
_Cold_ autoimmune haemolytic anaemia is caused, in contrast, by IgM antibodies that promote RBC agglutination and complement-mediated haemolysis. These are often secondary to infectious causes, particularly EBV and _Mycoplasma_ infections, but may be caused due to clonal proliferation (“CHAD”). 
![](Autoimmune%20Haemolytic%20Anaemia/image%202.png)
The blood film will show agglutination, spherocytes, and polychromasia.
