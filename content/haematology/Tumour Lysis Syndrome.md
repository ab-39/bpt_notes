# Tumour Lysis Syndrome
#bpt/haematology #bpt/oncology
- - -
### Overview 
A quartet of:
- Hyperuricaemia
- Hyperuricosuria
- Hyperkalaemia
- Hyperphosphataemia (with subsequent hypocalcaemia)

Mechanism: cell lysis results in release of intracellular ions, nucleic acids, and proteins into the circulation.

Can be _biochemical_ i.e. derangements as above, or clinical, i.e. beginning to see:
- Seizures
- Tetany
- Nausea/vomiting/diarrhoea
- etc. 

Treatment = fluids, fluids, fluids

![](Tumour%20Lysis%20Syndrome/image.png)
Allopurinol:
- Inhibits XO -> reduces formation of new uric acid

Rasburicase:
- Converts uric acid to allantoin -> rapidly decreases uric acid
- Samples need to be sent on ice as if present it will continue to degrade uric acid
