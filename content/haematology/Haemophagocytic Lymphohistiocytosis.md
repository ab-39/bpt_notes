# Haemophagocytic Lymphohistiocytosis
#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Overview
* Life-threatening disorder of excessive immune activation
* Causes variable - often genetic in children or triggered by an infection, autoimmune condition, or malignancy in adults
  * Familial HLH genes: *PRF1, UNC13D, STXBP2, and STX11*
  * Genes encoding for granules: *RAB27A, LYST, and AP3B1*
  * Genes related to XLPD: *SH2D1A and XIAP*
  * Strong associated with EBV and T-cell malignancies
* Involved cell types:
  * Macrophages → activation leads to release of proinflammatory cytokines and engulfment of RBCs (hence, *haemophagocytosis*)
  * CD8+ T-cells → release of proinflammatory cytokines (IFNγ, TNF, IL-6), tissue necrosis
  * NK cells -> fail to eliminate activated macrophages 
- - -
### Diagnosis
Clinical Sx include: Fever, severe constitutional symptom, enlarged lymph nodes +/- hepatosplenomegaly, neurologic dysfunction, and abnormalities in multiple organ function tests.

Diagnostic criteria = HLH-2024, need ≥5 of:
1. Haemophagocytosis in the BM/spleen/lymph nodes
2. Serum ferritin ≥500μg/L
3. Hypofibrinogenaemia (fibrinogen ≤1.5 g/L) or hypertriglyceridaemia (triglycerides ≥3 mmol/L)
4. Elevated soluble IL-2 receptor (CD25) ≥2400 U/mL
5. Bi- or tricytopenia (platelets <100 × 109/L, hemoglobin <9 g/dL, absolute neutrophil count <1 × 109/L)
6. Splenomegaly palpable >2 cm below left costal margin
7. Fever (≥38.5ºC)

Old criterion that may be useful in establishing the diagnosis = low NK cell activity

The H-score is a valid alternative, featuring:
- Immunosuppression (up to +18pts)
- Temperature (up to +49pts)
- Organomegaly (up to +38pts)
- Cytopenias (up to +34pts)
- Hyperferritinaemia (up to +50pts)
- Hypertriglyceridaemia (up to +64pts)
- Hypofibrinogenaemia (up to +30pts)
- Transaminitis (up to +19pts)
- Haemophagocytosis (up to +35pts)

An H-score greater than 240 has a >99% probability of HLH, while an H-score <100 is associated with a <1% chance. The best cutoff value is a score of 169, with a sensitivity and specificity of approx. 90%.

### Management 
HLH-94 protocol recommended by ASH:
* Dexamethasone
* Etoposide (strong anti-T cell activity)
* Ciclosporin (anti-IL2, leading to reduced T-cell activation) - controversial, may cause PRES
* Salvage therapy:
  * Methotrexate
  * Ruxolitinib 
  * Alemtuzumab
  * Empalumumab

⠀Allogeneic HSCT has extremely good outcomes after initial remission is achieved with chemotherapy. Can be carried out with myeloablative or reduced intensity conditioning.
