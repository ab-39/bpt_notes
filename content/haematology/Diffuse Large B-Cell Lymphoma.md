# Diffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma
#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Overview
- Common - ~1/3rd of all adult NHL and 90% of all aggressive lymphoma
- Most common in middle age
- Extranodal involvement in 30% of cases - CNS, gastric, prostate, breast, etc. 
- B-type symptoms in 30% of cases
- Generally quite curible with multi-agent rituximab chemotherapy
- Histopathology:
  - Loss of folllicular architecture
  - Large transformed B-cells with prominent nucleoli, basophilic cytoplasm
    ![](Diffuse%20Large%20B-Cell%20Lymphoma/image.png)
- Immunohistochemistry:
  - CD19, CD20
  - CD22, CD79a
- High mitotic rate: Ki67 usually elevated
- Genetics:
  - _GCB_ -> good prognosis
  - _ABC_ -> bad prognosis
- Clinical presentation:
  - Generally related to mass effect
    - e.g. pain, oedema, etc.
  - May get B-type symptoms 
  - Generalised pruritis
- Prognosis:
  - R-IPI - ‘APLES’	:
    - Age >60y
    - Performance status (ECOG >1)
    - LDH increased
    - Extranodal involvement
    - Stage III/IV (Ann Arbor)
  - CNS-IPI = R-IPI + kidney/adrenal involvement
- Treatment = R-CHOP
  - Need to check hepatitis B serology prior to treatment 
  - Emerging = anti-CD79a (polatuzumab-vedotin)
  - Use interim-PET-adapted approach with scan after 2-4 cycles to plan ongonig therapy
  - Relapse #1 -> rituximab + gemcitabine based chemothreapy
    - +/- autoSCT
  - Thid-line now offering CAR-T and BitTEs
