# Aplastic Anaemia
#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Overview
Aplastic anaemia is an immun-mediated disorder of haematopoiesis that is characterised by pancytopenias and marrow hypocellularity. Key associated genetic conditions include Fanconi syndrome and telmeropathies. 

Treatment is generally with triple immunosuppressive therapy: eltrombopag, ATG, and CsA.
