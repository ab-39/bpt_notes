# Waldenström Macroglobulinaemia
#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Overview
- More properly called lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma
- Low-grade NHL
- Neoplasm of B-lymphocytes and plasma cells, characterised by circulating monoclonal IgM
- Very rare condition, median age at diagnosis = 71y
- Cytogenetics: point mutation in the _MYD88_ gene (L265P) -> increased B-cell signalling
  - CXCR4 mutation -> aggressive, poor response to BTKi
- Precursor state = IgM MGUS (does _not_ progress to mutiple myeloma)
- Clinical presentation:
  - ~1/3rd = asymptomatic
  - ~1/3rd = fatigue due to anaemia
  - ~1/3rd = lymphoadenopathy
  - Hyperviscosity symptoms are very rare - e.g. fatigue, blurred vision, drowsy, confused, etc.
  - May also present with B-type symptoms
  - Possible to develop demyelination secondary to IgM binding -> peripheral neuropathy 
- Treatment:
  - Indolent disease -> wait and watch
  - Symptomatic disease:
    - Fit -> R-Bendamustine
    - Less fit -> R-DC
  - Frail/elderly -> zanubrutinib
  - Supportive therapy important: iron, IVIG, albumin, etc.
