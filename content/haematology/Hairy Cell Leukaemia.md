# Hairy Cell Leukaemia
#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Overview
![](Hairy%20Cell%20Leukaemia/image.png)
- Neoplasm of small B-cells with ‘hairy’ cytoplasmic projections
- Rare, male predominant
- Median age at diagnosis = 63y
- Clinical presentation: infections, splenomegaly, cytopenias
- Immunophenotype:
  - CD19, CD20, CD11c, CD25, CD103
  - TRAP+
- Genotype: _BRAF_ V600E positive in almost all cases
- Treatment:
  - Cladribine +/- rituximab highly effective
  - First relapse -> retreat with cladribine + rituximab
  - Multiple relapses, consider:
    - BRAF inhibitors - e.g. dabrafenib
    - BTK inhibitors - e.g. zanubrutunib-
