# Venous Thromboembolism
#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Overview
General points on management:
- Acute proximal DVT (or PE) -> 3-6 months of anticoagulation
- This is then extended with full dose anticoagulation if there is an indication, e.g.:
  - Recurrent VTE
  - APLS
  - Active cancer
  - etc. 
- If the patient had an unprovoked event but wishes to continue anticoagulation, low dose anticoagulation can be used 
- Risk of recurrence varies widely depending on the circumstances of the VTE:
  - Provoked VTE by a transient risk factor -> 3% 5y recurrence rate
  - Provoked VTE by a persistent risk factor -> 45% 5y recurrent rate
  - First unprovoked VTE -> 30% 5y recurrence rate
  - Second unprovoked VTE -> 45% 5y recurrence rate
- DOACs are non-inferior to warfarin with respect to VTE recurrence or death, but safer with respect to major bleeding - this is the major rationale for their use, along with reduced monitoring requirements 
  - There are no head-to-head studies for individual DOACs
