# Mantle Cell Lymphoma
#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Overview
- Characterised by atypical small lymphoid cells in the mantle zones, around normal germinal centres
- Diagnosis requires one of:
  - Cyclin D1 nuclear expansion >90%
  - t(11;14) on cytogenetics or FISH
- Cell surface markers: CD19, CD20, CD5
  - Differences from [[Chronic Lymphocytic Leukaemia|CLL]]:
    - Strongly CD20+ (CLL is weakly CD20+)
    - CD23- (CLL is CD23+)
    - CD200-
- Mean age at diagnosis: 68y, generally men
- Usually presents in an advanced stage
- Subtypes:
  - Indolent -> splenomegaly, marrow involvement, circulating lymphoma cells
  - Aggressive -> lymphoadenopathy, B-type symptoms
- Treatment:
  - Indolent -> observe
  - Aggressive:
    - Young -> R-DHAOx + autoSCT
    - Elderly -> R-Bendamustine x6
      - Both cases followed by 2y rituximab maintenance
  - Relapsed -> BTK inhibitors appear superior to chemotherapy
