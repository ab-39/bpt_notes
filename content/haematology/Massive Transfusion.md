# Massive Transfusion
#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Overview
In the case of massive haemorrhage, _massive_ transfusion (historically defined as ≥10 units of blood) may be required. During an MTP, blood products are typically given in a 1:1:1 ratio:
- Plasma - usually FFP
- Platelets 
- Packed red cells 

Additional considerations:
- Low fibrinogen -> cryoprecipitate
- Obstetric haemorrhage -> TXA
