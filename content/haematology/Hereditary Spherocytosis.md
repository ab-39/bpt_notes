# Hereditary Spherocytosis
#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Overview
Hereditary spherocytosis is an autosomal dominant disorder of the red blood cell membrane that is caused by alterations of deficiencies of numerous membrane proteins, most importantly ***spectrin***, but also including:
- Band 3
- Protein 4.2
- Ankyrin
![](Hereditary%20Spherocytosis/image.png)
This leads to reduced deformability of the cell as small portions of the membrane detach, ultimately causing macrophage-mediated extravascular haemolysis in the cords of Bilroth. 

The consequences of this include:
- Anaemia
- Pigment gallstones (due to the elevated bilirubin, which can occur in other chronic haemolytic states - e.g. sickle cell disease)

Treatment with splenectomy is typically only indicated if there is severe symptomatic anaemia.
