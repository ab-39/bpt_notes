# Anticoagulants
#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Reversal 
Warfarin - Vit K +/- FFP +/- beriplex
Dabigatran - idarucizumab
Xa-inhibitors - andexanet alfa **or** prothrombinex 
- - -
### Drug Interactions
Dabigatran is transported in the bloodstream by p-glycoprotein, which is also bound by verapamil, amiodarone, and quinidine.
