### Overview
Post-splenectomy, we expect to see the following on the FBC/blood film:
- Howell-Jolly bodies (nuclear remnants)
- Target cells
- Spherocytes
- Leucocytosis
- Thrombocytosis
![](Splenectomy/image.png)

#### Causes of Acquired Hyposplenism
- Infarction
  - Sickle cell disease
  - Essential thrombocythaemia
  - Polycythmaemia vera
- Atrophy/hypofunction
  - Coeliac disease
  - IBD
  - Most autoimmune conditions
  - Irradiation
  - Bone marrow transplantation/GvHD
- Infiltration
  - Amyloidosis
  - Sarcoidosis
  - Leukcaemia
  - MPNs
