### Rouleaux
![](Abnormal%20Blood%20Film%20Findings/image.png)
‘Stacks’ of red cells, typically caused by elevated globulins (e.g. multiple myeloma, LPL), or fibrinogen (chronic inflammation). 
- - -
### Toxic Neutrophils
![](Abnormal%20Blood%20Film%20Findings/image%202.png)
Evidence of inflammation.
- - -
### Reactive Lymphocytes
Also, atypical lymphocytes. Abnormal CD8+ T-cells.
![](Abnormal%20Blood%20Film%20Findings/image%203.png)
Most commonly EBV, less commonly CMV or toxoplasmosis. 
- - -
### Autoagglutination
![](Abnormal%20Blood%20Film%20Findings/image%204.png)<!-- {"width":194} -->
‘Clumps’ of red cells - due to circulating IgM antibodies -> cold agglutinin disease, typically due to:
- EBV
- Mycoplasma
- Lymphoma

DAT will be positive for C3b.

Cold agglutinins + haemolytic anaemia = either:
- Primary cold agglutinin disease -> secondary to a particular type of lymphoma
- Secondary cold agglutinin syndrome -> e.g. secondary to infection, other lymphoma, etc.
