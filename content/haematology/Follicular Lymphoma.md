# Follicular Lymphoma
#bpt/haematology
- - -
### Overview
- Indolent B-cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma
- Median age at diagnosis = 65y
- Cell surface markers: CD10, CD19, CD20
- Proteins expressed: BCL2+, BCL6+
- Cytogenetics: t(14;18) -> BCL2+
- High clonal heterogeneity
- Clinical presentation: asymptomatic lymphadenopathy, B-symptoms rare but are an indication for treatment
- Histology shows a follicular pattern:
  ![](Follicular%20Lymphoma/image.png)
- Not curable with chemotherapy **but** high median survival (~20y)
- Progression: ~4% rate of transformation to DLBCL in first 10y
- Indications to treat = GELF criteria:
  - Nodes >7cm
  - 3+ nodes of 3+cm each
  - B-type symptoms
  - Splenomegaly >16cm
  - Compression of vital organs
  - Serous effusions
  - Lymphocytes >5
  - Cytopenias 
- Treatment:
  - Grade <3a = mostly mature centrocytes -> treat as indolent lymphoma
    - Generally involves radiotherapy 
  - Grade ≥3b = diffuse infiltrate of centroblasts -> treat as DLBCL
- Options:
  - First-line: generally anti-CD20 + CTx (usu. CVP)
  - Relapse: consider anti-CD20 + CHOP/bendamustine
  - Consider early [[autologous stem cell transplantation]] in young early progressors
- New agents:
  - Rituximab-lenalidomide
  - Zanubrutinib-obinutuzumab - mPFS = 28m
  - BiTAs - e.g. mosunetuzemab (CD20-CD3) - 77% PFS at 2y
  - CAR-T -  81% PFS at 1y
