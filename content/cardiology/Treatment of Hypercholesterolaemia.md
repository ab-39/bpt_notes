### Overview
There are several classes of lipid-lowering medications:
| Drug Class       | Example         | Drop in LDL | Increase in HDL | Drop in TG |
|------------------|-----------------|-------------|-----------------|------------|
| Statins          | Atorvastatin    | ++++        | ++              | ++         |
| Niacins          | Nicotinic acid  | ++          | ++++            | +++        |
| Resins           | Colestyramine   | ++          | +               | Neutral    |
| Fibrates         | Fenofibrate     | +/-         | +++             | ++++       |
| Ezetimibe        | Ezetimibe       | ++          | +               | +          |
| n-3 Ethyl Esters | Icosapent ethyl | Neutral     | +               | ++++       |
Additionally, the newer classes of medications include those that target _PCSK9_:
- Evolocumab - anti-PCSK9 monoclonal antibody
- Inclisiran - siRNA inhibiting PCSK9 production

### Statins
Statins inhibit HMG-coA-reductase, thereby inhibiting the hepatic synthesis of cholesterol, thus leading to increased expression of hepatic LDL receptors and thus increased clearance of LDL-c.

The most well known side-effect of statins is myopathy and myalgias. This may be overcome by opting for low-dose statins, alternate-day statins, hydrophilic statins (rosuvastatin, pravastatin, fluvastatin), or non-statin therapies. 

_Bempedoic acid_ also acts on the same pathway as statins (albeit upstream of HMGCR, on ATP-citrate lyase/ACL). It is a prodrug that requires the action of very-long-chain acyl-CoA synthetase 1 in the liver. Inhibition of ACL reduces hepatic cholesterol synthesis, which leads to upregulation of LDL-cholesterol receptors and increased clearance of circulating LDL cholesterol. Unlike statins, the isozyme that activates BA is _not_ present in the muscles, which reduces myopathy and related side effects. 
- - -
### Ezetimibe
Ezetimibe inhibits NPC1L1, which reduced absorption in the small intestine. This leads to reduced transportation of cholesterol to the liver, reeduction of hepatic cholesterol, and thus increased LDL receptor expression and LDL-c clearance. 

Ezetimibe is _incredibly_ efficient at reducing LDL-c. 
- - -
### PSCK9
PCSK9 is a molecule that binds to LDL-R and causes it to be degraded endosomally along with LDL-C. Inhibition of PCSK9 with monoclonal antibodies allows for increased recycling of LDL-R, up to 150 times in its lifecycle! 

Evolocumab is the sole PCSK9 inhibitor (although an RNA PCSK9-synthesis inhibitor, inclisiran, is also used) available on the PBS, as a 2-4 weekly SC injection that has minimal side effects. It reduces serum concentrations of VLDL and LDL, while increasing HDL.

### Hypertriglyceridaemia
Random serum TGs are the most predictive of cardiovascular events. Treat by:
1. Achieving LDL goal
2. Low fat diets
3. Weight reduction
4. Exercise
5. ***Fibrates***
6. Nicotinic acid
7. High-dose fish oil
8. Improving diabetic control
