### Fractional Flow Reserve
Recall that the positive predictive value of cardiac CT is relatively poor for coronary artery stenosis and thus ischaemia. So what is?
> Invasive coronary angiography with _fractional flow reserve_ 

This is the _drop_ in pressure across a lesion in the coronary arteries. An FFR <0.8 is considered haemodynamically significant stenosis that will result in ischaemia.
