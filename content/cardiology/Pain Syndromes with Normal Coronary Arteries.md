# Pain Syndromes with Normal Coronary Arteries
#bpt/cardiology
- - -
### Overview
Can basically be divided into three phenotypes:
- Microvascular disease
- Abnormal vasoconstriction in the microvasculature
- Abnormal vasoconstriction of the coronary arteries
  - The treatment for vasospastic angina is calcium channel blockers and nitrates
  - Medications to avoid:
    - Nonselective β-blockers
    - Triptans
    - 5-FU (if possible)
    - Aspirin
