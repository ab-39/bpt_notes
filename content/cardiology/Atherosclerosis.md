### Overview
CVD is Australia’s leading cause of mortality and morbidity, and atherosclerosis is typically the underlying pathology.

The _INTERHEART_ study established nine key risk factors for acute myocardial infarction, of which the Apo-B:Apo-AI (~LDL/HDL) ratio was the most important factor:
- Apo-B/Apo-AI
- Smoking
- Diabetes
- Hypertension
- Abdominal obesity
- Psychosocial stress
- Daily consumption of vegetables and fruits
- Exercise
- Alcohol intake
- - -
### Natural History
Atherosclerosis develops as a result of the interplay between the systemic factors described above with local factors - i.e. the points at which the vessels are most prone to haemodynamic stress. Thrombosis occurs in the coronary arteries at focal points, typically proximally and at sites of vulnerable plaque. The progression is as follows:
1. Adaptive intimal thickening
2. Infiltration of the intima with cholesterol-containing macrophages (foam cells)
3. Development of pools of extracellular lipid (disease progressive from hereon)
4. Development of a core of extracellular lipid
5. Thickening and formation of a fibrous cap
6. Rupture and thrombus formation
