### Overview
LDL-C is typically the surrogate used in studies for outcomes, and most evidence for lipid-lowering therapies is framed in terms of this. Options include:
- Statins
- Cholesterol absorption inhibitors - e.g. ezetimibe
- PCSK9 inhibitors - e.g. evolocumab, alirocumab
- PCSK9 siRNA synthesis inhibitors - e.g. inclisiran

here is no evidence for a lower-limit of LDL-C: the lower the better. That being said, there are still guidelines:
- Primary prevention -> ≤3.0mmol/L
- Primary prevention in the presence of atherosclerotic plaque -> ≤1.8mmol/L
- Secondary prevention -> <1.4mmol/L
