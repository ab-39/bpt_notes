### Overview
Syncope refers to transient loss of consciousness due to transient global cerebral hypoperfusion characterised by:
- Rapid onset
- Short duration
- Spontaneous complete recovery

The majority of syncope is _reflex_ (= vasovagal) or orthostatic - essentially relating to a problem with regulation of blood pressure. The remaining ~10-15% is generally due to arrhythmias:
- VT/VF
- High-grade AV block with ventricular standstill
- Sinus node disease -> sinus pauses
or structural heart disease:
- LVOT obstruction
- Severe pulmonary hypertension
- Major pulmonary embolus
