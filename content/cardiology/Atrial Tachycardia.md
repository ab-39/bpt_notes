# Atrial Tachycardia
#bpt/cardiology
- - -
### Overview
[[Supraventricular Tachycardia|Supraventricular tachycardia]] arising in the atria but _not_ from the SA node are referred to as atrial tachycardias. These are long RP tachycardias. These are grouped according to the locus at which they originate:
- Focal atrial tachycardia (AT) = single ectopic focus
  - ECG appears like sinus achycardia, but with a different P-wave morphology
  - Management is with medications or ablation 
- Multifocal atrial tachycardia (MAT) = multiple ectopic foci
  - ECG shows an irregular rhythm with at least three different P-wave morphologies visible on a standard ECG
