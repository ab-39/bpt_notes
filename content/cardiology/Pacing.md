### Sinus Node Dysfunction
The ESC 2021 guidelines recommend pacing for sinus node dysfunction if:
- The patient is symptomatic 
- Tachycardia-bradycardia syndrome
- - -
### AV Block
The ESC 2021 guidelines recommend pacing for high-grade AV block irrespective of symptoms, or for Wenckebach phenomenon with symptoms. 
- - -
### Other Indications
The ESC 2021 guidelines recommend pacing for neurocardiogenic syncope in patients aged >40y with severe, unpredictable, recurrent syncope who have any of:
- Sponteanous documented symptomatic asystolic pauses >3s or asymptomatic pauses >6s
- Cardioinhibitory carotid sinus syndrome
- Asystolic syncope udring tilt-table testing
