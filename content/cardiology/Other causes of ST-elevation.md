# Other causes of ST-elevation
#bpt/cardiology

1. LVH
2. Pericarditis
   1. Concave, diffuse ST-elevation
   2. Depressed PR-segments (elevated in aVR)
3. Early repolarisation
4. Brugada Syndrome
   1. ST-elevation in the anterior leads
   2. Saddle shaped
   3. No slurring in the S wave to distinguish from RBBB
