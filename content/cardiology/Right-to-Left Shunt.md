# Right-to-Left Shunt
#bpt/cardiology 
- - -
### What is right to left shunt?
Right to left shunt is a term describing any situation in which deoxygenated blood from the right heart is able to bypass the pulmonary vasculature and enter the systemic circulation via the left heart. 
- - -
### Causes of right to left shunt
Anatomic:
- Cardiac - Eisenmenger syndrome (ASD, VSD)
- Intravascular shunt - pulmonary AVM

Physiologic:
- Capillary - e.g. fluid, pneumonia
- Alveolar filling disorders
- Complete airway obstruction
- - -
### Findings in right to left shunt
- Hypoxaemia
- Widened [[respiratory/Alveolar-Arterial Gradient|alveolar-arterial gradient]]
- Classically non-responsive to supplemental oxygen therapy

An uncommon consequence of right to left shunt is the platypnea-orthodeoxia syndrome. ([Platypnea-orthodeoxia syndrome](https://litfl.com/platypnea-orthodeoxia-syndrome/))
