# Kallman Syndrome
#bpt/endocrinology #bpt/genetics
- - -
### Overview
A distinctive dyad of hypogonadotrophic hypogonadism and anosmia with a significant male predilection. Most commonly due to defects int he _ANOS1_/_KAL1_ gene on the X-chromosome. This leads to a failure of migration of GnRH and olfactory neurons during embryonic development that often presents in puberty. Associated abnormalities include:
- Midline defects
- Renal agenesis
- Sensorineural hearing loss
- Abnormal eye movements
- Synkinesia 

MRI reveals lack of the olfactory bulbs. Hormone replacement (typically testosterone) is used to induce puberty, and spermatogenesis can also be induced if desired/
