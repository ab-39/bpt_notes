### Overview
The SCAs are a group of over forty hereditary ataxias chcaracterised by degeneration of the cerebellum +/- spinal cord. Although the genetic mechanism for all types is not known, there are a few common principles:
- Tend to be autosomal dominant
- Many (>12) are associated with repeat expansions
- Display anticipation

The symptoms of SCA typically set on in the fourth to fifth decade of life, and include:
- Progressive ataxia -> unsteady gait often the first symptom
- Coordination difficulties/loss of fine motor skills
- Dysarthria and dysphagia
- Oculomotor abnormalities
- Various non-ataxic neurological symptoms

Diagnostic testing for the SCAs requires specific testing, as exome sequencing cannot always detect repeat expansions, which require additional panels.
