# Fragile X Syndrome
#bpt/genetics
- - -
Definition: an ~[X-linked dominant](https://next.amboss.com/us/article/y50d5g#Zbe29ecc6f40ec6d12a9e6a54afc7416b)~ disease caused by a CGG ~[trinucleotide repeat expansion](https://next.amboss.com/us/article/y50d5g#Zf0e70424f991434ae37250803aa096dc)~ in the FMR1 gene (~[fragile X mental retardation 1 gene](https://next.amboss.com/us/article/240TiT#Zf13ffb053116c029036adbf241dc6812)~) during ~[oogenesis](https://next.amboss.com/us/article/O60IOS#Ze691f09022653bc54941bce3fa0f8714)~. This leads to its hypermethylation, which silences the ~[gene](https://next.amboss.com/us/article/y50d5g#Z94dd69013513c2616894ef6dbe01d837)~ and renders it unable to express its physiological ~[gene](https://next.amboss.com/us/article/y50d5g#Z94dd69013513c2616894ef6dbe01d837)~ product - leading to intellectual disability, ataxia, [[primary ovarian insufficiency]], and tremor. 
- - -
Fragile X syndrome demonstrates anticipation -> can be transmitted from males to males.
