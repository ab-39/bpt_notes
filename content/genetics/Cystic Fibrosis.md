### What is cystic fibrosis? 
Cystic fibrosis is a multisystem disease caused by an autosomal recessive mutation in the _CFTR_ gene on chromosome 7. While there are over 2,000 known variants of _CFTR_, the one most commonly associated with disease is F508del.

One of the roles of the CFTR protein is to function as a transmembrane chloride pump. If the pump is nonfunctional, it leads to a buildup of Cl- in the cell. Subsequently:
- Electrochemical gradient established
- ENaC channels pump Na+ into the cell
- High solute concentration within cell
- Water follows into cell
- End result is that secretory ducts (particularly in lungs and GIT) become dried out, leading to thickened, viscous secretions 

Improvement in survival in CF in the past decades can be attributed to:
- specialist care
- neonatal screening 
- antibiotics
- treatment of malabsorption 
- improved nutritional status
- PT/exercise training
- mucolytics
- lung transplantation 
- surgical management of meconium ileus

### Epidemiology
CF is the most common genetically inherited life-limiting condition in Caucasians, with a disease prevalence of 1/2500 — 1/3200 and a carrier prevalence of 1/25-30. It is also prevalent in other ethnicities but at orders of magnitude less.

It is an **autosomal recessive** condition.

### Diagnosis of Cystic Fibrosis 
The diagnosis of CF can be confirmed in several ways:
1. Presentation at birth with meconium ileus
2. Heel prick test looking for immunoreactive trypsinogen
   - Highly sensitive but less specific 
3. Sweat test:
   - [Cl-] > 60mmol/L
   - Borderline: 40-60mmol/L
4. Genetic testing
   - Difficult given how many alleles there are, commonly screen for the 50 most common 

The clinical features of CF are varied, and include:
- Chronic productive cough
- Sinusitis
- Nasal polyposis
- Bronchiectasis
- Digital clubbing
- Pancreatic insfufficiency
- Diabetes
- Recurrent pancreatitis
- Cirrhosis
- Bilateral absence of the vas deferens
- Subfertility
- Osteoporosis
- Aquagenic wrinkling of the hands (see below)

![](Cystic%20Fibrosis/image%202.png) 

To make the diagnosis, a patient must have clinical features or a family history of CF, along with one of:
- _Two_ known CF-causing mutations
- Positive chloride sweat tests of _two_ occasions
- Nasal potential difference tracing typical for CF

> [!TIP] Mnemonic
> Correctors have a tick (Z) and a cross (X) in their names - tezacaftor and elexacaftor

**Criteria for referral to lung transplant unit**
- FEV1 < 0.35 of predicted (or precipitous decline)
- Increased frequency of IV ABx
- pO2 < 65mmHg
- pCO2 > 50mmHg
- Colonisation with resistant organisms
- Impaired quality of life

### Complications of Cystic Fibrosis
- Meconium Ileus
- Distal Intestinal Obstruction Syndrome
  - Impaction of secretions at ileo-caecal junction-> RIF mass (may mimic appendicitis)
  - Faeces in small bowel on AXR
- GORD
  - Very common
  - PPI or fundoplication helpful 
- Rectal prolapse/adult intussusception 
- Bone disease
  - q2y DXA
  - Bisphosphonates
- Fertility and Infertility
  - Men: absent vasa deferentia (infertile)
  - Females: dehydrated secretions -> fallopian tube adhesions (sub-fertile)
  - Genetic counselling important 
- Diabetes
