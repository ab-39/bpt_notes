# Reproductive Options
#bpt/genetics #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
Couples with a personal or family history of a genetic condition should ideally be fully counselled regarding the risk to any children. The outcome then depends on the risk and the couple’s own views.

#### Natural Course - Do Nothing
Typically when the genetic variations involved carry a low risk to children. Involves not pursuing any pregnancy testing options. Sometimes also the case if a particular genetic/chromosomal variant is unknown.

#### Sperm/Egg Donation
Similarly does not involve pregnancy testing options. Usually the case if a particular genetic/chromosomal variant is unknown.

#### Detailed Anomaly Scans
Similarly does not involve pregnancy testing options. Usually the case if a particular genetic/chromosomal variant is unknown and when structural anomalies are known to be part of the syndrome.

#### Invasive Prenatal Sampling
Two options:
1. Chorionic villous sampling - available from 11/40. Involves sampling the placenta. 1-2% risk of miscarriage.
2. Amniocentesis - available from 16/40. Involves sampling the amniotic fluid. 0.5-1% risk of miscarriage.

#### Pre-Implantation Genetic Diagnosis
Like IVF, but with genetic testing. Generally private.

- Spinal muscular atrophy
