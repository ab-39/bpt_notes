### Overview
| Condition                                                    | Affected Gene     | Repeat | Chromosome | Pattern of Inheritance | Clinical Features                                            |
|--------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------|--------|------------|------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| [[neurology/Huntington Disease|Huntington Disease]]                                       | _HTT_             | CAG    | 4p         | AD                     | Chorea, akinesia, dementia, death                            |
| [[Fragile X Syndrome]]                                       | _FMR1_            | CGG    | X          | XLR                    | MVR, large genitalia, intellectual disability, ataxia        |
| [[neurology/Myotonic Dystrophy|Myotonic Dystrophy]]                                       | _DMPK_            | GTG    | 19         | AD                     | Cataracts, myotonia, progressive weakness; cardiomyopathy, premature hair loss, myotonia, arrhytmia, gonadal insufficiency, diabetes |
| [[neurology/Inherited Causes of Cerebellar Ataxia#Friedreich Ataxia\|Friedreich Ataxia]] | _Frataxin_        | GAA    | 9          | AR                     | Ataxia, dysarthria, diabetes, cardiomyopathy                 |
| Kennedy Disease (see [[Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis#Variants of ALS|Variants of ALS]]) | Androgen receptor | CAG    | X          | XLR                    | Onset age 20-60, progressive peripheral and bulbar weakness; gynaecomastia, azoospermia, androgen resistance |

> [!TIP] Mnemonic
> - Huntington = CAG = Chorea, Akinesia, Grotesque behaviour
> - Fragile X = CGG = eXtra-large Chin, Giant Genitalia
> - Myotonic dystrophy = CTG = Cataracts, Thinning hair, Gonadal atrophy
> - Friedreich ataxia = GAA = GAit is Ataxic
