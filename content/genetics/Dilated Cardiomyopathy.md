### Overview
DCM is one of the [[cardiology/Inherited Cardiomyopathies|inherited cardiomyopathies]] which is characterised by ventricular enlargement with impaired contractility in the absence of an underlying ischaemic cause. Although these can be asymptomatic for many years, patients often present with heart failure, arrythmias, conduction disease, or even systemic emboli from a ventricular thrombus. 

The genetic diagnostic yield is quite low, at about 25-40% of cases. The most common variants are truncating variants in the _TTN_ gene.
