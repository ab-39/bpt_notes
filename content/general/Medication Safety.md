### Classification of ADRs
ADRs are an interplay of _dose_, _time_, and _susceptibility_. 

ABCDE:
- Augmented -> related to the mechanism of action of the drug, common and predictable -> dose reduction
- Bizarre -> idiosyncratic and unpredictable -> cessation (e.g. drug allergy)
- Continuing -> develop with chronic use (e.g. amiodarone)
- Delayed -> develop after cessation (e.g. IRAEs, also fall under augmented)
- End of Use -> e.g. withdrawal 

As we can see, there is often overlap.

We often use the Naranjo criteria to determine how likely it is that a reaction is due to a medication. A score greater than 9 indicates a definite ADR, while a score greater than 5 indicates a possible ADR.
