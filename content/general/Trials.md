### Respiratory
1. PEITHO - thrombolysis in PE: In patients with intermediate-risk pulmonary embolism, fibrinolytic therapy prevented hemodynamic decompensation but increased the risk of major hemorrhage and stroke.
2. WISDOM - ICS in COPD: Among patients with severe COPD whose symptoms were stable while receiving triple therapy (tiotropium, salmeterol, and fluticasone), tapering off the inhaled corticosteroid (ICS) portion of therapy was not associated with an increase in the rate of COPD exacerbations. Withdrawal of ICS was associated with a modest decrease in the absolute FEV1 of uncertain significance
3. MOPETT - thrombolysis in PE: in patients with moderate PE, treatment with thrombolysis plus anticoagulation was associated with a reduction in pulmonary hypertension when compared with anticoagulation alone. 
4. ULTIMA - CDT in PE: Ultrasound-assisted catheter-directed thrombolysis using fixed-dose rtPA is superior to heparin alone in improving RV function in patients with submassive PE. Appears to be safer with respect to bleeding but study not adequately powered.
5. CAPE-COD - steroids in CAP: among patients with severe community acquired pneumonia requiring ICU or intermediate care unit admission, hydrocortisone lowers all-cause mortality at 28 days when compared to placebo.

### Cardiac 
1. TOPCAT - spironolactone in HFpEF: in patients with HFpEF spironolactone reduced hospital admissions but not mortality when compared with placebo. 
2. ARISTOTLE - apixaban in AF: in patients with non valvular atrial fibrillation and at >1 risk factor, apixaban is associated with a greater reduction in rates of stroke or systemic embolism (1.27% vs 1.60%) while having a lower rate of major bleeding (2.13% vs 3.09%).
3. OPTION - LAAC in AF: in patients with AF, LAAC is non inferior to anticoagulation in preventing stroke, with significantly lower risk (HR = 0.55) of major bleeding. 
4. RIVAL - radial access in PCI: in patients with ACS undergoing angiography, radial access is non-inferior to femoral access with respect to death, MI, stroke, and non-CAGS related bleeding, but associated with significantly lower vascular complications.

### Renal
1. CORAL - stenting in RAS: renal artery stenting does not improve renal or cardiac clinical outcomes in patients with hypertension or chronic kidney disease due to atherosclerotic renal artery stenosis.

### Infectious Diseases
1. MERINO - antibiotic choice in ESBL-producing GNB bacteraemia: among patients with extended-spectrum beta-lactamase *E. coli* or *Klebsiella pneumoniae* bacteremia, piperacillin-tazobactam was _not shown to be non-inferior_ (i.e. was worse) to meropenem for the treatment of E. Coli or Klebsiella pneumoniae bacteremia resistant to ceftriaxone (i.e., ESBL).

### Neurology
1. DAWN - MT in stroke: clot removal via endovascular thrombectomy reduces disability in select stroke patients presenting up to 24 hours after symptom onset.
2. ORATORIO - ocrelizumab in PPMS: in patients with PPMS, ocrelizumab was associated with lower rates of clinical and MRI progression than placebo.
