# NEJM Updates
#bpt/general
***
### 26 Jun 25
#### FGF21 Agonism in MAFLD
Afruxafirmin, a bivalent FGF21 analogue, did not significantly reduce fibrosis in patients with MAFLD when compared with placebo. 

#### Encorafenib-cetuximab in CRC
In patients with BRAF V600E mutated colorectal cancer, treatment with encorafenib-cetuximab and CTx was associated with improved PFS and OS when compared to standard treatment alone. 
***
### 19 Jun 25
#### PD1 Inhibition in MMR-Deficient Locally Advanced Rectal Cancer
Among patients with locally-advanced MMR-deficient rectal cancer, PD1 inhibition with dostarlamab reduced the need for surgery. 

#### CD19-cell Depletion in Myasthenia Gravis
Among patients with myasthenia gravis, inebilizumab, a CD19-targeting B-cell depleting agent, caused greater reductions in the MG ADL score when compared with placebo. ADRs included cough, headache, and UTIs. 
***
### 12 Jun 25
#### PDEIVb Inhibition in IPF
In patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, treatment with neurandomolast, a phosphodiesterase IVb inhibitor was associated with significantly smaller decline in FVC over a period of 52 weeks when compared with placebo. The most frequent adverse event was diarrhoea. 

#### GLP1/Glucagon Agonism in Overweight
Among adults with obesity or overweight associated with complications, treatment with mazdutide, a dual GLP1 and glucagon agonist was associated with clinically significant reduction in weight and improvements in pre-specified cardiac and renal parameters. ADRs were predominantly related to GI upset. 

#### AAV-Mediated Gene Therapy in Haemophilia B
In a cohort of patients with haemophilia B, a single infusion of an AAV-vector encoding a codon-optimised factor IX transgene lead to durable factor IX expression, sustained clinical benefit, and no late onset safety concerns over a period of 13y. 
*** 
### 5 Jun 25
#### GLP1R Agonism in MAFLD
Among patients with MAFLD and fibrosis, treatment with semaglutide was associated with significant reductions in fibrosis when compared with placebo. 

#### Biopsy Strategies for Peripheral Lung Nodules 
Among patients with peripheral lung nodules, navigational bronchoscopy provided more accurate results and fewer side effects when compared with transthoracic needle biopsy. 

#### Adjuvant Nodal Irradiation in Breast Cancer
In patients with N1 breast cancer with nodes proven cancer-free following neoadjuvant chemotherapy, the addition of adjuvant regional nodal irradiation did not decrease the risk of invasive breast cancer recurrence or death from breast cancer.
