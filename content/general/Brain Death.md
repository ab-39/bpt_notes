# Brain Death
#bpt/general
- - -
### Overview
Diagnosing brain death in Australia requires first the full exclusion of other causes of reduced conscious state (e.g. hypoglycaemia, hypothermia, etc.), and then **all three of**:
- Absence of brain reflexes to all stimuli including pain (i.e. coma)
- Absence of brainstem reflexes
- Apnoea 

See the [ANZICS position statement](https://www.anzics.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/Brain-Death-Determination-Statement-FINAL.pdf).
