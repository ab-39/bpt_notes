# Acute-Angle Closure Glaucoma
#bpt/general
- - -
### Overview
An ocular emergency characterised by a dramatic increase in intraocular pressure that leads to pain, nausea, and decreased visual acuity. The ‘classic’ examination findings include:
- Red eye
- Ciliary flush
- Corneal haziness 
- 
![](Acute-Angle%20Closure%20Glaucoma/Angleclosureglaucoma.jpg.jpeg)

Diagnosis is confirmed with an IOP >22mmHg. Definitive treatment is with surgery (usually an iridotomy), but medical therapy (e.g. acetazolamide) can be used in the first instance and may significantly improve symptoms.
