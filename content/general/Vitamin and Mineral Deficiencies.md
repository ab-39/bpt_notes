### Fat-Soluble Vitamins
| Vitamin | Dietary Sources                                             | Manifestations                                               | Epidemiology                                                 |
|---------|-------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| A       | Oily fish, dairy, carrots, leafy greens, etc.               | Xerophthalmia, night blindness, keratomalacia, follicular hyperkeratosis  | Generally a problem in the developing world (and Albury)     |
| D       | Oilly fish, eggs, milk, sun exposure                        | Ricketts, osteomalacia                                       | Linked to a wide range of diseases (e.g. MS)                 |
| E       | Plant oils, animal fats, nuts, seeds, vegetables, wheatgerm | Neurological disorders, ataxia                               | Possible role in preventing atherosclerosis; associated with abetalipoproteinaemia |
| K       | Leafy greens, fruits, liver                                 | Coagulation defects, haemorrhagic disease of the newborn     | Given to children at birth                                   |

### Water-Soluble Vitamins
| Vitamin         | Dietary Sources                                              | Manifestations                                               | Epidemiology                                                 |
|-----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| B1 (thiamine)   | Many foods, often fortified<br><br>Key exception is polished rice | [[neurology/Wernicke-Korsakoff Syndrome|Wernicke-Korsakoff Syndrome]], dry beri-beri (peripheral neuropathy), wet beri-beri (peripheral oedema) | Strong association with alcohol dependency; beri-beri develops when the majority of calories come from polished rice |
| B3 (niacin)     | Plants, meat, fish<br><br>Lost from grains when the bran is removed | Pellagra: dermatitis, dementia, and diarrhoea                | Generally present only in communities where maize is the chief foodstuff (e.g. parts of Africa). This has _not_ traditionally been a problem in the Americas due to the process of nixtamalisation |
| B6 (pyridoxine) | Many foods                                                   | Peripheral neuropathy                                        | Typically only occurs in the context of isoniazid therapy    |
| B9 (folate)     | Many animal and plant foods                                  | Megaloblastic anaemia                                        |                                                              |
| B12 (cobalamin) | Dairy, meat, eggs                                            | Megaloblastic anaemia, subacute combined degeneration of the cord, dementia, haemolytic anaemiaA |                                                              |
