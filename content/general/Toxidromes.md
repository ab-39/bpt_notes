# Toxidromes
#bpt/general #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
A **toxidrome** is a *toxi*c syn*drome*, attributable to a given toxin.
- - -
### Sympathetic
‘Flight, fright, fight’
- - -
### Muscarinic
‘SLUDGE’ or ‘DUMBELS’:
- Diarrhoea
- Urination
- Miosis
- Bradycardia
- Bronchorrhoea
- Bronchospasis
- Emesis
- Lacrimation
- Salivation
- - -
### Nicotinic
Commonly seen with nicotine and some pesticides.
***
### Anticholinergic
Typically anti*muscarinic* specifically:
- _Mad_ as a hatter - altered conscious state
- _Blind_ as a bat - mydriasis
- _Red_ as a beet - plethora
- _Hot_ as a hare - anhidrosis, tachycardia
- _Dry_ as a bone
