### Bernoulli Equation
Fundamentally, this investigates the fact that when a fluid flows into a smaller vessel, its speed increases:
> For a fluid in a vessel, the sum of pressure, kinetic energy per unit volume, and gravitational potential energy per unit volume is constant 

In notation:
> $P + 0.5ρv^2 + ρgh$ = constant

Where:
- P = pressure
- ρ = fluid density
- v = velocity
- g = g
- h = height 

Now assuming that we have a fluid flowing at a constant height, this can be rearranged to give:
> $P1 + 0.5ρv1^2 = P2 + 0.5ρv2^2$
> $ΔP = 0.5ρ(v1^2 - v2^2)$

This shows that the change in pressure is proportional to the square of the velocity of the fluid flowing through the orifice. For practical echocardiography, this is often simplified to:
> $ΔP = 4v^2$

### Law of Laplace
In fluid dynamics, the law of Laplace relates tension to pressure and the radius of a tube:
> The pressure inside a vessel is directly proportional to the tension of the wall, and varies inversely as the radius of the vessel

The medical (over)simplification of this gives:
> $P = 2T/r$

### Pouseille’s Law 
Put simply, Pouseille’s law, or the Hagen-Pouseille equation, states that for liquids obeying laminar flow:
> The volume flow rate of a fluid is given by the pressure difference by the viscous resistance

Where the viscous resistance itself varies inversely as the radius to the fourth power. Representing this mathematically gives:
> $Q = πΔpr^4 / 8μL$

Where:
- Q = volumetric flow rate
- Δp = pressure differential across the length of the pipe
- r = radius 
- μ = viscosity
- L = length of the pipe

### Ohm’s Law
> $V = I * R$

Pressure is related to the product of flow and resistance.

### Fick’s Law of Diffusion
Simplified, Fick’s law of diffusion relates the volume of gas diffusing across a barrier (as a rate) to the surface area, diffusivity of the given gas/barrier interaction, thickness of the barrier, and partial pressure difference of the gas across the barrier:
> $V_{gas} = {(A/T) * D * (P_1 - P_2)}$

Rearranging this gives:
> $(A*D)/T = V_{gas} / (P_1 - P_2)$

Where the term $AD/T$ is denoted $D_{L_x}$ - the diffusing capacity of substance X.
