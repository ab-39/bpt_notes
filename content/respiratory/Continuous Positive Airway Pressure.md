### What is CPAP?
CPAP is the provision of a constant level of airway pressure to splint the upper and lower airways.

The key clinical indications are:
- Acutely:
  - [[COPD]] - acute exacerbations 
  - Congestive Cardiac Failure
- Chronically:
  - OSA 
- - -
### Role in [[Obstructive Sleep Apnea]]
CPAP is the highest evidence and most efficacious treatment for OSA, but requires good adherence to ≥4h/night. The best predictor of adherence to CPAP is adherence in the 2 week to 3 month period. 

CPAP reduces the risk of all complications of OSA, but there is no evidence from RCTs that there is a reduction in mortality. The key evidence for this comes from the RICCADSA and SAVE trials, which were limited by the fact that patients were non-sleepy. Importantly, CPAP _does_ reduce the risk of MVA.
