### What is chylothorax?
Chylothorax is an accumulation of chyle, a lymphatic fluid, in the pleural space. It is characterised by a milky-white pleural effusion, and defined by the identification of chylomicrons in the pleural fluid sample. 

It is most commonly caused by lymphomatous malignancies, then other malignancies invading the thoracic duct, then trauma/iatrogenesis. Interesting causes include filariasis, TB, and sarcoidosis. There is a case report from Brazil of chylothorax being caused by paracoccidioidiomycosis.
