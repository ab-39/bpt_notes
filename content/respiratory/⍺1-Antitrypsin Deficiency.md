# ⍺1-Antitrypsin Deficiency
#bpt/gastroenterology #bpt/respiratory
- - -
### Overview
A rare condition caused by autosomal co-dominant mutations in the _SERPINA1_ gene. This leads to an inability to degrade several proteins, that causes disese via two mechanisms:
1. COPD - due to failure to inhibit neutrophil elastase
   - Typically in a basal distribution with panacinar involvement 
2. Liver disease - due to an accumulation of AAT polymers in the liver (where AAT is synthesised)

There are multiple genotypes, of which:
- PI*ZZ = most common severe genotype with liver and respiratory involvement
- PI*null/null = severe respiratory involvement but no liver involvement (due to absence of AAT)
- PI*SZ/MZ = milder phenotypes - vulnerable to damage from smoking 
- PI*MM = normal 

Diagnosis is with measurement of serum AAT levels: >1g/L (~50% of normal) appears to be protective against the effects of neutrophil elastase in the lung. AAT is an acute phase reactant so should be paired with a serum CRP.
