# NIV in NMD
#bpt/respiratory #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
Good evidence for NIV reducing mortality and morbidity. 
- Improves QOL
- Reduces intercurrent infections
- Maintains gas exchange
- Reduces symptoms of chronic hypoventilation

General consensus is to initiate NIV if there are signs of:
- symptoms of sleep-disordered breathing
- daytime sleepiness
- cognitive impairment
- nocturnal arousals
- hypoventilation 

Seems to be worse morbidity if initiated early/prophylactically in DMD.
