# Indications for Intubation
#bpt/respiratory #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Include:
- Need to secure airway
- Depressed sensorium (usually GCS < 8)
- Depressed airway reflexes
- Upper airway instability after trauma
- Decreased airway patency
- Need for sedation in the setting of poor airway control
- Imaging/transport of the unstable patient
