### Uses
The uses of NIV have been clearly established for:
- AECOPD
- Cardiogenic pulmonary oedema (ERS/ATS guidelines: https://publications.ersnet.org/content/erj/50/2/1602426)
- Weaning from invasive ventilation
- Chest wall disease/NMD
- Elderly patients with CAP
- Palliation (GOC-B2)
