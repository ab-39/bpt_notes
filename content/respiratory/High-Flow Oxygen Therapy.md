- - -
### What is HFNP?
Unlike low-flow nasal cannulae, high-flow offers humidified air through the nose at rates in excess of 40L/min, while also offering a little (~as much as 6cmH2O). 
- - -
### Use in Hypercapnic Respiratory Failure
Evidence:
- Some evidence for prevention of acute exacerbations
- No improvement in mortality
- No improvement in 6-minute walk test

_but_, this is a poor quality study.
