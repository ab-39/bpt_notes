# Indications for Ventilation
#bpt/respiratory #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Remember…
The indications for ventilation, from a physical standpoint, are the same irrespective of the mode of ventilation (i.e. NIV, mechanical, etc.), the decision to use mechanical ventilation is often made if there are [[indications for intubation]]. 
- - -
### Include:
- Hypoxia/acute hypoxaemic respiratory failure
- Hypoventilation
- Unacceptably high work of breathing
- Haemodynamic compromise
- Cardiorespiratory arrest
- Refractory shock
- Raised ICP (hyperventilation to reduce CO2) 
- Flail chest
