#bpt/respiratory #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### The final line…
The final line for respiratory failure is to ‘dialyse’ off the carbon dioxide using ECMO.

Indications for ECMO include:
- ALI/ARDS
- Aspiration
- Pneumonia 
- Asthma
- Post-lung transplant
- Lung contusion
- Post-cardiac arrest
- PE
- Drug overdose
- Post-cardiac surgery
- Bridge to transplant
- Post-cardiac transplant
- Cardiogenic shock
