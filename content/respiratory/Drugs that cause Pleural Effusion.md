# Drugs that cause Pleural Effusion 
#bpt/respiratory #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### List
Some drugs implicated in pleural effusion include:
- nitrofurantoin
- dasatinib (and other Y-kinase inhibitors)
- amiodarone
- methotrexate
- clozapine
- phenytoin
- immunotherapy
- ovarian stimulation
- hydralazine
- procainamide
