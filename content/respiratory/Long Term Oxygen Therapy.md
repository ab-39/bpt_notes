### COPD
Nocturnal oxygen therapy: RR of death = 1.94.
Otherwise, daytime O2 therapy has no effect on days worked, hospitalisation, RBC mass, or pulmonary haemodynamics. 
**But** - physically active patients with COPD have lower mortality, and seem to be a different cohort from those who require O2. Patients who intermittently desaturate during exercise _also_ do not benefit from intermittent O2 therapy.
