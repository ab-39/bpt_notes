### Overview
Insomnia is a persistent difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep, or attaining restorative sleep, which affects daytime functioning and quality of life. 
- Chronic insomnia is insomnia for at least three nights a week, persisting for at least three months 

This is a common condition that affects one in three people. The best evidence for treatment is for cognitive behavioural therapy.
