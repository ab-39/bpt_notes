### Overview
Monoclonal antibodies are used to target specific molecules with therapeutic intent. Nomenclature gives us clues as to the origins and structure of the molecules:
- _-omab_ -> murine antibody = 100% mouse protein -> high risk of reactions
  - In general, the more murine protein present, the more these antibodies are able to induce antibody-dependent cell-mediated cytotoxicity 
- _-uximab_ -> chimaeric antibody = ~34% mouse protein
  - e.g. rituximab
- _-uzumab_ -> humanised antibody = ~10% mouse protein
  - e.g. alemtuzumab
- _-umumab_ -> fully human antibody = 100% human protein
  - e.g. ofatumumab
