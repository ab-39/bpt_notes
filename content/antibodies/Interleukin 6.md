### Overview
IL-6 is a pleiotropic cytokine produced by T-cells, macrophages, and endothelial cells that is a potent inducer of local and systemic inflammation, and which plays a key role in the acute phase response including the induction of fever. 

The ⍺ chain of the IL-6 receptor is targeted by the monoclonal antibody _tocilizumab_, that is PBS indicate for:
- Juvenile idiopathic arthritis
- Rheumatoid arthritis 
- [[Giant cell arteritis]]
- COVID-19
