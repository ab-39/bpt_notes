### Overview
IL-17 plays an important role in host defence through the mobilisation and activation of neutrophils. Pathological IL-17A responses lead to the development of autoimmunity, and can be treated with an inhibitory monoclonal antibody: _secukinumab_. 

Secukinumab is indicated in psoriasis/psoriatic arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, and possibly in [[multiple sclerosis]]. There is no evidence for benefit in [[Crohn disease]].
