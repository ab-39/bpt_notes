# Mechanisms of Metastasis
#bpt/oncology
- - -
### Overview
What is required to metastasise?
1. Invasion
2. Intravasation
3. Circulation
4. Extravasation
5. Colonisation
![](Mechanisms%20of%20Metastasis/image.png)

There are two key routes of spread:
- Haematogenous
- Lymphatic - metastasis can be arrested at this stage

Additionally to this, gastrointestinal and ovarian cancers can seed directly into the peritoneal cavity - _transcoelomic spread_. Some cancers can also be spread iatrogenically during interventions.
