# Malignant Hypercalcaemia
#bpt/oncology
- - -
### Overview
A common complication of cancer, most often due to:
- PTHrP = humoral hypercalcaemia - ~80%
- Osteolytic metastases
- Ectopic PTH secretion
- Vitamin D dysregulation 

The clinical features are classic - bones, groans, moans, and time on the throne. 

IV bisphosphonates are the cornerstone of management for malignant hypercalcaemia. The most evidence is for the use of zoledronic acid. Adjunctive therapy can include:
- Normal saline
- Calcitonin
- Loop diuretics
- Steroids
- Calcimimetics
- Dialysis
