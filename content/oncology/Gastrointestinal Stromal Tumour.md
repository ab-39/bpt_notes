# Gastrointestinal Stromal Tumour
#bpt/oncology
- - -
### Overview
A very rare type of mesenchymal tumour arising in the GI tract, characterised by staining positive for CD117/C-kit (or, less commonly PDGFR mutants). Diagnosis is based on the presence of _spindle cells_ on histology. Treatment is generally dependent on extent:
- Localised -> surgery
- Advanced disease -> high sensitivity to imatinib
- Disease progression -> ==🟢escalate dose== **or** sunitinib, regorafenib, ripretinib

This is a rare example of a tumour where the dose can be escalated.
