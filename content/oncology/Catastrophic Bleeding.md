# Catastrophic Bleeding
#bpt/oncology
- - -
### Overview
A significant and often lethal complications of many malignancies, commonly haematologic or large head and neck cancers. Defined as a bleed that has the capacity to cause death within minutes. Identifying sentinel bleeds and planning accordingly is of critical importance. 

In general, if a catastrophic bleed occurs, management is supportive:
- Positioning to avoid aspiration/suffocation
- High dose benzodiazepines - e.g. midazolam 10mg
