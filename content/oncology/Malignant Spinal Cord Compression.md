# Malignant Spinal Cord Compression
#bpt/oncology
- - -
### Overview
A bona fide medical emergency, where early identification and treatment is required to prevent neurologic deterioration and save the possibility of ambulation.

Most commonly secondary to:
- Prostate cancer
- Breast cancer
- Lung cancer
- Multiple myeloma

Key signs/symptoms include:
- New, progressive severe back pain
- Motor deficits
- Bowel or bladder impairment - late sign

Prompt imaging with an MR whole spine (or CT myelogram if unavailable) is required to evaluate for multi-segment compession.

Treatment is two-step:
1. Symptomatic and preventive care:
   - High-dose steroids -> dexamethasone 16mg daily
   - Pain control
   - Management of urinary retention and constipation
2. Definitive treatment, either:
   - Surgical decommpression
   - External beam radiotherapy - dose proportionate to underlying prognosis
