# Leptomeningeal Carcinomatosis
#bpt/oncology
- - -
### Overview
A rare but devastating complication of advanced cancer, leptomeningeal carcinomatosis involves seeding of disease to the pia and arachnoid matres. It is frequently associated with breast cancer, lung cancer, and melanoma. Leptomeningeal carcinomatosis is classically multifocal, with the most common symptoms/signs being headache (39 percent), nausea and vomiting (25 percent), leg weakness (21 percent), cerebellar dysfunction (17 percent), altered mental status (16 percent), diplopia (14 percent), and facial weakness (13 percent)CT is a poorly sensitive tool, while MRI can often reveal multiple areas of abnormal enhancement in the leptomeninges.
