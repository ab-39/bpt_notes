# Cancer Screening
#bpt/oncology
- - -
### Overview
The best evidence for cancer screening exists for:
- Breast cancer - NNTS (age 60-69y) = 476
- Lung cancer - NNTS (among smokers) = 320
- Bowel cancer - NNTS (age >59) = 492
  - Largest effect on mortality as this is the only screening program offered to the whole population
- Cervical cancer - NNTS = 1140
  - Number artificially increased by low population prevalence of cervical cancer in the post-Gardasil era
