# Postpartum Depression
#bpt/obstetrics #bpt/psychiatry #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
Postpartum depression is very common, affecting up to 20% of new mothers. In most cases, it is comorbid with anxiety. The highest risk is in the first year postpartum.
- - -
### Postpartum Psychosis
Much rare - 1-2/1000 pregnancies. Correlates with unmanaged postpartum depression.
