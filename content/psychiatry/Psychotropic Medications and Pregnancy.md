# Psychotropic Medications and Pregnancy

### Mood Stabilisers
- Overall recommendation is to continue maintenance therapy to minimise the risk of mood episodes 
- Most teratogenic = valproate and carbamazepina
- Generally safe to continue = lamotrigine
- Lithium does increase the risk of congenital malformations (esp. Ebstein anomaly) but the overall risk is small - in general, if a patient is on lithium it can be continued 
- First- and second-generation antipsychotics are generally considered safe in pregnancy, for mood stabilisation purposes quetiapine or risperidone is generally the best option
