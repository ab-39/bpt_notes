### Overview 
[[Cytokines]] play a critical role in host defence, but can lead to tissue damage if excessive responses occur, and are also ultimately responsible for the development of  autoimmunity. 

Recall that IL-1, IL-6, and TNF⍺ play a critical role in the [[initiation of the immune response]], while antigen presenting cells also harvest foreign material to activate the adaptive immune system.
