### Wiskott-Aldrich Syndrome
Caused by a defect in the _WASP_ gene. Triad of eczema, thrombocytopenia, and immune dysfunction.
- - -
### Ataxia-Telangiectasia
Caused by a defect in the _ATM_ gene leading to impaired DNA repair.
- - -
### DiGeorge Syndrome
Also called velocardiofacial syndrome. One of the [[chromosomal disorders]] caused by a chromosome 22q11.2 deletion leading to embryologic abnormalities with the third and fourth pharyngeal pouches. Causes a syndrome of thymic defect leading to an absolute deficiency of T-cells, as well as several characteristic abnormalities including:
- Cardiac abnormalities - include Fallot’s tetralogy, VSD, ASD
- Facial dysmorphism - prominent nasal bridge, dysplastic ears, micrognathia
- Cleft palate
- Hypoparathyroidism

> [!TIP] Mnemonic
> A handy mnemonic is **CATCH-22**:
> - Cardiac abnormalities
> - Anomalous face
> - Thymic defects -> reduced T-cells
> - Cleft palate
> - Hypoparathyroidism
> - Chromosome **22**q11.2 deletion
