### Overview
CVID is the most common of the [[primary immunodeficiencies]]. As the name implies, it is a genetically and phenomenologically variable _collection_ of disorder, that is typically a diagnosis of exclusion.

Serum testing reveals a _hypogammoglobulinaemia_, typically:
- Markedly reduced IgG
- +/- reduced IgA
- +/- reduced IgM
Diagnostic confirmation is often with the _absence_ of antibody formation in response to vaccination.

The clinical presentation is variable, with some patients being susceptible to recurrent infections, while others can remain largely asymptomatic. 

Treatment is with immunoglobulin replacement, although this does not address the autoimmune and lymphoproliferative risks of the disease (NHL is the most common cancer in CVID patients).
