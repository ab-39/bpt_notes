### Leucocyte Adhesion Deficiency 
Impaired adhesion of neutrophils to endothelium due to mutations in CD18 (LAD1) or CD15 (LAD2). Characterised by recurrent bacterial and fungal infections, delayed wound healing, and persistent neutrophilia. 
- - -
### [[Chronic Granulomatous Disease]]
Defects of the neutrophil respiratory burst due to deficiencies in NADPH oxidase/_CYBB_ gene.
- - -
