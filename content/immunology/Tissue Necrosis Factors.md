### Overview
The tissue necrosis factors are a large superfamily of [[cytokines]] that are particularly important in the activation of the innate immune system, but also in the growth and differentiation process of other immune cell types.

The most ‘famous’ tissue necrosis factor is TNF⍺, which is a major, potent inflammatory cytokine involved in activating immune cells during infection and inflammation. Blockade of TNF⍺ is the most well-established target in the treatment of autoimmune disease.
