### Overview
Immunotherapy is used in the treatment of [[atopy and allergy]], and works by exposing the individuals and their APCs to (progressively higher) doses of antigen, with the aim of switching the immune response _away_ from a Th2 -> IL4/5 -> IgE/eosinophil rseponse _towards_ Th1 -> IFNγ -> neutralising IgG and Treg -> IL10/TGFβ -> IgG4/A responses.
