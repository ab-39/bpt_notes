### Overview
_Chimeric antigen receptors_ are artificual receptors which join specific antigen recognition domains (e.g. mAb variable regions) to T-cell activation domains. CAR-T cells are those that have been engineered to express these receptors, typically after introduction via a viral vector. This enables _MHC-independent_ identification and activation of T-cells.

### Current CAR-T Indications
All currently available CAR-T products in Australia target one of two B-cell antigens:
- CD19 -> B-ALL, DLBCL, mantle cell lymphoma etc.
- BCMA -> MM

### Class Specific Toxicities
Shared with bispecific T-cell engagers. Axicel tends to be the most associated with these syndromes due to the highly-active nature of its CD28 subunit. 

#### Cytokine Release Syndrome
- Fevers
- Hypoxia
- Hypotension
- Organ dysfunction

Grading:
1. Fever only -> paracetamol + fluids; tocilizumab and steroids if persistent >72h
2. Fever + hypotension/hypoxia -> fluids/SpO2; tocilizumab and steroids if refractory
3. Fever + hypotension requiring pressors/hypoxia requiring HFNC
4. Fever + hypotension requiring multiple pressors/hypoxia requiring NIV/intubation -> steroids + tocilizumab +/- anakinra

#### Immune Effector Cell Associated Neurotoxicity Syndrome
- Language difficulties
- Confusion
- Reduced level of consciousness
- Seizures
- Diffuse cerebral oedema (main mechanism of neurological changes) and intracranial haemorrhage

Monitor with ICE score:
- Orientation
- Naming
- Following commands
- Writing - most sensitive indicators of onset of ICANS
- Attention

If suspected -> steroids +/- IL-1 inhibition 

#### Other important toxicities:
- Lymphodepletion
- ICAHT = haematotoxicity - risk increases with increasing number of previous lines of therapy
- Hypogammaglobuinaemia
- BMCA-CAR-related Parkinsonism - generally follows recovery from CRS or ICANS
  - Possibility that BCMA is expressed in the basal ganglia
  - Often unresposive to L-DOPA
  - Therapy -> CAR-T cell elimination with steroids or chemotherapy -> high risk of secondary infections
- HLH
- Secondary malignancies - including CAR-expressing T-cell lymphoma

### CAR-T in Non-Haematologic Conditions
As most autoimmune diseases are ultimately mediated by aberrant B-cell clones producing autoreactive antibodies, this is the [most promising area of research](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2308917) for application of CAR-T:
- SLE - [case series](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-022-02017-5)of patients achieving deep and durable responses with anti-CD19 CAR-T treatment
- Idiopathic inflammatory myositis
- Systemic sclerosis
- Multiple sclerosis
