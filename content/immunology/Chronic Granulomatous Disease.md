# Chronic Granulomatous Disease 
#bpt/immunology #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
CGD is an X-linked recessive (or, less commonly, autosomal recessive) deficiency in the gene encoding for NADPH oxidase that leads to an inability of neutrophils and macrophages to generate a respiratory burst.

This leads to a propensity for infections with catalase-positive organisms, including _Staphylococcus aureus, Escherichia coli, Pseudomonas aerugunosa, Nocardia_ spp., and others, as well as for the formation of granulomas in the skin and GIT. 

Diagnosis is with the nitro blue tetrazolium test or dihydrorhodamine flow assay (viz. [[Testing in Immunology]]), and other features include hypergammaglobulinaemia and anaemia. 

Treatment typically involves:
- Treatment of infections
- TMP-SMX prophylaxis
- Antifungal prophylaxis 
- IFNγ
- Bone marrow transplant 

There is an association between phagocyte deficiencies and delayed separation of the cord.
