### Overview
The haematopoietic [[cytokines]], or growth factors, are involved in the production of lymphocytes in the bone marrow. The include the colony stimulating factors, IL-3, IL-7, and IL-2:
| Cytokine      | Source                      | Major Target                                              | Function                                                     |
|---------------|-----------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| GM-CSF, M-CSF | Stromal cells, T-cells      | Bone marrow progenitor cells, stem cells, precursor cells | Haematopoiesis, directed growth and differentiation of monocytes and granulocytes  |
| IL-3          | CD4+ T cells, keratinocytes | Bone marrow progenitor cells, stem cells, precursor cells | Haematopoiesis, directed growth and differentiation of monocytes and granulocytes  |
| IL-7          | Bone marrow stroma          | Bone marrow progenitor cells, stem cells, precursor cells | Growth of pre-B cells, T-cells, and NK cells                 |
| IL-2          | CD4+ T-cells                | Lymphocytes                                               | Proliferation                                                |
