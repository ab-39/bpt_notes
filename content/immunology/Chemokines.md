### Overview
Chemokines are a class of immune proteins that direct the movement of white blood cells within the body. 
- - -
### Classifying Chemokines
Chemokines are classed according to their chemical structure:
- C chemokines (27)
- CC chemokines (17)
- CXC chemokines (2)
- CX3C chemokines (1)

Where the relevant carbon groups are joined by disulphide bridges.

Alternatively, chemokines can be grouped functionally:
- Homeostatic chemokines -> control the migration of cells during normal development and maintenance (expressed constitutively) 
  - e.g. CCL14, CCL19, CCL20, CCL25, CCL27, CXCL21, CXCL13
- Inflammatory chemokines -> produced in response to infection or injury to direct the migration of leucocytes to the affected area (expression induced)
  - e.g. CXCL8, CCL2, CCL3,, CCL4, CCL5, CCL11, CXCL10
- Angiogenic chemokines -> promote or prevent the development of blood vessels
  - e.g. CXCL1-14
