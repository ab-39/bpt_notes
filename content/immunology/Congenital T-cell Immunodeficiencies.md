### Autosomal Dominant Hyperimmunoglobulin E Syndrome
Job syndrome is caused by an autosomal dominant _STAT3_ mutation leading to defective TH17 signalling and subsequently impaired neutrophil chemotaxis. It is characterised by elevated levels of IgE and attendant eosinophilia, and reduced IFNγ. 

Clinically, it presents with:
- Coarse facies
- Recurrent staphylococcal infections and cold abscesses
- Retained primary teeth
- Eczema 
- Decreased bone mineral density

Treatment is with prophylactic and treatment antibiotics, and courses of IVIg.

### IL-12 Receptor Deficiency 
Viz. [[immunology/Interleukins 12 and 23|Interleukins 12 and 23]], [[defects in innate immunity]] 
IL-12R deficiency is an autosomal recessive lack of IL-12 receptors, leading to a deficiency of TH1 cells and subsequent lack of IFNγ release to activate macrophages to attack intracellular pathogens. This is the most common cause of mendelian susceptibility to mycobacterial disease. Treatment is with antibiotics and IFNγ therapy.
