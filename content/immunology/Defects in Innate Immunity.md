### Overview
Innate immunodeficiencies are typically associated with susceptibilities to specific pathogens:
- Toll-like receptor 3 deficiency -> herpes simplex encephalitis
- CARD9 deficiency -> invasive fungal diseases
- Mendelian susceptibility to mycobacterial disease:
  - [[immunology/Interleukins 12 and 23|Interleukins 12 and 23]] b1 chain deficiency 
  - STAT1 deficiency
  - IFNγ receptor/autoantibodies against IFNγ
- GATA2 deficiency -> mono-MAC syndrome = monocytopenia + _Mycobacterium avium_ intracellulare infections
