# Severe Combined Immunodeficiency
#bpt/immunology
- - -
### Overview
 A rare, most commonly autosomal recessive disorder of stem cells leading to deficiencies of both T- and B-cells with resultant severe, recurrent infections. Most commonly due to mutations in either:
- Adenosine deaminase - involved in purine metabolism
- Common γ chain of cytokine signalling - lack of proliferative signalling (XLR inheritance)
- Less common mutations:
  - _RAG_ 
  - _JAK3_ 

Diagnosis is with qPCR revealing reduced TCR excision circles, and flow cytometry confirming an absence of T-cells. Furthermore, lymph node biopsy will show absent germinal centres. Treatment is with IVIg and bone marrow transplantation. Live vaccines _must_ be avoided.
