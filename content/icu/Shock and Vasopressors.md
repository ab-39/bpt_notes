### What is shock?
Shock is a state of circulatory failure leading to inadequate cellular oxygen utilisation
- - -
### Some key equations 
MAP = HR * SV * TPR
DO2 = CO * Arterial oxygen content = CO * (1.34 * Hb * SaO2) + PaO2*0.003

Normal oxygen delivery = 1L/min
Normal oxygen consumption = 250mL/min

### Signs of shock
Hypotension is a late sign 
May need fluids 
PPV on arterial waveform -> needs fluid 
CVP ~0-1mmHg needs fluid 
TTE - IVC collapsibility 
More invasive ICU things - PAC, PiCCO
- - -
### Things to do for shock 
Fluid bolus - 250-500mL balanced crystalloid
Straight leg raise - ~500mL
Consider vasopressors

### Inotropes and Vasopressors
Vasopressors = increase TPR -> increase BP
Inotropes = increase contractility -> increase SV -> increase BP
(Chronotropes = increase HR -> increase BP)
(Dromotropes = increase conduction -> increase BP)

### Pharmacology of blood pressure support 
⍺1 -> vasoconstriction
β1 -> inotropy

Metaraminol
- Vasopressor of the sympathomimetic class
- ⍺1 agonist + induces release of endogenous noradrenaline 
- Prone to tachyphylaxis -> don’t use as prolonged infusion 
- Bolus (0.5mg) or infusion 
- Can cause bradycardia (compensatory) - contraindication

Noradrenaline
- Catecholamine 
- Vasopressor with inotropic effects at high doses 
- ⍺1 agonist with β1 (and ⍺2/β2) effects at high doses 
- Should be given through a CVC due to risk of extravasation and tissue necrosis 
- Can cause reflex bradycardia 
- Dosed as a weight-based infusion, usually starts at a rate of 1mcg/min and should be dosed to effect - sky’s the limit 

Adrenaline
- Catecholamine 
- affects all adrenoceptors
- Weight based infusion - 1-20mcg/min through CVC
- Low rate -> β effects 
- High rate -> ⍺ effects 
- Can cause a lactataemia 

Vasopressin
- pure Vasopressor that affects V1R to increase TPR
  - Also acts st V2R for fluid effects 
- works well in severe acidosis 
- Can cause severe splanchnic vasoconstriction 

Dopamine very rare now
Dobutamine - essentially a β1 agonist 
Milrinone - PDE inhibitor “inodilator” - need to be used with noradrenaline, usually used in cardiogenic shock
Levosimendan - calcium sensitiser

Hydrocortisone - IV 50mg QID, typically used in septic shock
