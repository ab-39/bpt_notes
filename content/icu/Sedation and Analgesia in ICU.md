# Sedation and Analgesia in ICU
#bpt/icu
- - -
### Sedation
#### Indications for Sedation
- tolerance of ICU procedures - intubation, ventilation, cooling, etc.
- treatment of disease processes - seizures, ICP, EtOH withdrawal
- decrease oxygen consumptions
- maintain safety
- palliation

#### Level of Sedation
The lighter the better. Tend to use the RASS: -5 -> 4 is from unarousable to combative, where 0 is calm. 
- - -
### Analgesia
#### Indications for Analgesia
- pain associated with procedures
- pain associated with a disease process

There are numerous pain scores.
Heaps of adverse effects associated with pain - importantly may worsen ventilation. 
- - -
### Drugs

#### Morphine v Fentanyl 
| Fact        | Morphine                                                 | Fentanyl                                                     |
|-------------|----------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Class       | Natural                                                  | Synthetic                                                    |
| Mechanism   | MOP/KOP/DOP                                              | MOP > KOP                                                    |
| Dose        | mg/mL                                                    | microg/mL                                                    |
| Metabolism  | Phase 2 hepatic metabolism -> active metabolites (6-MAM) | Phase 1 hepatic metabolism -> inactive metabolites           |
| Elimination | Builds up in renal failure                               | Longer due to high Vd                                        |
| CSHT        | Not relevant                                             | CSHT:<br>4hr infusion -> 200min<br>8hr infusion -> 300min<br>>8h infusion -> unpredictable  |
