# Cytochrome p450
#bpt/pharmacology

### Overview
The cytochrome p450 superfamily of metalloproteinases are a group of microsomal proteins that are heavily involved in the metabolism of drugs. There are multiple families which fall into two broad categories: those involved in the biosynthesis of steroids and bile acids, and those involved in the metabolism of xenobiotics.

#### Nomenclature
Italics denote the gene, regular upper case denotes the protein
- Family = ≥40% sequence homology - denoted by the first Arabic numeral
- Subfamily = ≥55% sequence homology - denoted by the uppercase letter in the middle position
- Gene = ≥98% sequence homology - denoted by the final Arabic numeral
  e.g. CYP3A4
