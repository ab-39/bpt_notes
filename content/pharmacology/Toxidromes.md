#bpt/pharmacology

### Overview
Unlike regular pharmacology, in cases of poisoning both PK and PD mechanisms tend to become saturated leading to maximal effect and altered clearance. Treatment is generally centred around administration of an antidote (if one exists), decontamination, and supportive measures. The [[serotonin syndrome]] is discussed separately.

#### Decontamination
Activated charcoal is the mainstay of decontamination. Gastric lavage is used relatively less often, typically only if the patient presents within _one hour_ of having taken a highly toxic drug for which there is no antidote (e.g. paraquat). Whole bowel lavage is an alternative option for slow-release formulations (e.g. verapamil SR). Using expectorants such as ipecac is no longer recommended. 

#### Antidotes
Some discussed below, others include:
- Calcium for CCB toxicity
- Diazepam for chloroquine toxicity
- Desferrioxamine for iron toxicity
- Digifab for digoxin toxicity
- Fuller’s earth for paraquat poisoning
- Glucagon and isoprenaline for β-blocker toxicity
- N-acetylcysteine for [[paracetamol toxicity]]
- Bicarbonate for arrhythmias in the setting of TCA toxicity

### Anticholinergic
#### Clinical Signs
Relate to blockage of muscarinic and nicotinic receptors (i.e. anti-SLUDGE):
- Delirium
- Tachycardia
- Widening of the QRS complex
- Dry, flushed skin
- Dilated pupils
- Myoclonus
- Mild fevers
- Urinary retention
- Decreased bowel sounds -> note: delayed gastric emptying will also affect drug absorption

#### Offending Agents
- TCAs
- Datura
- Atropine
- Benztropine
- Antihistamines
- - -
### Cholinergic
#### Clinical Signs
Relate to activation of muscarinic and nicotinic receptors (i.e. SLUDGE):
- Confusion
- CNS depression
- Salivation
- Lacrimation
- Urination
- Defaecation
- Gastrointestinal cramping
- Emesis
- Diaphoresis
- Fasciculations
- Pulmonary oedema
- Miosis
- Bradycardia (or tachycardia)
- Hypotension
- Weakness
- Seizures

#### Offending Agents
Most often organophosphates - e.g. [Novichok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisoning_of_Alexei_Navalny), [Sarin gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_subway_sarin_attack), [VX gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Kim_Jong-nam), etc.

> [!CAUTION] 
> Organophosphates can be absorbed through the skin, so caution must be taken when approaching the patient.

#### Antidotes
- Atropine
- Pralidoxime

### Hypnosedatives
#### Clinical Signs
- Coma
- CNS depressoin
- Nystagmus
- Hypotension
- Hypothermia
- Hyporeflexia

#### Offending Agents
- Benzodiazepines
- Zopiclone
- Barbiturates

#### Antidotes
- Flumazenil (benzodiazepines)
- - -
### Opioids
#### Clinical Signs
- Miosis
- Coma
- CNS depression
- Respiratory depressoin
- Hyoptension
- Pulmonary oedema

#### Antidotes
- Naloxone
- - -
### Sympathomimetic
#### Clinical Signs
Relate to activation of adrenergic receptors:
- Delusions
- Paranoia
- Tachycardia
- Hypertension
- Pyrexia
- Diaphoresis
- Piloerection
- Mydriasis
- Hyperreflexia

#### Offending Agents
- Amphetamines
- Decongestants - e.g. pseudoephedrine
- Cocaine
