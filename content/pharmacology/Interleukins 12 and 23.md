# Interleukins 12 and 23

### Overview
IL-12 and IL-23 are dimeric molecules that share one common chain - **p40**. This means that by targeting p40, it provides an opportunity to inhibit both the TH1 (IL-12) and TH17 (IL-23) pathways involved in the switch from the [[innate to adaptive responses]]. _Ustekinumab_ is a monoclonal antibody targeting p40 that is indicated for [[Crohn disease]], psoriatic arthropathy, and severe chronic plaque psoriasis. There may also be some evidence for the use of ustekinumab in [[Takayasu arteritis]] and [[Behçet Disease|Behçet disease]]. 
- - -
### Conditions That _Can’t_ Be Treated with IL-12/23 Inhibition
- Rheumatoid arthritis 
- Refractory GCA
- Ankylosing spondylitis/[[axial spondyloarthritis]]
