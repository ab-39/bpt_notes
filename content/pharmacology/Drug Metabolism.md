# Drug Metabolism
#bpt/pharmacology

### Key Classes of Reactions
#### Phase One
Transformation of the drug from lipophilic to hydrophilic via redox reactions, hydroxylation, or hydrolysis, typically mediated by the cytochrome P450 superfamily of metalloenzymes. 

#### Phase Two
Conjugation of the drug or metabolite making it more water-soluble, leading to enhanced clearance or decreased activity. Predominantly glucoronidation, but also reactions such as sulfation, acetylation, methylation, etc. Facilitated by a range of enzymes, including the uridine diphosphate glucoronyl transferase family, glutathione S-transferase, and sulfotransferase.

#### Phase Three
Transport of the drug across the canalicular membranes into the bile, mediated by the P-glycoproteins as well as the ATP-binding cassette superfamily.
