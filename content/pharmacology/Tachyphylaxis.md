# Tachyphylaxis
#bpt/pharmacology
- - -
### Overview
_Tachyphylaxis_ refers to a hysteretic phenomenon where tolerance to a drug occurs rapidly. Initially, we observe a strong response to a given plasma concentration, but as time passes, the response to the _same_ concentration rapidly declines. This is often the case with non-therapeutic drugs such as cocaine and amphetamines.

On the other hand, if the response to a drug appears to be _increasing_ at the same plasma concentration, this could suggest the presence of an active metabolite or secondary messengers, or else due to redistribution.
