### Overview
Antagonist drugs can exert their function in two main ways:
- Competitive inhibition - i.e. binding to the same receptor/binding site as the ligand 
  - This is able to be overcome by increasing the concentration of the ligand, i.e. $EC_{50}$ is increased, but $E_{max}$ remains the same
- Non-competitive inhibition - i.e. allosteric modulation 
  - This is unable to be overcome by increasing the concentration of the ligand, i.e. $E_{max}$ drops, but $EC_{50}$ does not necessarily change
