# Genetic Susceptibility and Adverse Reactions
#bpt/pharmacology
- - -
### Overview
| Drug            | Gene        | Reaction                                                     |
|-----------------|-------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Abacavir        | HLA-B*57:01 | Abacavir hypersensitivity                                    |
| Allopurinol     | HLA-B*58:01 | Severe cutaneous adverse reactions                           |
| Aminoglycosides | mtDNA       | Deafness                                                     |
| Anticonvulsants | HLA-B*15:02 | Severe cutaneous adverse reactions (particularly in East Asian populations) - carbamazepine, phenytoin, lamotrigine |
| Carbamazepine   | HLA-A*31:01 | Carbamazepine hypersensitivity in Europeans                  |
| Imatinib        | ABCG2       | Imatinib resistance                                          |

> [!TIP] Mnemonic
> For SCARs secondary to ***anticonvulsants***:
> - CarbamazePINE at HLA-B-FIFTEEN
