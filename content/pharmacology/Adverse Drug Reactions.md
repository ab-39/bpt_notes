### Overview
Drug reactions fall into two broad classes:
- Pharmacological -> side effects or direct toxicity relating to the mechanism of action of the drug itself, or other off-target effects
  - ~80% of drug reactions 
- [[Hypersensitivity Reactions]] -> immune or non-immune reactions to a drug unrelated to the pharmacological mechanism 
  - ~20% of drug reactions, about half each type
  - Can be either:
    - Immediate = IgE-mediated = [[type I hypersensitivity]]
    - Delayed = T-cell mediated = [[type IV hypersensitivity]]
