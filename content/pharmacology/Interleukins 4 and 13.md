### Overview 
IL-4 is critical in the regulation of lymphocytes, myeloid cells, and non-haematopoietic cells. Its roles include:
- Induction of naive CD4+ T-cells to differentiate into TH2 cells
- Induction of B-cell immunoglobulin class switching to IgG1 and IgE
- Induction of alternate macrophage activation (along with IL-13)

_Dupilumab_ is a humanised monoclonal IgG4 antibody that targets the ⍺ chain of the IL-4 receptor, that is common to both the type I receptor that binds IL-4 and the type II receptor that binds both IL-4 and IL-13. It is indicated in the treatment of:
- Chronic severe atopic dermatitis
- Uncontrolled severe [[respiratory/Asthma|asthma]] - greatest efficacy with fortnightly dosing
- Chronic rhino sinusitis with nasal polyposis
- [[Eosinophilic oesophagitis]]
