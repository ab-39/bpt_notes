# The Naranjo Scale
#bpt/pharmacology
- - -
### Overview
The [Naranjo scale](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK548069/) is an (imperfect) tool for assessing the likelihood of an adverse drug reaction. Its components include:
- Existing reports of the given reaction
- Temporal correlation between drug and adverse event/removal and improvement
- Reapparance of the adverse effect after reintroduction
- Alternative causes
- Dose-dependent effect
- Patient history
- Objective evidence

A score of nine or more correlates to a definite drug reaction. Intermediate scores are accordingly less probable.
