# Functional Hypothalamic Amenorrhoea
#bpt/endocrinology
- - -
### Overview
Functional hypothalamic amenorrhoea refers to the absence of menses, often due to stress, weight loss, or exercise (= relative energy deficiency in sport), without the presence of other identifiable anatomic or physiologic causes. Patients often exisit in a state of energy deficit, that while associated with weight loss is independent of it. This leads to a suppression in GnRH pulsatility that ultimately results in reduced menses. Biochemical changes associated with this include:
- Reduced: FSH, LH, oestrogen
- Reduced: TSH, fT3, IGF-1, leptin
- Reduced bone mineral density
- Increased cortisol

A _Provera challenge_ can be used to differential FHA from PCOS, which will result in a withdrawal bleed in patients with the latter condition due to the presence of the endometrial lining being shed under the influence of progesterone. It is important to consider other diagnoses including hypopituitarism. 

Treatment is with correction of the underlying energy deficit, which may involve weight gain or psyschological support with eating. The oral contraceptive can be used for hormone replacement, but is not associated with significant improvements in bone mineral density. Alternatively, transdermal oestrogen and cyclical progesterone can be used, which does not provide contraception but has been found to improve BMD. It is also important to replete calcium and vitamin D, and treat osteoporosis if present.
