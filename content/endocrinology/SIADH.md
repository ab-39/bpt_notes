# SIADH
#bpt/renal #bpt/endocrinology

Note: plasma sodium concentration (i.e. Na:H2O) relates to plasma osmolality and **not** total body sodium content. It is regulated by water homoeostasis. 

Pathway:
> Excessive ADH secretion
> -> overactivation of V2R 
> -> over expression of AQP 
> -> impaired water excretion 
> -> reduced urine output 
> -> water retention 
> -> low [Na+]

This leads to a **euvolaemic hypotonic hyponatraemia**, i.e.:
1. Serum Na is low
   1. K and pH are normal 
2. Urine Na is high
3. Plasma osmolality is low
4. Urine osmolality is high
