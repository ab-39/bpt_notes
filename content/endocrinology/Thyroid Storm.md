# Thyroid Storm
#bpt/endocrinology
- - -
### Overview
A rare, life-threatening presentation of thyrotoxicosis, occurring in patients who have not received treatment or are non-adherent and are exposed to some provoking factor (e.g. trauma, surgery, infection).

Treatment is with:
1. Antithyroid drug - PTU preferred
2. **_Then_** potassium iodide to exploit the Wolff-Chaikoff effect
3. β-blockade
4. Glucocorticoids

And, of course, treatment of the underlying precipitant.
