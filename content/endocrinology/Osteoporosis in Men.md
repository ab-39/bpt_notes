# Osteoporosis in Men
#bpt/endocrinology

1. 1/3rd of all hip fractures occur in men
2. 1/5 men over 50 will develop OP
3. The cause of OP in 60% of men is secondary - need to investigate!

Major risk factors for OP in men include:
1. Hypogonadism
2. Smoking
3. GC use
4. Antiepileptic drugs
5. Excessive alcohol intake
6. Vitamin D deficiency
