# Osteomalacia and Rickets
#bpt/endocrinology
- - -
### Autosomal Dominant Hypophosphataemic Rickets 
Caused by mutations in the gene encoding [[endocrinology/FGF23|FGF23]] that lead to impaired enzymatic degradation of the protein and subsequently elevated serum levels. This causes hypophosphataemia, impaired bone mineralisation, and ultimately a clinical syndrome of bone pain, pathologic fractures, and myalgias. 
![](Osteomalacia%20and%20Rickets/image.png)
- - -
### X-Linked Hypophosphataemic Rickets
Similar to above, but caused by mutations in the _PHEX_ gene, which encodes cell surface endopeptidases that may be involved in the degradation of FGF23. 
- - -
### Tumour-Induced Osteomalacia
Caused by ectopic secretion of FGF23 by tumours, typically of the bone.
