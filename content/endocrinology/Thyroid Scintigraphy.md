# Thyroid Scintigraphy
#bpt/imaging #bpt/endocrinology 
- - -
### Overview 
Thyroid Tc-99m pertechnetate scintigraphy is a type of functional nuclear medicine scan that assesses the uptake of radiolabelled pertechnetate anions into the tissue of the thyroid via the sodium-iodide symporter. 
- - -
### Patterns of Thyroid Scintigraphy
#### [[Grave Disease]]
Smooth, diffuse uptake throughout the entire gland, that is much more intense than normal.
