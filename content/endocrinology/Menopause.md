# Menopause
#bpt/endocrinology
- - -
### Definition
Permanent cessation of menstruation due to ovarian failure, with diagnosis being made after twelve months of amenorrhoea. The average age at onset is 51y, often earlier in smokers. while some women may be asymptomatic, many can experience:
- Menstrual disturbances 
- Hot flushes
- Urinary symptoms
- Sexual dysfunction
- Mood changes 

While HRT in menopause is associated with increased rates of heart attacks, strokes, breast cancer, and venous thromboembolism, this is predominantly in women over the age of sixty. In women with significant symptoms under sixty who do not have significant risk factors, HRT can be used for symptom relief. While oestrogen is most effective for treating vasomotor symptoms, it is often used in combination with progesterone to minimise the risk of endometrial hyperplasia/carcinoma. Genitourinary symptoms can be treated with topical vaginal oestrogen.
