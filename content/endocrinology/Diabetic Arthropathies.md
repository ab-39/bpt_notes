# Diabetic Arthropathies
#bpt/endocrinology
- - -
### Feet
‘Charcot foot’ destruction of the midfoot secondary to neuropathy. Painful. Solution is NWB and glycaemic control.
- - -
### Hands
‘Diabetic cheirarthroapthy’ enzymatic glycation of collagen leading to fibrosis and thickening of periarticular structures in hands causing thickened skin and limited mobility. Solution is PT and glycaemic control.
