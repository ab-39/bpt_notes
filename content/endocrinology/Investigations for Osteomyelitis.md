# Investigations for Osteomyelitis
#bpt/endocrinology

1. Routine Bloods and Inflammatory Markers
   1. ESR > 70
2. XR -> looking for soft tissue gas suggestive of gangrene
   1. True radiological changes of OM can take up to six weeks
3. Probe-to-bone test
   1. Low-risk: negative test largely excludes osteomyelitis
   2. High-risk: positive test is diagnostic
4. MRI highly sensitive
   1. SPECT/PET can be used too
5. Sterile bone samples are gold standard
