# Pseudohypoparathyroidism
#bpt/endocrinology #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
A hormone resistance disorder. Associated with hypocalcaemia, hyperphosphataemia, brachydactyly, short stature, mental retardation, and soft tissue calcification. 

Type Ia pseudohypoparathyroidism is associated with abnormal GNAS coding. The clinical manifestation depends on the mode of inheritance:
- From the mother -> PTH resistance (and other hormones, particularly TSH)
- From father -> osteodystrophy without endocrine defect (“*pseudo*pseudohypoparathyroidism”)

Type Ib pseudohypoparathyroidism is associated with hypocalcaemia without osteodystrophy.
