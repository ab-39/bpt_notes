### Overview
Fibroblast-related growth factor 23, or FGF23, is a molecule released from the bone in response to elevated levels of PTH, 1,25-vitamin D, and phosphate. Its main function is to inhibit vitamin D hydroxylation and phosphate reabsorption in the kidney. It works with a co-receptor klotho that is expressed in the kidney and parathyroid glands. Loss of klotho, most commonly due to CKD, leads to increase secretion of FGF23 from osteocytes and osteoblasts, triggering the cascade that leads to the development of CKD-MBD.

> [!NOTE] FGF-23 Summary:
> 1. Bone-derived hormone - produced by osteocytes and osteoblasts
> 2. Inhibits vitamin D activation and renal phosphate reabsorption
> 3. Suppresses parathyroid hormone 
> 4. Levels rise in CKD -> long-term association with poor CV outcomes
