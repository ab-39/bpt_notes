### Paget's Disease
Mostly idiopathic, genetic links include SQSTM1, possible viral triggers + osteoclast, bone remodelling - but disorganized, thickened but weak bone
Most common site - pelvis
Symptoms - bone pain, often at night, fractures, bowed tibia, kyphosis can be asymptomatic, but incidentally found increased ALP
Treatment - Bisphosphonates
Zoledronic acid 5 mg IV once (preferred) - will last ~ 5 years
- Alternative: Risedronate (30 mg PO daily × 2 months)
Monitor ALP to assess for response
