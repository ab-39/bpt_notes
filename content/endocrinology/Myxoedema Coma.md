# Myxoedema Coma
#bpt/endocrinology
- - -
### Overview
A rare complication of hypothyroidism with mortality as high as 50%. Clinical presentation is generally with hypothermia and reduced consciousness, typically after a precipitating factor such as infection. The patient will be:
- Obtunded
- Bradycardic
- Hypotension
- Bradypnoeic
- Hypothermic
- Dry skin

Treatment is with mechanical ventilation if indicated, glucocorticoids, and parenteral thyroid hormone replacement.
