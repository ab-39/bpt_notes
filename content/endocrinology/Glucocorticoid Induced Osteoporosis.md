# Glucocorticoid Induced Osteoporosis
#bpt/endocrinology
- - -
### Mechanism
While there are many mechanisms by which glucocorticoids damage bone, the primary issue is decreased osteoblastogenesis and increased apoptosis of osteoblasts and osteocytes.
![](Glucocorticoid%20Induced%20Osteoporosis/image.png)
See [Glucocorticoid-Induced Bone Disease](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMcp1012926).
- - -
### Clinical Features
Vertebral fractures are the most common fractures in long-term glucocorticoid use:
- Increased risk by three months of treatment
- Risk peaks at twelve months of treatment
Doses of ~5mg prednisolone/d are associated with an increase of almost 50% in fracture risk, but reduces rapidly once steroids are discontinued. 
- - -
### Treatment
In general, treatment is reserved for patients:
- With a history of previous fractures
- Aged >70y
- On prednisolone >7.5mg/d
- DXA T-score <—2.5 (in some cases —1.5)
- FRAX risk of OP fracture >20% of hip fracture >3%
