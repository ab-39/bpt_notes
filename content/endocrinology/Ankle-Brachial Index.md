# Ankle-Brachial Index
#bpt/endocrinology

The ratio of the ankle to the brachial systolic BP - a good noninvasive assessment for PVD:
1. <0.8 -> PVD
2. <0.5 -> severe

90% sensitive and 98% specific for stenosis >50%.

Toe pressures are even better:
1. Toe pressure <30mmHg indicative of PVD
2. In T2DM: <50mmHg
