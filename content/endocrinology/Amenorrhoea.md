### Approach
#### Rule #1 = rule out pregnancy
Then - check:
- FSH/LH
- TSH
- Prolactin
- Pelvic ultrasonography + karyotype

This gives five groups of diagnoses:
1. Low LH/FSH + present uterus -> hypothalamic (functional) or pituitary problem
2. Normal LH/FSH + present uterus -> outflow tract problem, other*
3. Elevated LH/FSH + present uterus -> primary ovarian insufficiency
4. No uterus + 46XX -> Müllerian agenesis
5. No uterus + 46XY -> androgen insensitivity syndrome, 5⍺-reductase deficiency
