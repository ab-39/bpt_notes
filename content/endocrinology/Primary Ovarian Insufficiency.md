# Primary Ovarian Insufficiency
#bpt/endocrinology
- - -
### Overview
Primary ovarian insufficiency refers to the cessation of ovarian function in women _before_ the age of forty. This is characterised by elevated FSH (into the menopausal range, >40iu/L) on two or more occasions, with associated low oestradiol and AMH levels. The cause is most usually idiopathic, although iatrogenic, environmental, autoimmune, and genetic (often [[fragile X syndrome]]) causes are also possible. Treatment is possible with hormone replacement therapy, and donor oocytes if pregnancy is desired.
