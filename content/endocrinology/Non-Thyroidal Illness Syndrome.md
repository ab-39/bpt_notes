# Non-Thyroidal Illness Syndrome
#bpt/endocrinology
- - -
### Overview
The fundamental issue in ‘sick euthyroid syndrome’ is that 5’-monodeiodination of thyroxine in the periphery is inhibited by severe non-thyroidal illness, resulting in a low fT3. This leads to a transient secondary hypothyroidism, and subsequent low TSH and fT4 levels. Additionally, total thyroid hormone level is usually low due to reduced production of thyroid binding globulin. This conditionis usually transient and self-limiting, and there is no evidence for intervention.
