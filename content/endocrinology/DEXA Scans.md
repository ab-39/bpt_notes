# DEXA Scans
#bpt/endocrinology

Indications for DEXA:
1. Women ≥65 and men ≥70
2. At risk men and postmenopausal women
3. Post-fracture to determine disease severity
4. Radiographic findings (e.g. TLXR) suggestive of osteoporosis
5. Glucocorticoid ≥3 months 
6. Primary hyperparathyroidism
7. Monitoring response to treatment

DEXA results are T- and Z- scores
1. T = standard deviations compared to young, healthy, sex- and ethnicity-matched controls
   1. 1 SD ~ 1.5-2x risk of fracture 
   2. -1 to -2.5 = osteopenia
   3. ≤-2.5 = osteoporosis
2. Z = standard deviations compared to healthy, age-, sex-, and ethnicity-matched controls (more useful if patient <50y)

Need to consider both the absolute density and the rate of change.
