### Subclinical Hypothyroidism
Subclinical hypothyroidism is a biochemical disorder that requires normal thyroid hormones but an elevated TSH in on at least two separate readings. It affects ~10% of the population, most of whom have [[Hashimoto thyroiditis]]. There is a signal of association with increased cardiovascular risk due to increased LDL, which improves with thyroxine. 

Generally reserve treatment for:
- Pregnant patients
- Those with TSH >10miu/L **if** <70y
- High cardiovascular risk
- Symptomatic 
- Clinicians’ discretion
- - -
### Subclinical Hyperthyroidism
Subclincial hyperthyroidism is the biochemical state of a reduced TSH with normal thyroid hormones. It is much rarer, affecting 0.7% of the population. It is associated with increased cardiovascular mortality and AF, particularly if TSH <0.1miu/L, and an increased risk of fractures.

Treat if:
- TSH <0.1miu/L
- TSH 0.1-0.4miu/L **and** >65y with known cardiac disease
