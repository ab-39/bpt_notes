# Vitamin D
#bpt/endocrinology #bpt/fracprac
- - -
Ultraviolet (UV) radiation, particularly UVB rays, catalyzes the conversion of 7-dehydrocholesterol in the skin to previtamin D3. This previtamin D3 undergoes a thermal process to form vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol). This process occurs naturally in the skin when it’s exposed to sunlight, particularly UVB radiation, and is an essential step in the production of vitamin D within the body.
Summary of vitamin D synthesis:
1 Skin- 7-dehydrocholesterol is photolyzed by UV light to form vitamin D3(cholecalciferol).
2 Liver- Vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol) is hydroxylated in the liver to form 25- hydroxycholecalciferol (calcidiol) catalyzed by 25-hydroxylase.
3 Kidneys- 25-hydroxycholecalciferol (Calcidiol) is converted to 1,25- dihydroxycholecalciferol (calcitriol), catalyzed by 1-alpha-hydroxylase.
