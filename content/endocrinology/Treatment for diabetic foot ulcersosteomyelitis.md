# Treatment for diabetic foot ulcers/osteomyelitis
#bpt/endocrinology

1. Antibiotics
   1. Need to consider good Gram+ coverage
2. Surgery
3. Negative pressure wound therapy (can’t go straight onto bone):
   1. Continuous negative pressure to wound surface
   2. Maintains moisture
   3. Reduces oedema
   4. Increases local blood flow
   5. Promotes tissue healing
4. Hyperbaric oxygen therapy
