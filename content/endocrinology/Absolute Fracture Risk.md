# Absolute Fracture Risk
#bpt/endocrinology

Preferred calculator is WHO FRAX calculator: https://www.shef.ac.uk/FRAX/tool.jsp for patients *not yet* on treatment. Factors in:
1. Age
2. Sex
3. Weight
4. Height
5. Previous #
6. Parent # NOF
7. Smoking status
8. Glucocorticoid therapy
9. Rheumatoid arthritis
10. 2’ osteoporosis
11. Alcohol (>3 STD/day)
12. Femoral neck BMD

Optimisation is required for osteoporosis prevention, including increasing weight-bearing physical activity.
