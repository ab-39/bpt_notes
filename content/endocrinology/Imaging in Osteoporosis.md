# Imaging in Osteoporosis
#bpt/endocrinology

TLXR is useful for identifying fractures, but not for excluding osteoporosis. 

[[DEXA scans]] assess the density of vertebral and hip bones compared to healthy young sex- and ethnicity-matched controls. Two XR beams pass through the subject’s body, and soft tissues are subtracted out. Typically investigate two of: lumbar spine (L1-4), proximal femur, and distal radius (if problems with femur). 

Benefits:
1. Noninvasive
2. No fasting
3. Low dose
