### Overvier 
The International Federation of Clinical Chemistry and Laboratory Medicine (IFCC) has a different unit for reporting Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) levels compared to the old DCCT format.

The formula for converting the old DCCT format (reported as a percentage) to the new IFCC format (reported in mmol/mol) is:
> IFCC (mmol/mol) = (DCCT (%) – 2.15) x 10.929
> Using this formula:
>IFCC = (8.6 – 2.15) x 10.929 IFCC ≈ 65.35 mmol/mol

Now, to estimate the mean glucose level corresponding to an HbA1c level, there isn’t a direct conversion, but several studies have established approximate correlations. For an HbA1c of 8.6%, the estimated mean glucose level is approximately 10.4 mmol/L (or 187 mg/dL).
However, it’s important to note that this is an estimation and individual variations can occur due to factors like red blood cell turnover rates and other health conditions that might influence the relationship between HbA1c and mean glucose levels. This approximation can serve as a general guideline but should not replace individualized medical advice or decisions.
