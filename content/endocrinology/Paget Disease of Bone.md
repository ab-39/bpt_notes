# Paget Disease of Bone
#bpt/endocrinology
- - -
### Overview
Paget disease of bone is a focal disorder of bone metabolism assocaited with ageing. In PDB, increased bone turnover leads to normal _lamellar_ bone being replaced with weak, woven bone.

While the majority of PDB patients are asymptomatic, some may present with bone pain or pathological fractures. There is an increased risk of osteosarcoma. Skull involvement may present with skull enlargement and impaired hearing. 

Biochemical findings include elvated ALP, elevated P1NP and CTX, and normal calcium and phosphate. Plain film generally shows osteolytic and osteosclerotic lesions, while the skull may display a ‘cotton wool’ appearance. Scintigraphy can reveal the extent of metabolic activity. 

Treatment is generally only indicated if there are symptoms, skull involvement, vertebral involvement, joint involvement or if hypercalcaemia develops. A single dose of zoledronic acid is the usual treatment.
