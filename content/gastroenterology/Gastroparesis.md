# Gastroparesis
#bpt/gastroenterology
- - -
### Overview
Common neurological complication of T2DM. 

Diagnosis is with scintigraphic gastric emptying study with retention >10% at 4h or >60% at 2h. 

Treatment is with prokinetics, e.g.:
- Dopamine antagonists - e.g. metoclopramide, domperidone
- Motilin agonists - e.g. macrolide antibiotics
- 5-HT4 agonists - e.g. cisapride
