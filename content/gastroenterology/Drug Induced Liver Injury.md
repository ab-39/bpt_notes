# Drug Induced Liver Injury
#bpt/gastroenterology
- - -
### Overview
The most common cause of [[acute liver failure]] and acute hepatitis, most often due to paracetamol or antibiotics (especially amoxicillin-clavulanic acid). The pattern of injury can be either hepatic, cholestatic, or mixed, and the mechanism of toxicity is variably idiosyncratic or predictable. 
- - -
### Treatment
#1 = drug withdrawal.

Specific treatments exist for specific drugs:
- Paracetamol -> N-acetylcysteine
- Valproic acid -> L-carnitine
