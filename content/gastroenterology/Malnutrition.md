### Overview 
Malnutrition is a complex, multisystem state that arises from insufficient nutritional intake to meet the body’s needs, affecting an estimated 29% of hospital inpatients. There are several scores to quantify malnutrition, including the MUST, MST, GLIM, and BNR. 

Clinical signs of malnutrition include:
- Body habitus
- Muscle wasting
