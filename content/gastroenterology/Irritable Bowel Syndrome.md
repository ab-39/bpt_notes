# Irritable Bowel Syndrome
#bpt/gastroenterology
- - -
### Overview
Defined according to the Rome IV criteria:
- Recurrent abdominal pain on at least one day of the week for the last three months 
- With a total duration of symptoms at least six months
- Plus two of more of:
  - Temporal association with defaecation
  - Change in stool frequency
  - Change in stool consistency

This is a highly prevalent condition, predominantly affecting women in the third and fourth decade.
- - -
### Symptoms
There are several associated GI symptoms:
- Bloating
- Distention
- Dyspepsia
- Functional constipation/diarrhoea

And non-GI symptoms:
- Migraines
- Fibromyalgia/chronic fatigue
- Anxiety/depressoin
- Interstitial cystitis
- Dyspareunia
- - -
### Aetiology
Likely complex and multifactorial, relating to:
- Stress
- Somatisation
- Anxiety/depression
- Maladaptive cognitions
- Food
- Central sensitisation
- History of GI infection
- Microbiome
- - -
### Management
- Improve diet - particiularly soluble fibre
- Hydration
- Low-FODMAP (i.e. low in fermentable sugars that fuel gut dysbiosis) 
  - Lactulose is a FODMAP! -> if constipated, Movicol is a better option 
- Exercise
- Probiotics 

Specific therapies:
- Peppermint oils
- CBT
- Mebeverine
- Hyoscine
- TCAs
- SSRIs
- Antibiotics can be used if there is evidence of SIBO

Avoid NSAIDs and opioids.
