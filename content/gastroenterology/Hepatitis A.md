# Hepatitis A
#bpt/gastroenterology #bpt/infection
- - -
### Overview
HAV is a common picornavirus for which humans are the only reservoir. It replicates in the liver, is transmitted via the faecal-oral route, and is shed for two weeks prior to the onset of jaundice and one week after. In general, it causes a mild, transient non-specific illness followed by jaundice, although in a minority of cases it can progress to fulminant [[acute liver failure]]. Diagnosis of acute infection is with IgM antibodies. High risk groups should be vaccinated, and post-exposure vaccination can also be utilised in healthy individuals. Immunoglobulin can also be used in those younger than 1 or older than 40, the immunocompromised, or in those with comorbid liver disease.
