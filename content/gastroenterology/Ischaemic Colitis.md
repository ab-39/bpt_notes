# Ischaemic Colitis
#bpt/gastroenterology
- - -
### Overview
Ischaemia of the colon typically due to either an occlusive thrombus or hypoperfusion. Characterised by sudden onset of severe, cramping abdominal pain; bloody diarrhoea; and an elevated lactate. When caused by hypoperfusion, the most vulnerable areas are the watershed zones of the colon:
- SMA-IMA watershed: splenic flexure 
- IMA-SRA watershed: rectosigmoid junction
Colonic ischaemia is typically self-limiting and requires supportive care, but severe cases (e.g. associated with sepsis, peritonitis, etc.) may require a laparotomy +/- stoma formation.
