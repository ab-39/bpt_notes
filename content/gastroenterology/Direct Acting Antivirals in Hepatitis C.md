### Overview
![](Direct%20Acting%20Antivirals%20in%20Hepatitis%20C/image.png)

1. NS3/4A - involved in polyprotein processing -> ‘-previr’ drugs
   - Note that these are contraindicated in patients with decompensated cirrhosis  
2. NS5A - membrane associated phosphoprotein involved in RNA replication -> ‘-asvir’ drugs
3. NS5B - RNA polymerase involved in viral RNA synthesis -> ‘-buvir’ drugs

The most common combination therapy drugs in Australia are:
- Vosevi - voxilaprevir + ledipasvir + sofosbuvir
- Epclusa - velpatasvir + sofosbuvir
