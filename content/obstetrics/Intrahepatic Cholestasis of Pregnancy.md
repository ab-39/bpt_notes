# Intrahepatic Cholestasis of Pregnancy
#bpt/obstetrics #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
IHCP is a rare but important condition of the third trimester, that is characterised by pruritis (particularly on the palms and soles), mildly elevated transaminases, and an association with fetal distress, meconium at birth, and stillbirth. Treatment is with ursodeoxycholic acid.
