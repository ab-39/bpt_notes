# Hypertension in Pregnancy
#bpt/obstetrics #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Definition
Hypertension in pregnancy is defined as:
> SBP >140mmHg
> **or**
> DBP >90mmHg
Taken on two occasions over several hours **after 20/40 weeks gestation**. Severe/urgent hypertension is generally understood to be BP ≥160/110mmHg. 

The hypertensive disorders of pregnancy encompass several related conditions:
- Pre-eclampsia - hypertension developing after 20/40 with evidence of end-organ dysfunction
  - The HELLP syndrome is the most serious manifestation of this
- Gestational hypertension - hypertension developing after 20/40 without evidence of end-organ dysfunction
- Chronic hypertension
- Chronic hypertension with superimposed pre-eclampsia 
- - -
### Management
The three best-known medications for HTN in pregnancy are:
- Labetalol
- Methyldopa
- Nifedipine XR

Target BP <140/90mmHg, consider modifying therapy if BP <110/70mmHg. The key studies in theis area are _CHIPS_ and _CHAPS_ ([Treatment for Mild Chronic Hypertension during Pregnancy](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2201295)).

Treatment of hypertension does help decrease the risk of the following:
- progression to severe hypertension
- respiratory distress in neonate
- induction of labour
- maternal stroke
