### Overview
Antenatal depression is a common condition, affecting up to 20% of pregnant women. It is assocaited with an increased risk of prematuriy, fetal distress, neonatal behaviour, and [[psychiatry/Postpartum Depression|postpartum depression]].

Of patients with a history of depression, almost half will have an episode in pregnancy, and that number rises to ~70% among those who have discontinued antidepressants.
