# Important Scores and Scales
#bpt/clinical
- - -
### Short Case
Grading murmurs - the key difference is between a III and a IV:
- III - easily audible but no thrill
- IV - easily audible with a palpable thrill 

Grading power - everything hinges around level 3:
- 2 = only able to move with gravity eliminated
- 3 = able to move against gravity
- 4 = able to move against gravity **and** resistance
- - -
### Long Case
NYHA scale - the key difference between grade III and IV is the presence of symptoms at rest:
- II = comfortable at rest, symptoms with normal activity
- III = comfortable at rest, symptoms with minor activity
- IV = symptomatic at rest 

mRS - the key pivot in this scale is from mRS 3 to mRS 4, with the defining characteristic being the inability to walk:
- mRS 2 = mild disability, independent in ADLs
- mRS 3 = moderable disability, able to walk
- mRS 4 = moderately severe disability, unable to walk and dependent in pADLs
- (mRS 6 = dead)

ECOG - the key pivot is between ECOG 2 and 3:
- ECOG 2 = symptomatic, in bed <50% of the day
- ECOG 3 = symptomatic, in bed >50% of the day
- (ECOG 5 = dead)
