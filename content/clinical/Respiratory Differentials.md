# Respiratory Differentials
#bpt/clinical
***
### Bronchiectasis
Characterised by coarse crackles. If non-CF related, differentials include:
- ANCA vasculitis
- Immune deficiencies - e.g. Kartagener, CVID
- TB

Can be focal in the setting of a foreign body or severe infection. 
***
### ILD
- IPF
- Bird fanciers lung
- NSIP (secondary to autoimmune disease)
- CT-ILD (can be UIP or NSIP)
- Occupational 
- Drugs - MTX, bleomycin, nitrofurantoin
- Sarcoidosis
