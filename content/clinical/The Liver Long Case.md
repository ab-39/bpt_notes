### Cirrhosis Surveillance:
1. HCC surveillance = at least an USS q6/12, some say AFP also
2. Variceal surveillance:
   1. Primary prophylaxis based on Baveno criteria (i.e. fibroscan with stiffness >25kpa and thrombocytopenic) -> start carvedilol
   2. Secondary prophylaxis -> carvedilol + endoscopy
3. Osteoporosis = DXA q2-5y
4. Age appropriate vaccines
