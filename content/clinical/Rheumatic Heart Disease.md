### Overview
Rheumatic Heart Disease is a systemic immune response to β-haemolytic (group A) _Streptococcus_ infection due to cross-reactivity between myosin on cardiomyocytes and bacterial M-proteins. It is characterised by two phases:
1. Acute rheumatic fever -> pancarditis, fever, polyarthritis, erythema marginatum, Sydenham chorea
2. Rheumatic heart disease -> mitral >> aortic = tricuspid >> pulmonary 
   - Commonly mitral stenosis

Rheumatic mitral stenosis is characterised by thickening of the valves (‘hockey stick’ deformity) and commissural fusion (‘fish mouth’ sign). This is associated with a very limited ability to tolerate tachycardia or atrial fibrillation, and a _very_ high thromboembolic risk in atrial fibrillation requiring warfarinisation (irrespective of CHADS-2-VASC score). 

The murmur is characterised by a low-pitch diastolic rumble, and a possible loud P2 (due to [[pulmonary hypertension]]). It is best heard in the left lateral position in full expiration. 

Mitral stenosis severity is determined on echocardiography with three metrics:
1. Appearance and valve mobility
2. Valve area/continuity equation (<1sqcm - some sources now say <1.5sqcm) 
3. Mean valve gradient (>10mmHg)

Current ESC guidelines recommend intervention for patients with moderate-to-severe mitral stenosis and symptoms. The preferred approach is percutaneous mitral comissurotomy with the alternative being surgery.

Options include a balloon valvuloplasty, as well as open surgery. The Wilkin score is used to stratify patients.

### Clinical Aspects of MS
Key causes to know:
1) RHD - particularly in women
2) Calcific valve disease
3) Post-repair for MR - relatively rare

Severe = <1-1.5sqcm valve area, +/- clinical signs:
- Narrow pulse pressure
- Early-opening snap
- Long murmur duration (mid-diastolic rumble)
- Diastolic thrill at the apex
- Presence of pHTN, signs of which include:
  - Prominent a-waves in the JVP
  - RV impulse
  - Loud or even palpable P2
  - PR
  - TR

Investigations to expect:
- ECG - p mitrale if in SR, AF (suggests chronicity), right axis deviation
- CXR - MV calcification,
