### Issues
DARN FAST IF:
- D = diagnosis/duration
  - When was the diagnosis made?
  - What were the symptoms at diagnosis?
- A = aetiology
- R = risk factors
- N = now = current status; progress to now
  - How have symptoms progressed?
  - How frequent are exacerbations?
  - Have there been any complications?
- F = function
- A = adherence
  - To medicatons, diet, fluid restriction, etc.
  - Also attendance at appointments, etc. 
- S = symptoms
- T = treatment/targets
  - What medical management has been tried?
  - What surgical management has been tried?
  - What is the current management?
- I = impact of illness
- F = future
  - Is the patient on/planned for biologics?
  - Is the patient on the transplant list?

### Social History
4A’s, 4F’s, GI, GI, ψ:
- 4A's:
  - Accommodation
    - Does the patient: own their own house? Rent? Live in supported accommodation or an RACF?
  - ADLs
    - Personal = eating/drinking/hygiene/toileting/dressing
    - Domestic = cooking/cleaning/gardening
    - Community = shopping/banking/etc.
    - Do they require any supports/home help with this?
  - Aids
    - Does the patient use gait aids?
    - Are there steps in the house which make mobility more difficult?
  - Access to community
    - Can the patient drive?
    - If not, how do they attend appointments - e.g. taxi, bus, etc.?
- 4F’s:
  - Finances
    - Does the patient rely on a pension or do they have private funds?
    - How does their financial situation affect their ability to afford treatment?
    - How does their financial situation affect their quality of life?
  - Family and friends
    - Do they have supports in the community?
    - Do they live with anyone?
  - Free time
    - How do they like to spend it? Do they have any hobbies?
  - Forecast 
    - How has the disease affected their life, and how will management help improve their quality of life?
- GI, GI:
  - GP
    - Do they have a good, regular GP?
  - Immunisations and preventive health
    - Include cancer screening here
  - Goals of care
  - Insight
- Psychiatric history 
  - Include cognition

### Approach
1) Ask the patient for a quick (i.e. D + A) overview of **all** their medical conditions
2) Cross-check this with the medication list
3) Check for any ‘unexplained medications’
4) Do a quick systems review 
5) Quickly check for any additional interventions - transfusions, venesections, OTC medications, etc. - or investigations - e.g. things being worked up without a definite diagnosis 
6) Establish the issues:
   1) The **patient’s** main issue
   2) Their doctor’s main issue
7) Then… start taking the history 
   1) Try doing this _one condition at a time_ - it needs to be a disciplined endeavour

In terms of presentation - everything should be synthesised, and start with the _patient’s_ main issue as the key issue (after the opening statement):

8) Start with the main issue as identified by the patient
9) Then progress to additional _active_ issues
10) Then progress to less active issues and previous/resolved problems
    1) e.g. “Gout well controlled on allopurinol 100mg, with no flares since 2024”
11) Make sure to comment on adherence issues and the socioeconomic factors driving them
12) Make sure to include psychiatric history
13) Family history 
14) Social history as above
