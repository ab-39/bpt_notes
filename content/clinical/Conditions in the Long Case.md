### Diabetes Mellitus
D = how and when was the diagnosis made? Were there symptoms at onset?
A/R = risk factors include family history, steroids, pancreatic insufficiency, etc.
N = current management, current level of control
	Important to include hypo episodes at this point
	Are the neuropathic symptoms painful?
	Do they have hypoglycaemic awareness?
	Is there autonomic dysfunction?
	Have they had any foot ulcers?
F = is function impaired e.g. by retinopathy or neuropathy?
A = _very_ important in DM 
S = current symptoms
T = what is the current management?
	How do they look after their feet?

### Adherence
Try to get at _what_ the patient is struggling to adhere to, as well as _why_. Possible causes of poor adherence can include:
- Psychosocial issues
- Depression
- Financial issues
- Cognitive issues
- Low level of insight
- Poor health literacy
- Age-related issues (e.g. low vision)
- Stigma
- Difficulty swallowing, etc.
