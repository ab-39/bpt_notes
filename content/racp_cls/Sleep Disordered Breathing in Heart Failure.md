# Sleep Disordered Breathing in Heart Failure
#bpt/respiratory #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview 
Sleep disordered breathing in heart failure can be either [[obstructive sleep apnea]], [[central sleep apnea]] (/Cheyne Stokes Respiration, CSR-CSA), or a combination of both. 

The best treatment is to improve the underlying heart failure. 

CPAP has no long-term benefit in heart failure alone, but can be beneficial if there is a significant component of OSA, and can in fact improve ejection fraction in that case.
