# Staphylococcal Bloodstream Infections
#bpt/infection #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Key Points
- In-person ID consultation was found to reduce mortality when compared to phone advice 
- Clearance cultures should be taken every 24-48h until negative
- Prolonged bacteraemia is associated with higher rates of mortality
- All patients with SBI should receive an echocardiogram, with TOE being significantly more sensitive for the diagnosis of [[infective endocarditis]] when compared to TTE
  - _But_ a normal TTE can be used to rule out endocarditis in patients without:
    - Community-acquired disease
    - High-risk cardiac conditions
    - Intravenous drug use 
- Treatment:
  - MSSA -> cefazolin or flucloxacillin 
    - Note that vancomycin is _less effective_ than β-lactams for MSSA treatment, and should not be used
  - MRSA -> vancomycin 
  - Duration:
    - Uncomplicated -> 14d
    - Complicated -> 28+d
  - No role for combination therapy 
  - Actively under investigation with the _SNAP_ trial 
- Prognosis is poor - three month mortality 27%
