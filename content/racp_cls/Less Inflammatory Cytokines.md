### Overview
The TH2 cells tend to be less inflammatory than the TH1 and TH17 cells involved in the switch from the [[innate to adaptive responses]], and are primarily involved in the response to worm/helminthic infections and also allergies. The key cytokines they produce are IL-4, IL-5, and IL-10:
| Cytokine | Source                             | Major Target                       | Function                                                     |
|----------|------------------------------------|------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| IL-4     | CD4+ T cells (TH0, TH2, TFH, ILCs) | T and B cells                      | T and B cell growth and differentiation, IgG/A/E production, TH2 responses, allergic responses  |
| IL-5     | TH2 (CD4+) cells                   | B cells and eosinophils            | T and B cell growth and differentiation, IgG/A/E production, TH2 responses, allergic responses  |
| IL-10    | TH2 and TREG cells                 | B cells, TH1 and TH17 (CD4+) cells | B-cell growth, inhibition of the TH1 and TH17 responses      |
