# Primary Headache Disorders

- - -
### Overview
Key types:
- Tension-type headache
  - Rarely seen in clinical practice as these are the ‘routine’ headaches that people get from time to time, with a limited effect on daily functioning 
- [[Migraine]]
- [[Trigeminal Autonomic Cephalalgia]]
- New Daily Persistent Headache 
  - A refractory, specialised headache disorder
  - A new headache, without an identifiable cause, that occurs on a persistent, daily basis 

Importantly, when assessing a patient with headache, it is important to exclude secondary causes and consider red flags (‘SNOOP-4’):
- Systemic features
- Neurologic signs/symptoms
- Onset sudden or orthostatic
- Older onset 
- Pattern change - progressive, precipitated by Valsalva, postural aggravatoin

Complex investigations are typically not required.
