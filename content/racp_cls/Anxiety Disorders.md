### Overview
The anxiety disorders encompass:
- Generalised anxiety disorder
- Panic disorder
- Obsessive-compulsive disorder
- PTSD - often attributable to a single episode
- Complex PTSD - often attributable to multifactorial issues
- Phobias 
- - -
### Panic Disorder
Panic attacks are a key feature of panic disorder, but are not pathognomic. Long term treatment involves CBT and SSRIs, with benzodiazpines being used _only_ as a bridge while these therapies take effect.
- - -
### Obsessive Compulsive Disorder
Characterised by intrusive thoughts and compulsions.
- - -
### Social Anxiety Disorder
Any mode of scrutiny, including being watched eating.
