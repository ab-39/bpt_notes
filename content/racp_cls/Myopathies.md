The key investigation is often the serum creatine kinase, although this is not necessarily elevated in all myopathies, and does not always correlate with the level of muscular weakness. Other important tests include:
- LFTs
- TFTs
- Electrolytes
- ANA/ENA/autoimmune panel
- Molecular genetic studies 

EMG tends to show motor unit action potentials with small amplitude and duration. Muscle biopsy can also be helpful, generally of the biceps, quadriceps, or deltoid.
