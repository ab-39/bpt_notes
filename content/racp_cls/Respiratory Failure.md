### What is respiratory failure?
The definition of “respiratory failure” is based on the partial pressures of oxygen and carbon dioxide _in an arterial blood sample_.
- - -
### [[Type 1 Respiratory Failure]]
Also “hypoxic” respiratory failure:
> Low PaO2 in the presence of low or normal PaCO2

Fundamentally, pulmonary failure 
- - -
### [[Type 2 Respiratory Failure]]
Also “hypercarbic” respiratory failure:
> Elevated PaCO2 in the presence of low PaO2

Fundamentally, ventilatory failure
