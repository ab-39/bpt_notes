### Types of Obstruction
Obstructions of the large airways fall into two categories:
| Type                   | Causes                                                       | Effect on Expiratory Spirometry                              | Effect on Inspiratory Spirometry                             |
|------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Fixed                  | Circumferential tumour, stenosis post-prolonged ETT          | Obstruction during expiration -> flattening of upper loop    | Obstruction during inspiration -> flatterning of lower loop  |
| Intrathoracic variable | Tracheal polyps, non-circumferential tumours, vocal cord polyps | Obstruction during expiration -> plateauting of expiratory limb of spirometry<br> | Negative pressure during inspiration actually opens airways -> normal inspiratory limb of flow-volume loop |
| Extrathoracic variable | “”                                                           | Air pressure during expiration opens airway -> preserved expiratory limb of spirometry<br> | Negative pressure during inspiration closes airway around obstruction -> flattening of lower loop |
