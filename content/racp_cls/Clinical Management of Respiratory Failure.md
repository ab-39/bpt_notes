### Basic Principles
Clinical management of respiratory failure hinges on identifying the primary issue:
> Oxygenation vs ventilation

If the issue is oxygenation, we need to think about [[the Fick principle]] and adjust the relevant parts:
- VO2 can be reduced by reducing activity or temperature (usually means sedation)
- Q is the product of HR and stroke volume, and can be modified:
  - Fluids will increase preload 
  - Vasodilatation will decrease TPR
  - Inotropes will increase contractility
- CaO2, the arterial concentration of O2, is the product of Hb, 1.34, and SaO2 can be increased by:
  - Increasing Hb
  - Increasing FiO2 
  - Increasing PEEP (i.e. functional residual capacity)

If the issue is ventilation, this typically requires mechanical ventilation to ensure adequate alveolar minute ventilation, although there can also be a role for BPAP.
