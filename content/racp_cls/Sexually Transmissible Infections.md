### Overview
Incredibly, over **one million** STIs are contracted _every day_, the majority of which are asymptomatic. These largely fall into three groups of four diseases:
#### Four Curable (Bacterial) STIs:
1. Syphilis
2. Chlamydia
3. Gonorrhoea
4. Trichomonas

#### Four Incurable (Viral) STIs:
1. [[Genital Herpes]]
2. [[Hepatitis B]]
3. [[HIV]]
4. HPV

#### New and Re-Emerging STIs:
1. Monkeypox
2. Lymphogranuloma venereum
3. Zika
4. Ebola
- - -
### Prevention
In general, behavioural change and condom usage can prevent the majority of STIs. In some cases, particularly the viral STIs, vaccination can be used to prevent them altogether. PEP and PrEP are also emerging as useful treatments. 
- - -
### STI Screening for MSM
Current [Australian guidelines](https://sti.guidelines.org.au/populations-and-situations/men-who-have-sex-with-men/) recommend 3-monthly screening for sexually active MSM, looking for:
- Gonorrhoea/chlamydia with - oropharyngeal + anorectal swab and first pass urine NAAT
- HIV antibody/antigen
- Syphilis serology
- Hepatitis C
- Hepatitis A (if not vaccinated)
- Hepatitis B
