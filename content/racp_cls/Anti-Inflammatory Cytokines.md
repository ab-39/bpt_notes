### Overview
The anti-inflammatory [[cytokines]] are produced by regulatory T (TREG) cells to try and wind down immune responses. They include IL-10 and TGFβ:
| Cytokine | Source          | Major Target                                   | Function                                                     |
|----------|-----------------|------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| IL-10    | TREG, TH2 cells | B cells, TH1 and TH17 (CD4+) cells             | B cell growth, inhibition of the TH1 and TH17 responses      |
| TGFβ     | TREG CD4+ cells | B cells, T cells, macrophages, dendritic cells | Suppression of all immune responses, promotion of tolerance and wound healing  |
