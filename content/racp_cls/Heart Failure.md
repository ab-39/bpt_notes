### Overview
Heart failure is a _complex clinical syndrome_ characterised by exertional and resting symptoms and signs attributable to an abnormality of cardiac structure or function that impairs the ability of the heart to fill with blood at normal pressures and/or eject sufficient blood to meet the needs of the metabolising organs.

The universal definition of heart failure states that the syndroms is one of:
1. Symptoms and signs caused by a structural or functional cardiac abnormality, e.g.:
   - LVEF < 50%
   - Abnormal cardiac chamber enlargement
   - E/E’ > 15
   - Ventricular hypertrophy
   - Valvular disease 
2. Corroborated by at least one of:
   - Elevated natriuretic peptide levels:
     - BNP = B-type natriuretic peptide (produced in the ventricles)
     - Rule out cut-offs:
       - BNP < 100ng/L
       - NT-proBNP < 300ng/L
     - Rule-in:
       - BNP > 400ng/L
   - Objective evidence of cardiogenic or systemic congestion, e.g.:
     - CXR
     - Echocardiography
     - Catheterisation
     - Exercise testing

Heart failure is generally characterised into four clinical phenotypes:
1. HFrEF = heart failure as defined above with left ventricular ejection fraction below 40%
2. HFmrEF = heart failure as defined above with LVEF between 41-49%
3. HFpEF = heart failure as defined above with LVEF > 50% 
4. HFimpEF = heart failure as defined above with a baseline LVEF ≤40% with a subsequent improvement to an LVEF >40% after treatment

### Aetiology
The most common underlying aetiology of heart failure is ischaemic heart disease. As such, all patients with a new diagnosis of heart failure should undergo coronary artery imaging, either with an angiogram or CT/MRI.

In the situation where patients have increased LV wall thickness, other important investigations include:
- cMRI with late Gd enhancement
- PET/bone scintigraphy - ?infiltrative cardiomyopathies (e.g. amyloidosis)

In terms of pathophysiology, there are probably three key systems affected:
- [[The Renin-Angiotensin System]] - overactive
- The Sympathetic Nervous System - overactive
- cGMP - deficient

### Echocardiographic Diagnosis of HFpEF
- E/E’ ≥13
- Or, E/E’ ≥8 **and** left atrial volume index >40ml/sqm
