### Overview
The MAHAs are the result of red cell fragmentation due to endothelial pathology and platelet-fibrin thrombi in the circulation. Peripheral blood film shows keratocytes (horn cells), schistocytes (helmet cells), and microspherocytes. There may also be a pronounced thrombocytopenia. The bone marrow response is typical, with polychromasia and nucleated RBCs. 
![](Microangiopathic%20Haemolytic%20Anaemia/image.png)
The causes are varied:
- Valve haemolysis - typically mechanical valves  
- [[Haemolytic uraemic syndrome]]
- [[Thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura]]
- [[Pre-Eclampsia]]
