### Overview
Diabetic foot infections are qualitatively different from ‘routine’ SSTIs, and require specialist management. Owing to the complex milieu of factors involved, including neurological and vascular impairment, diabetic foot infections are commonly centred around complex wounds, and can be polymicrobial. The ‘usual suspects’ (e.g. GAS, GBS, _S. aureus_) are frequently implicated, but so too are environmental Gram-negative organisms such as _Pseudomonas_ spp. 

DFUs require multidisciplinary assessment and management.
