### Overview
Microcytic [[anaemia]] refers to when the MCV of the erythrocytes is lower than 80fL. The most common causes are “I SALT”:
- [[Iron deficiency anaemia]]
- Sideroblastic anaemia
- Anamia of chronic disease (although this can also be normocytic)
- Lead poisoning 
- Thalassaemia and other [[haemoglobinopathies]]
