### Overview
TDP-43 is the key neuropathological marker in the condition known as **LATE** = limbic-predominant age-related TDP-43 encephalopathy. 

It was initially found in the brains of dementia patients wth neuronal loss and gliosis who _lacked_ the characteristic changes of Alzheimer disease. The key comorbid finding was of (often unilateral) _hippocampal sclerosis_, as well as neuronal dropout and astrocytosis. 

LATE is characterised by a pattern of progressive amnesia which mimics [[Alzheimer disease]], but in which the deposition of TDP-43 continues to progress, unlike in AD where ADNC plateaus.
