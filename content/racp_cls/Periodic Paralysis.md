### Overview
The periodic paralyses belong to a class of disorders known as _channelopathies_, where disorders in electrolyte channels in muscles lead to altered excitability. The inheritance pattern is typically autosomal dominant. The clinical presentation typically involves proximal limb muscles, and typically spares respiratory and bulbar muscles. ‘Full’ episodes involve complete paralysis of all four limbs and areflexia, while abortive episodes can have more subtle weakness. Stabilisation of serum K+ levels is important in all conditions.
