### Overview
IL-1 is a potent innate cytokine, that is involved in the activation of macrophages and epithelial cells. It has a natural antagonist, IL-1R antagonist (IL-1RA), for which a human recombinant, _anakinra_, exists. Anakinra is indicated for:
- Cryoporin associated periodic syndromes (on the PBS)
- Neonatal onset multi system inflammatory disease 
- Muckle-Wells syndrome
- Acute and chronic gout
- Rheumatoid arthritis
- [[Behçet Disease]]
- Ankylosing spondylitis
- Uveitis
