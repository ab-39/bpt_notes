### Neutrophil Oxidative Burst Testing
When normal neutrophils are exposed to nitro blue tetrazolium, they metabolise this dye into a blue colour which they take on (RIGHT). In the absence of a functional _NADPH oxidase_ enzyme (_CYBB_ gene/GP91FOX protein), the respiratory burst is unable to occur, and neutrophils remain the same colour (LEFT).

### Dihydrorhodamine Flow Assay
This is a flow based assay that is functionally giving us the same information as NOBT, but which involves dying the neutrophils with dihydrorhodamine, an intracellular dye, and then activating them with PMA, a potent mitogen. On activation, dihydrorhodamine should become fluorescent, and ‘shift to the right’ of the flow assay:

### Testing the Complement Pathway
Serum assays can show the direct levels of proteins such as C3, C4, and C2, while there are also functional assays for the classical (CH50) and alternative (AH50) pathways. 

General:
- Measurement of C3/C4 levels

Specific:
- Measurement of specific complement factors
- Measurement of the regulatory proteins:
  - C1 Inhibitor Level
  - Factor H
  - Factor I
- Measurement of MAC activity = haemolytic assay
