### Overview
The human herpes viruses are a family of double-stranded DNA viruses with capsids, tegument, and glycoprotein bilayer envelopes. 

The key members of this family are:
- Alphaherpesvirinae - target mucoepithelial cells and lie latent in the sensory ganglia:
  - HHV1 = HSV1
  - HHV2 = HSV2
  - HHV3 = VZV
- Beta- and gammaherpesvirinae:
  - HHV4 = EBV - targets B-cells and epithelial cells, and lies latent in B-cells
  - HHV5 = CMV - targets monocytes and lies latent in the myeloid compartment
  - HHV6/7 = roseolovirus - targets T-cells and lies latent in them
  - HHV8 = KSHV - targets lymphocytes and lies latent in monocytes, most famous for causing Kaposi sarcoma in AIDS
