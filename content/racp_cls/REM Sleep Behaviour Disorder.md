# REM Sleep Behaviour Disorder
#bpt/respiratory #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
RBD is a _loss of the usual muscle atonia_ during REM sleep (i.e. it is the opposite of [[Narcolepsy|cataplexy]], in which atonia and REM sleep set on while awake). This can lead to dream re-enactment behaviour and carries a high risk of violent injury to bed partners.

There is a strong association with the ⍺-synucleinopathies (e.g. [[Parkinson’s disease]], [[Dementia with Lewy Bodies]]), but RBD may predate the emergence of these conditions by decades. They may be precipitated by SSRI or TCI use, but it is unclear if this is cause or an unmasking effect. 

Treatment usually involves bedroom safety for the patient and their partner, melatonin and/or clonazepam, and careful screening for neurodegenerative conditions.
