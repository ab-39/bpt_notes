# Tuberculosis
#bpt/infection #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
Commonest site of involvement for enteric TB is the ileocaecal area. 

MDR PO regimen:
- Bedaquiline
- Pretomanid
- Linezolid
- Moxifloxacin

IGRA vs. TST: IGRA not affected by vaccination status, but can get false negatives from advanced disease, AIDS, and young age
