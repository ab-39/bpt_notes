# Haemolytic Uraemic Syndrome
#bpt/haematology #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
Triad of anaemia (specifically, microangiopathic haemolytic anaemia), thrombocytopenia, and AKI. Can be caused due to a reaction to Shiga toxin (typically produced by _E. coli_ 157:H7) which binds to the Gb3 receptors in renal endothelial cells, or ‘atypically’ due to other infections or over activation of the alternative complement pathway, the latter typically being due to loss of function cariants in _CFH_, _CFI_, or CD46, or gain of function variants in _CFB_ or CD3. 

Management is typically supportive, although in aHUS terminal complement inhibitors such as eculizumab can be used in severe cases. Peripheral blood smear is characterised by schistocytes.

There are some associations with other causes:
- Drug-induced - e.g. quinine, cyclosporine
- Allogeneic BMT
- Metastatic carcinoma
- Pregnancy
- Autoimmune causes
