# Rhabdomyolysis
 #bpt/general #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview 
Rhabdomyolysis is caused by the breakdown of muscle tissue and the release of its byproducts into the bloodstream. The clinical presentation can vary from asymptomatic to profound volume depletion with AKI and electrolyte abnormalities. 
> Diagnosis is based on [CK] > 1000 U/L or x5 the ULN

Treatment involves correction of the underlying cause, correction of electrolyte imbalances, and aggressive fluid replacement to protect the kidneys.
