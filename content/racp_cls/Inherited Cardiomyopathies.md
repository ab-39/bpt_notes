### Overview
A group of structural and functional disorders of the heart that are typically transmitted in an autosomal dominant fashion. Broadly grouped into:
- [[Arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy]]
- [[Dilated cadiomyopathy]]
- [[Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy]]
- Restrictive cardiomyopathy
- Left ventricular non-compaction

Patients are often asymptomatic, or else develop symptoms only on exertion - e.g. dyspnoea, palpitations, presyncope/syncope, etc.

The inherited cardiomyopathies tend to exhibit autosomal dominant transmission, albeit with variable expression and age-related penetrance. They are susceptible to secondary disease modifiers, including blood pressure, male sex, eexercise, diet, alcohol, and pregnancy.
