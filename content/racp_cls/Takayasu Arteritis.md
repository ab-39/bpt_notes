# Takayasu Arteritis

- - -
### Overview
Takayasu arteritis is a granulomatous, large-vessel [[vasculitis]] characterised by three of the following six diagnostic ARA criteria:
1. Age at disease onset ≤40y
2. Claudication of extremities
3. Decreased pulsation of one or both brachial arteries
4. Difference of at least 10mmHg in blood pressure between the two arms
5. Bruit over one or both of the subclavian arteries
6. Bruit over the abdominal aorta
