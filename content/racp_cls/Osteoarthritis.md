### Overview
OA tends to be a polyarticular arthritis that worsens with use. In the hands, tends to affect the DIP joints (Heberden’s nodes) more than the PIP joints (Bouchard’s nodes), and the first carpometacarpal joint (at the base of the thumb). Rarely affects the MCP joints). Aggrecanase implicated in pathophysiology.

Classic imaging findings are:
- Loss of joint space
- Osteophytosis
- Subchondral sclerosis
- Subchondral cysts
