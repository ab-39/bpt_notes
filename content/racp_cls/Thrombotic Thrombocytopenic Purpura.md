# Thrombotic Thrombocytopenic Purpura
#bpt/haematology #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
Form of [[haemolysis]] caused by the deposition of platelet-rich, hyaline thrombi in the terminal arterioles and capillaries (NB. this is distinct from the fibrinous thrombi of DIC). Presents with a classic dyad that warrants further investigation and treatment:
- [[Microangiopathic haemolytic anaemia]]
- Thrombocytopenia
With three other classic features that make up a pentad:
- Fever
- Neurologic abnormalities
- AKI

TTP is characterised by reduced levels (<10%) of ADAMST13, either due to an inherited deficiency or the development of an autoantibody. This leads to the persistence of ultra-large multimers of [[von Willebrand factor]] in the bloodstream, and downstream activation of primary haemostasis, leading to the formation of platelet rich thrombi.
![](Thrombotic%20Thrombocytopenic%20Purpura/image.png)
A key differential is [[haemolytic uraemic syndrome]], which tends to present with AKI more commonly than TTP, due to the affinity of the Shiga toxin for Gb3 receptors in the kidney.  A low ADAMST13 assay suggests a diagnosis of TTP rather than HUS and can be temporised by the administration of fresh frozen plasma. If the TTP is immune-mediated, the patient may respond to corticosteroids and B-cell depleting therapy, although the definitive treatment is PLEX.
