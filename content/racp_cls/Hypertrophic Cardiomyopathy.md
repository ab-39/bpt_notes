# Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy
#bpt/genetics #bpt/cardiology #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
HCM is characterised by an asymmetrical, non-dilated hypertrophy of the left ventricle, often associated with outflow tract obstruction. Histologically, there is evidence of myocytes being:
- Hypertrophied
- Disarrayed
- Fibrosed

These conditions tend to be caused by variations in genes encoding _sarcomeric proteins_. Pathogenic variants tend to be found in only 30-60% of individuals.

Management:
1. Β-blockade
2. Mavacamten - if LVOT obstructed (cardiac myosin inhibitor)
3. Ablation

_Several_ other conditions can be association with HCM, including:
- Noonan syndrome (also associated with pulmonic stenosis)
- Fabry disease
- Danon disease
- Pompe disease
