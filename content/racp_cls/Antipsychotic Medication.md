### Overview
Aripiprazole is unique among antipsychotics in being a _dopamine D2 **agonist**_. 
- - -
### Clozapine
Associated with myocarditis, characterised by myocardial eosinophilic infiltration. Most common (and most commonly fatal) side effect is severe constipation.
- - -
### Side Effects
EPSEs
Oculogyric crisis
NMS
Tardive dyskinesia
- Meige syndrome = OLB dystonia + blepharospasm
