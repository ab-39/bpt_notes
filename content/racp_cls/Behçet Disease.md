### Overview
Behçet disease is an autoinflammatory disease affecting arteries and veins of all sizes. It is characterised by:
> Oral aphthous ulcers - x3 in twelve months

Plus, at least two of the following four:
- Ocular inflammation
- Genital ulcers 
- Pathergy reaction (reaction to stress)
- Skin lesions - pustules, erythema nodosum

Behçet disease ‘classically’ affects the Turks, where its prevalence is about 2/1000 persons, as well as other ‘Silk Road’ countries. There is a genetic component to Behçet disease (HLA-B51), but the exact pathogenesis remains to be elucidated. Behçet disease is associated with venous thrombosis, as the inflammatory process is primarily a venous perivasculitis. Pulmonary artery aneurysm is unique to Behçet disease. 

Treatment for Behçet disease consists of:
1. Glucocorticoids
2. Colchicine
