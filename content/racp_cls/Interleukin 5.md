### Overview
IL-5 is produced by TH2 and mast cells, and stimulates B-cell growth and immunoglobulin (predominantly IgA) secretion. Eosinophils are the primary cell type that express the ⍺-type IL-5R (and in fact, IL-5 was originally called eosinophil colony stimulating factor). There are two monoclonal antibodies that target the IL-5 system:
- IL-5 -> mepolizumab
- IL-5R⍺ -> benralizumab

These are indicated for:
- Uncontrolled severe [[asthma]]
- [[Eosinophilic oesophagitis]]
- [[Eosinophilic granulomatosis with polyangiitis]]
- Hypereosinophilic syndromes
- Chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps
