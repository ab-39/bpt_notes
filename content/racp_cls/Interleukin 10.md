### Overview
IL-10 is a naturally anti-inflammatory cytokine, that attenuates the production of pro-inflammatory cytokines. Interestingly, it is a prominent participant in human inflammatory disease, and can be found in significant quantities in the synovium of patients with rheumatoid arthritis.

Humanised recombinant IL-10 has typically been found ineffective where studied (e.g. in RA or IBD), but there may be a role in treatment of skin manifestations of psoriatic arthropathy.
