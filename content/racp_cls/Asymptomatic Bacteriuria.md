# Asymptomatic Bacteriuria
#bpt/infection #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
Do not treat(!) _unless_:
- The patient is pregnant
- Prior to an invasive urological procedure
- Within three months of a renal transplant

The NNH for drug side-effects in ABU is **_3_**!
