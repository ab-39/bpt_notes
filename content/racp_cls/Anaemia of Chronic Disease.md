### Overview
A normocytic anaemia that is predominantly due to a reduction in production by the bone marrow, underpinned by hepcidin-induced alterations in iron metabolism -> reduced GI absorption of iron and trapping of iron in macrophages. This is initially an adaptive response to inflammation, but then becomes maladaptive as there becomes an inability ot increase erythropoiesis in response to the anaemia.
