### Janus Kinase Inhibitors
JAK inhibitors inhibit cytokine induced cell activation, and consequent inflammation.

_Tofacitinib_, a JAK1 and JAK3 inhibitor, has shown benefit in rheumatoid arthritis similar to adalimumab (anti-TNF monoclonal antibody). 
- - -
### Spleen Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors
STK inhibitors inhibit downstream synthesis of pro inflammatory cytokines. 

Fostamatinib is showing promise in treatment of patients with RA, albeit with significant side effects.
