### What is a respiratory alkalosis?
Respiratory alkalosis is a situation in which ventilation exceeds demand, causing serum levels of CO2 to drop, and pH to rise. 
- - -
### Acute and Chronic Compensation
Acutely, metabolic compensation for a respiratory alkalosis involves the release of protons from stores within the body, leading to:
1. 2mmol/L reduction in HCO3- for every 10mmHg drop in pCO2 acutely

In contrast, chronic compensation (rare except for adaptation to altitude) will involve the loss of bicarbonate in the kidneys, leading to:
2. 5mmol/L reduction in HCO3- for every 10mmHg drop in pCO2 chronically
