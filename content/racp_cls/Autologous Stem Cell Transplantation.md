# Autologous Stem Cell Transplantation
#bpt/haematology #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
ASCT refers to the process of stem cell collection, administration of myeloablative chemotherapy, and subsequent stem cell infusion. 
> *Fun fact*: stem cells are preserved in DMSO after being harvested 

Autologous SCT allows for _very_ high dose chemotherapy to be adminstered for haematological malignancies. It is a _low-mortality_ procedure with a risk of death of about ~1%. 

Used widely in [[multiple myeloma]] with melphalan conditioning, as well as in other conditions such as lymphoma and some T-cell lymphomas. There is a role for ASCT in other conditions:
- Myeloma
- Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma
- Hodgkin Lymphoma - particularly at relapse
- Germ cell tumours
- CNS tumours
- Amyloidosis 
- AML/ALL without allo-donor
- CML without allo-donor
- Autoimmune conditions 

Risks of autoSCT include:
- Death (~1-3%)
- Early complications:
  - Bacterial sepsis secondary to neutropenia (note: engraftment usually between d10-20)
  - Mucositis
  - Venoocclusive disease -> treatment is with defibrotide 
  - Engraftment syndrome
  - Idiopathic pulmonary syndrome

- Late complications:
  - Infections: esp. viral and PJP, usually warrants 6/12 prophylaxis
    -> revaccinate at 6+ months
  - Secondary cancer - MDS, AML, skin and solid organ transplants
  - Psychological
