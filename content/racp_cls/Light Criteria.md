# Light Criteria 
#bpt/respiratory #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### What are the Light criteria?
The Light criteria allow us to determine between the two main types of [[pleural effusion]]: transudative and exudative. 

Note that diuresis can affect the protein concentration, making application of Light criteria tricky.
- - -
### Defintion
The Light criteria state that a pleural fluid is exudative if _any one_ of the following three criteria are met:
1. `Pleural fluid [protein] / serum [protein] > 0.5`
2. `Pleural fluid [LDH] / serum [LDH] > 0.6`
3. `Pleural fluid [LDH] > 0.66 ULN of serum [LDH]`
- - -
### Alternative Approaches
The ‘Pleural Fluid Only Three Test Rule’ - exudative if:
1. `Pleural fluid [protein] > 30g/L`
2. `Pleural fluid [cholesterol] > 55mg/dL`
3. `Pleural fluid [LDH] > 0.66 ULN of serum [LDH]`
- - -
### Additions
Low pH -> infection or malignancy 
- low glucose -> infection
- low or normal glucose -> malignancy
