### Uses of NIV
[[Non-invasive ventilation]] is most effective in hypercarbic respiratory failure. 
There is also good evidence when used as a bridge to wean from invasive ventilation.
- - -
### Predicting Failure
Failure rate is between 5-60%, and can depend on:
- Severity of acidosis (pH < 7.25)
- De novo hypoxaemia
- Signs of respiratory distress
- Non-pulmonary organ failure
