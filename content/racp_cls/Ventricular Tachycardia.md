### Overview
The ventricular tachycardias are the group of broad complex tachycardias that originate in the ventricles. They can be classified by:
- Electrocardiographic characteristics: monomorphic vs. polymorphic
- Electrophysiologic mechanism: focal vs. reentrant 
- Substrate: idiopathic vs. scar related
- Duration nonsustained vs. sustained
- - -
### Idiopathic Ventricular Arrhytmia Syndromes
These are focal idiopathic ventricular tachycardias, that are usually due to triggers (e.g. diastolic calcium overload leading to delayed afterdepolarisations). There is a known association with exercise, particularly [[arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy]] -> tends to be associated with the RVOT. 

#### Treatment
Excellent prognosis. Generally only treat if the patient is symptomatic, or if the patient is developing cardiomyopathy. Β-blockers or else flecainide in the presence of a structurally normal heart.
