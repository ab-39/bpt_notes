### Overview
The interleukins are a large family of [[cytokines]] that play many key roles in the immune response:
- IL-1 -> inflammation
- IL-2 -> T-cell growth
- IL-4 -> B-cell growth
- IL-5 -> eosinophil growth
- IL-15 -> NK cell growth 
- IL-10 -> regulatory/suppressive
