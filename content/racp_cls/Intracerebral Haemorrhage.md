# Intracerebral Haemorrhage
#bpt/neurology #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Key aspects of management
1. Intensive blood pressure lowering:
   - Aim ~140mmHg systolic (_INTERACT-2_), but not lower (_ATTACH-2_)
2. Reverse anticoagulants 
3. Stroke unit care

Consider:
- Haemostatics
- Surgery - particularly if mass effect
