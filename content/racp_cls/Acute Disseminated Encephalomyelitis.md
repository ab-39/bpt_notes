# Acute Disseminated Encephalomyelitis
#bpt/neurology #bpt/infection #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
ADEM is a _post-infectious_ encephalopathy syndrome that presents with a rapidly progressive course in an afebrile patient. It typically occurs some weeks after infection or vaccination. 

It is characterised by the development of acute, polyfocal neurological deficits, and can involve both the central and peripheral nervous systems. There is typically neuroimaging evidence of multifocal demyelination.

It is distinguished from [[acute viral encephalitis]] by the absence of fever, recent infection/immunisation, and evidence of demyelination on imaging. Early use of steroids are the mainstay of treatment.

#### Acute Haemorrhagic Leucoencephalopathy
Considered to be a severe form of ADEM, most commonly affecting adults after viral infection.
