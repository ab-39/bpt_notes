Key biomarker is serum creatinine, which is a metabolite of creatine in skeletal muscle and dietary meat intake. This varies widely with age, sex, and body size. 

Creatinine is freely filtered across the glomeruli and not reabsorbed in the proximal tubules (although there is a little tubular secretion).

We can use creatinine clearance to arrive at a proxy measure of glomerular filtration rate, the eGFR. The tubular secretion of creatinine increases as GFR drops, which makes eGFR less and less reliable as it drops. We can either collect 24h worth of urine to calculate CrCl, or use equations.
