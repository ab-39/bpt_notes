### Overview
The term _anaemia_ refers to the reduced capacity of blood to carry oxygen from lungs to the tissues, measured by a reduction in the haemoglobin level:
> Females: <115g/L
> Males: <120g/L

Anaemias can be classified according to the mean volume of the erythrocytes (MCV):
- [[Microcytic anaemia]] = MCV < 80fL
- Normocytic anaemia = 80fL < MCV < 100fL
  - Generally implies [[bone marrow failure]], acute bleeding, haemolysis, or chronic disease
  - Also think of: CKD, marrow infiltration, inflammatory processes, chemotherapy, or possible MDS
- Macrocytic anaemia = MCV >100fL
