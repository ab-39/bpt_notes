### Overview
Brugada syndrome is a sodium channelopathy that is associated with cardiac conduction abnormalities that can result in sudden cardiac death.

It is characterised by _coved ST-elevation_ in leads V1-3m as well as other conduction defects (e.g. AF, SSS). 
![](Brugada%20Syndrome/image.png)

While the diagnosis is often clinical, genetic testing can be useful for confirming the diagnosis and testing family members.

SCD occurs at a mean age of 40y, and precipitating factors include fevers, cocaine use, electrolyte derangements, and certain medications. 

The only definitive gene implicated in the Brugada syndrome is **_SCN5A_**, which is transmitted in an autosomal dominant fashion. Although only 20-30% of patients with _SCN5A_ have a resting ECG diagnostic of Brugada sydnrome, ~80% will manifest the characteristic changes when challenged with a sodium channel blocker (e.g. ajmaline).
