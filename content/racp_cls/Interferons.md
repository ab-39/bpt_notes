### Overview
Interferons are a class of over twenty [[cytokines]] that are produced by multiple cell types in response to infections, with particular therapeutic use in response to viral infections. There are two key types of interferons:
- Type I = IFN ⍺ and β 
  - Play an important role in defence against viral infections
  - Used therapeutically to treat viral infections, notably [[hepatitis C]]
  - Interestingly, autoantibodies against type I interferons appear to be implicated in the pathogenesis of many human respiratory viral illnesses, including SARS-CoV 2 (see [Bastard et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7857397/))
- Type II = IFN γ
  - Plays a major role in many immune responses, particularly to mycobacteria, viruses, and other pathogens
