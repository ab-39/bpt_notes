# Diabetic Ketoacidosis
#bpt/endocrinology #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
Diabetic ketoacidosis is an acute metabolic complication resulting from an absolute deficiency of insulin. Diagnosis requires:
- Capillary blood glucose >11mmol/L **or** known diabetes
- Capillary ketones >3.0mmol/L **or** ketonuria ++
- Venous pH <7.3 **or** venous bicarbonate <15mmol/L

Markers of severity include:
- Ketones >6mmol/L
- pH <7.1 and/or HCO3 <5mmol/L
- Potassium <3.5mmol/L
- GCS <12
- SpO2 <92%
- SBP <90mmHg +/- tachycardia +/- bradycardia

Management centres around:
- Rapid correction of hypovolaemia and dehydration with IV fluid
- Restoration of carbohydrate metabolism with insulin (IV) with regular capillary BSL monitoring
- Monitoring and correction of electrolyte deficiencies (particularly potassium)
- Investigation and treatment of the underlying cause
