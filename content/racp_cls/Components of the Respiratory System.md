### The Air
The quality of the air can determine both FiO2, and also other sorts of inhaled pollutants.
- - -
### Chest Wall Elastic Load 
Implicated in so called ‘respiratory embarrassment’, the chest wall elastic load determines how freely the chest wall can expand to generate pressures. Causes include:
- Abdominal distension/obesity/ascites
- Pleural effusion
- Pneumothorax
- Rib fracture
- Tumour
- - -
### Lung Elastic Load
Increased elastic load effectively reduced long compliance (i.e. increased stiffness)
- Alveolar oedema
- Atelectasis
- Infection
- Intrinsic PEEP
- - -
### Minute Ventilation Load 
High minute ventilation (e.g. to remove carbon dioxide or lactate) leads to fatiguing of the pulmonary system 
- Excess calories 
- Hypovolaemia
- PE
- Sepsis
- - -
### Resistive Load
- Bronchospasm
- Oedema/scarring
- OSA
- Upper airway obstruction
- - -
### Impaired Respiratory Drive
- Brain stem lesion
- Narcosis or other overdose
- Hypothyroidism
- Sleep-disordered breathing
- - -
### Impaired Neurotransmission
- Aminoglycosides
- ALS
- Botulism
- Spinal cord lesion
- GBS
- MG
- Neuromuscular blocking agents
- Phrenic nerve injury
- - -
### Muscle Weakness
- Electrolyte abnormalities
- Fatigue
- Mitochondrial myopathies
- Hyperaemia
- Hypoperfusion
