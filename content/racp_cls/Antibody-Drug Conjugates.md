# Antibody-Drug Conjugates
#bpt/oncology #bpt/pharmacology #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
![](Antibody-Drug%20Conjugates/image.png)
Antibody-drug conjugates are comprised of:
- Antibodies targeted to tumour-specific or tumour-associated antigens
- A stable linker that connexts the antibody to
- A cytotoxic payload

This allows for cytotoxic chemotherapy to be used while minimising some of the systemic side effects.
- - -
### Examples:
Breast cancer:
- Trastuzumab-emtansine
- Trastuzumab-deruxtecan
- Sacituzumab-govitecan

Urothelial carcinoma:
- Enfortumab-vedotin

Lung cancer:
- Telisotuzumab-vedotin

Ovarian cancer:
- Mirvatuximab-soarvtansine (targeting folate receptor ⍺)

Haematologic malignancies:
- Gemtuzumab-ozogamicin
- Brentuximab-vedotin
