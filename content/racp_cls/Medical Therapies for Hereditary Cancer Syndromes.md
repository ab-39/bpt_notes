### PARP Inhibition
Poly-ADP ribose inhibitors (e.g. olaparib) are used to reduce prepare of ssDNA breaks in cancer cells that are homologous recombination deficient. Typically, we see these used in breast or prostate cancers that are _BRCA_ or _ATM_ positive.

### Immunotherapy and dMMR Tumours
Mismatch repair (dMMR) is the most predictive marker of response to immune checkpoint inhibitor therapy.
