### Pseudomonal Colonisation
Both patients with [[bronchiectasis]] and [[cystic fibrosis]] can develop the problem of pseudomonal colonisation. This is associated with the development of a mature biofilm that increases virulence and makes treatment more difficult. 

> [!NOTE] Quorum Sensing
> Bacteria such as _Pseudomonas_ can adaptively regulate biofilm formation though a process known as _quorum sensing_. 

The first step is to confirm the diagnosis. Then, strategies to control _Pseudomonas_ include:
- Eradication therapy: two weeks of IVABx with ~3/12 of  ciprofloxacin in between
- Long term PO or nebulised ABx to reduce the burden of colonisation
- Deactivation of quorum-sensing signals
- Deactivation of biofilms 
- Airway clearance

It is important to note that there is a high risk of person-to-person transmission with _Pseudomonas_ and other infections (such as _Burkholderia cepacii_).
