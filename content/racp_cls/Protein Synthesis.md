### Transcription
_Transcription_ is the process of turning DNA into RNA, which is mediated by an enzyme called _RNA polymerase_. The final messenger RNA (mRNA) molecule has specific chemical modifications (e.g thymine -> uracil) that allow it to be translated into a protein.
- - -
### Translation
_Translation_ is the process of turning mRNA into a protein, which is mediated by proteins called _ribosomes_ which contains multiple _tRNA_ molecules which link new amino acids to the polypeptide chain. Ribosomes read mRNA in units of three bases called _codons_, that each encode specific amino acids. In addition to this, there are also _start_ and _stop_ codons.
