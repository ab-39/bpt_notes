### Overview
Bone marrow failure causes a normocytic anaemia, and we generally group its causes into three:
- Aplastic:
  - Idiopathic/autoimmune (= ‘aplastic anaemia’)
  - Medication-induced (usu. chemotherapy, also chloramphenicol, AZA, carbimazole, etc.)
  - Viral/post-viral
  - Inherited (e.g. Fanconi anaemia, 
- Infiltration:
  - Haematologic malignancies
  - Other malignancies
  - Amyloid
  - Sarcoid
- Dysfunction:
  - [[Myelodysplastic syndrome]]

A low reticulocyte count, as well as other cytopenias, are generally suggestive of bone marrow failure. A reticulocute count <1%/40 is suggestive of aplastic anaemia.
