# Wolff-Parkinson-White Syndrome
#bpt/cardiology #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
A form of _ventricular pre-excitation_ characterised by the presence of an accessory pathway between the atria and ventricles (most frequently in the left lateral AV ring or else the septum) that predisposes patients to development of an orthodromic (direction shown in diagram) AV [[re-entrant tachycardia]] (AVRT).
![](Wolff-Parkinson-White%20Syndrome/image%202.png)

β-blockers should be used with caution as they can _speed_ conduction down the accessory pathway, leading to a broad complex, irregular tachycardia. 

The risk of sudden cardiac death in WPW is around 2/1,000 patient years. The current recommendations are that if an asymptomatic patient demonstrates high-risk features on an EP study, or if they have a high risk occupation (including competitive sports) they should proceed to a catheter ablation.
- - -
### ECG Morphology
Characterised by a short PR interval and a delta wave leading to a ‘slurred upstroke’ into the QRS complex. 
![](Wolff-Parkinson-White%20Syndrome/image.png) 
An adenosine challenge can be used to ‘unmask’ pre-excitation, as it will block the AV node but not the accessory pathway. This will appear as a broad complex beat.
