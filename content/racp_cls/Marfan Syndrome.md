### Overview
Marfan syndrome is a systemic connective tissue disorder caused by pathogenic variants in the _FBN1_ gene that encodes fibrillin. It is inherited in an autosomal dominant fashion.

Cardinal features include:
- *Aortic root dilatation* 
- *Ectopia lentis*
  - In the absence of family history, the presence of these two features is considered diagnostic of MFS
- Eyes - myopia, retinal detachment, glaucoma, cataracts
- Skeletal - bone overgrowth, joint laxity and reduced mobility, disproportionately long extremities (Marfanoid habitus), chest wall deformities, scoliosis, flat feet
- CV - MR, TR
- Skin striae
- Lung bullae

Diagnostic criteria:
- If no family history:
  - Aortic root Z-score ≥2 AND ectopia lentis OR _FBN1_ mutation = MFS
  - Aortic root Z-score ≥2 AND systemic score ≥7
  - Ectopia lentis AND an _FBN1_ mutation
- If family history positive, one of:
  - Ectopia lentis 
  - A systemic score ≥7 points
  - Aortic root dilatation
