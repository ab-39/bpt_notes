# Endovascular Clot Retrieval 
#bpt/neurology #bpt/racp_cls 
- - -
### Overview
While effective, [[thrombolytic therapy in ischaemic stroke]] will not always be able to open an artery. Despite initial poor evidence, more recent evidence is showing that endovascular clot retrieval, or thrombectomy can be an important part of the arsenal to treat [[ischaemic stroke]].

When to use it?
- Clearcut benefit -> ICA and M1 occlusion; basilar occlusion 
- Benefit -> large proximal M2 occlusion 
- Ongoing research -> M3/4/etc. occlusion 

The _DAWN_ and _DEFUSE_ trials were critical in providing evidence for ECR as a therapy. Importantly, they found that:
- Treatment with favourable imaging within 24h is of benefit to patients

Building on this, _MR CLEAN LATE_ and _SELECT2_ found that ECR can, in fact, improve outcomes compared to thrombolysis alone. 

Importantly, patients who are candidates for ECR _should still get thrombolysis_ if they are eligible for it. (_EXTEND-IA_). While there are no age or clinical severity limits for ECR itself, the patient’s premorbid function is an important consideration.
