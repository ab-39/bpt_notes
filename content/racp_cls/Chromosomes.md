### Overview
Each cell contains 46 chromosomes arranged in 23 pairs:
- The first 22 pairs - the _autosomes_ - are numbered from largest to smallest
- The final pair - the _sex chromosomes_ - are labelled X and Y

Most chromosomes are made up of four components:
- A central, cinched region called the _centromere_, which plays an important rolein aligning the chromosomes during cell division
- A short arm - labelled the _p_ arm
- A long arm - labeleld the _q_ arm 
- _Telomeres_ - sections of repeated DNA at the ends of the chromosomes that play a protective role
