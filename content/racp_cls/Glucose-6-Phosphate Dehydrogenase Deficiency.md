# Glucose-6-Phosphate Dehydrogenase Deficiency
#bpt/haematology #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
An enyzmatic deficiency of G6PD that normally plays an antioxidant role in protecting cells from oxidative stress, leading to [[haemolysis]]. 
![](Glucose-6-Phosphate%20Dehydrogenase%20Deficiency/image%202.png)
X-linked inheritance, so most commonly males but less commonly homozygous females or those with atypical Lyonisation. Common in Greece and the Levant. Situations that increase oxidative stress (e.g. infection, eating fava beans, etc.) deplete the limited capacity of RBCs to deal with oxidative stress, leading to haemolysis. 

> [!TIP] Historical Point
> Pythagoras forbade his followers from eating fava beans!

- - -
### Characteristic Findings 
Blood film:
- Irregularly contracted cells
- Small protrusions = Heinz bodies
- The results of the spleen removing these inclusions = bite cells
- Hb retracting to one side of the cell = Hemi-ghost RBCs
- Hyposplenism -> Howell-Jolly bodies 
- Polychromatic macrocytes
