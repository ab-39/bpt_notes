### Overview
_Deoxyribonucleic acid_ is a molecule made up of two intertwining chains arranged in a double helix that contain the genetic information for many living creatures. This information is encoded through sequences of repeating nucleotide subunits, that are made up of four bases in two groups:
- Purines = adenine (A) and guanine (G)
- Pyrimidines = cytosine (C) and thymine (T)

These bases bind in a reliable fashion to one another on opposite chains:
- Adenine to Thymine
- Cytosine to Guanine

### The Mitochondrial Genome
Mitochondria contain many copies of circular, double-stranded DNA (mtDNA). The mitochondrial genome is essential for normal mitochondrial maintenance and function,and ontains 16,569 nucleotides and 37 genes.
