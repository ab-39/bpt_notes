# Measles
#bpt/infection #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
_Measles morbilivirus_ is a major cause of epidemics due to the recent growth of the anti-vaccination movement. It is a single-stranded virus that is spread via airborne transmission, with droplets remaining airborne for up to 2h. It is _incredibly_ infectious, with a [[reproductive number]] of 12-18, and has a [[herd immunity threshold]] of 95%. Patients are considered infectious from four days prior to the emergence of rash to up to four days after. 
- - -
### Clinical Syndrome
1. Incubation period - 6-21 (usually 8-14) days
2. Prodrome period - 2-4 days
   - Fever, coryza, cough, conjunctivitis/conjunctival suffusion
   - Koplik spots - small, white spots on the pharyngeal mucosa which is usually erythematous
3. Exanthem phase
   - Macular, erythematous rash that begins on the head and descends
   - Persistent prodrome symptoms and lymphadenopathy

Note that a modified, attenuated illness is posisble in those with incomplete immunity. 

The [[case fatality rate]] of measles is as high as 30% in developing countries. 

Complications include:
- Croup
- Bronchopneumonia
- Sinusitis
- Bacterial superinfection
- Bronchiectasis (late)
- Encephalitis (~1/1000)
- Subacute sclerosing panencephalitis (~1/10,000) - subacute and inevitably fatal complication of measles, occurring 7-10y after primary infection
- - -
### Treatment
There is no proven treatment for measles. 

Vitamin A deficiency appears to contribute to the development of complications and impaired recovery, and should be administered to all adults with severe disease. 

Measles has been found to be susceptible to ribavirin _in vitro_, and as such it can be considered in patients who have severe diseases (particularly pneumonia) or who are immunosuppressed.
- - -
### Prophylaxis
The MMR live vaccine is used to prevent measles (as well as mumps and rubella). Immunisation with two doses of the MMR vaccine, at least 28 days apart is recommended for all children. In outbreak or exposure settings:
- Immune globulin is _not_ recommended as a routine treatment measure 
- Nonimmune + immunocompetent + nonpregnant -> MMR vaccine: first dose within 72h + second dose after 28d
- Nonimmune + immunocompromised **or** pregnant -> IVIG within six days followed by vaccination after six months
