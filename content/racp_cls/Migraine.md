### What is migraine?
Migraine is one of the [[primary headache disorders]] characterised by recurrent attacks lasting up to three days, with typical clinical features including:
- Unilateral location
- Pulsing quality
- Moderate-to-severe pain intensity
- Aggravation by or causing avoidance of routine daily activities
And associated with nausea/vomiting and photophobia/phonophobia. 
- - -
### Natural History
Migraine typically consists of four phases:
- Prodrome
- Aura
- Headache
- Postdrome 

#### Aura
Aura is a critical discriminator in migraine, and are typically a stereotypical neurological symptom that occurs prior to the onset of the headache itself. Visual changes are the most common, followed by hemiplegic motor changes. 

#### Triggers
Common triggers include sleep, diet, exercise, hydration, caffeine, environment, relaxation, supplements, chocolate, and wine.

### Management
Treatment of migraine has two goals:
1. Cessation of headache within 2h
2. Prevention of recurrence within 48h

Additionally to this, there are also prophylactic treatments aimed at reducing the frequency and severity of attacks. 

#### Acute treatment
Simple analgesia may help with symptoms, but will not terminate the attack. The typical dose is 900mg aspirin or 600mg ibuprofen. 

_Triptans_ are disease-specific rescue medications, that agonise serotonin 1B/1D receptors. They can also cause vasoconstriction, so tend to be avoided in patients with comorbid arterial disease. There is a new emerging therapy - rimegepant (CRGP antagonist), which block vasoconstriction rather than causing vasoconstriction.
