### Summary
The Fick principle states that _the total uptake of a substance by the peripheral tissues is equal to the product of the blood flow to the tissues, and the arterial-venous concentration difference of the substance_. The VO2max is the maximal level of oxidative metabolism using the major muscle groups. [Deranged Physiology](https://derangedphysiology.com/main/cicm-primary-exam/cardiovascular-system/Chapter-811/ficks-principle-cardiac-output-measurement)has an excellent explanation.

i.e., for cardiac output and oxygen consumption:
> $V_{O2} = (Qt * Hb * 1.34) * (S_aO2 — S_vO2)$

Where, 
- VO2 = oxygen utilisation
- Q = cardiac output
- Hb = haemoglobin
- SaO2/SvO2 = arterial/venous saturation of O2 

This is not to be confused with [[Physics for Medicine/Fick’s Law of Diffusion|Fick’s law of diffusion]].
