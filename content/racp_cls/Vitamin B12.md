### Overview
Haptocorrin is released by the salivary glands to stabilise B12 on its way to the stomach.
In the stomach, intrinsic factor binds B12 to stabilise it on the way to the terminal ileum.
In the terminal ileum, the IF-B12 binds to the cub-am receptor, where B12 is taken up and then bound to transcobalamin II, which delivers it to tissues around the body.
