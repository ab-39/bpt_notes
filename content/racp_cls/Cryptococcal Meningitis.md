# Cryptococcal Meningitis
#bpt/infection #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
- Dexamethasone is contraindicated.
- Very strongly associated with raised ICP (Sx = diplopia) - so may need to repeat the LP -> important to check the opening pressures. 
- Symptoms are often vague - headache, fever, altered mental state **_without_** meningism
- Generally occurs in immunocompromised hosts (e.g. HIV), but _C. gatii_ can affect the immunocompetent
- Cryptococcal antigens very sensitive
