# Tricuspid Regurgitation
#bpt/cardiology #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
Although all people have some degree of regurgitant flow across the tricuspid valve (it is the basis for calculating pulmonary arterial pressures), from time to time it can become problematic. That said, around 80% of TR cases are functional related to RV or tricuspid annular dilatation.

Most TV surgery is carried out in conjunction with left-sided surgery, as outcomes after isolated right heart surgery are poor. 

Based on the [[Physics for Medicine/Bernoulli Equation|Bernoulli equation]], we know that the velocity of the TR jet will be proportional to the right ventricular systolic pressure (RVSP). 
- - -
### Treatment
Despite the significant mortality and morbidity of severe TR, it remains a space in cardiology where there a limited treatment options. Management often centres around the use of diuretics, as surgery can often be high risk. TEER (TriClip) is now emerging as a key treatment option for severe TR, with good evidence (_TRILUMINATE_) for reduction in disease severity.
