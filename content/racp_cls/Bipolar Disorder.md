### Overview
As the name implies, bipolar disorder involves fluctuations between depression and mania. The ‘classic’ screening question for mania is:
> Have you ever gone three days/nights without the need for sleep?

It is important not to misdiagnose a depressive episode of bipolar as major depression, as inappropriate treatment with an antidepressant can _trigger mania_, and the end of a manic episode is when a patient with bipolar is at the highest risk of suicide.

Note that NSAIDs increase serum concentrations of lithium through an unknown mechanism.
