# Diffuse Idiopathic Skeletal Hyperosteosis
#bpt/rheumatology
- - -
### Overview
Aetiology unknown; noninflammatory condition characterised by calcification of the spinal ligaments and entheses (i.e. where bone meets tendon/ligament); typical XR finding is ‘flowing linear calcification, crossing the joint space’.  _Not_ associated with HLA-B27.
