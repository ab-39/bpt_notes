# Complement
#bpt/rheumatology

Major component of innate immune system with three overlapping pathways.

Primary functions:
1. Identify foreign and damaged materials
2. Eliminated
3. Provide responses 

Low complement levels associated with SLE -> used to monitor (particularly C4)

Also associated with:
1. MCTD
2. SS
3. APLS
4. Cryoglobulinaemia 
5. PAN
