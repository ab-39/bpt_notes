### ANA Staining Patterns
A = Homogeneous:
- Entire nucleus is diffusely stained
- Suggests that the antibodies are directed against histone proteins rather than specific extractable nuclear antigens
- Strongly associated with anti-dsDNA and SLE; or else with histone proteins in drug-induced SLE

B = Speckled:
- Fine or corase speckles throughout the nucleus
- Caused by antibodies against:
  - Fine = Ro, topoisomerase I, Ku, Mi-2, TIF1γ, La
  - Smooth = Sm, U1, RNP

C = Centromere:
- Presence of 30-60 uniform speckles throughout the nuclues that align with chromosomes at metaphase in mitotic cells
- Caused by antibodies against proteins A, B, and C

D = Nucleolar:
- Homogeneous or speckled staining of the nucleolus produced by autoantibodies agains U3-RNP, RNA polymerase, and topoisomerase
