# Inflammatory Back Pain
#bpt/rheumatology #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Definition
ASAS defines inflammatory back pain as at least **4** out of 5 of:
1. Age at onset yonuger than 40y
2. Insidious onset
3. Improvement with exercise
4. No improvement with rest
5. Pain at night (often causing waking from sleep, in the second half of the night)

Characteristically, these are also often associated with buttock pain and a good response to NSAIDs.
