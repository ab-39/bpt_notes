### Overview


### Pathophysiology
Initiation of inflammation in gout is dependent on IL-1 and _the inflammasome_. Two signals are required to activate the NLRP3 inflammasome:
1. Phagocytosis of MSU crystals
2. Unknown 

IL-1 then induces secondary inflammation through cytokines, which recruit neutrophils and cause ROS formation. This pathophysiology is also implicated in familial Mediterranean fever and cryopyrin-associated periodic syndromes.

### Diagnosis
DECT can differentiate gout from other crystal arthropathies and detect subclnical tophi, due to the unique dual X-ray attenuation profile of sodium (in MSU) compared to other common culprit crystals in other conditions. 

The double contour sign on USS is pathognomic for gout,

### Treatment
Febuxostat has favourable serum uric acid profile compared to allopurinol. Reductions in gout flare incidence and tophi tend to be similar.  Febuxostat causes inhibition of both reduced and oxidised forms of xanthine oxidase. However, it is not recommended in individuals with a history of cardiovascular disease.
