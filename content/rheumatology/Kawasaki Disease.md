### Overview
Kawasaki disease is a vasculitis affecting children that is characterised by an otherwise-unexplained fever lasting for ≥5 days, as well as four of the following five diagnostic criteria:
1. Conjunctivitis (bilateral)
2. Rash (polymorphous, widespread)
3. Extremities (erythema and desquamation)
4. Adenitis (cervical lymphadenopathy)
5. Mucosal changes (oral)

This gives the handy mnemonic **Warm CREAM**. 

This is not an uncommon condition in paediatrics, and is one of the few indications for aspirin in children.
