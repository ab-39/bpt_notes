# ESR
#bpt/rheumatology

Erythrocyte sedimentation rate. 

The rate in mm/hr at which erythrocytes fall when placed in a vertical tube -> i.e. how viscous is the plasma?

The slower they sediment, the more likely it is that some process (i.e. inflammation) is activating fibrinogen.

Falsely elevated in:
1. Increased age
2. Female sex
3. Anaemia
4. ESKD
5. Obesity 
6. Hypergammaglobuinaemia
7. High room temperature
