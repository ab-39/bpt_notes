# Eosinophilic Granulomatosis with Polyangiitis
#bpt/rheumatology #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Eponym
Churg-Strauss Disease 
- - -
### Overview
EGPA is a not-uncommon vasculitis that is typically (~50%) [[ANCA]] positive, usually for p-/MPO-ANCA (although ANCA does not correlate with disease specificity).

Clinical features that suggest EGPA include:
- History of [[respiratory/Asthma|asthma]] - although the vasculitis may present years afterwards
- Pulmonary opacities
- Eosinophilia

EGPA typically affects the skin, peripheral nerves, and heart, bowel involvement is also possible, and kidney involvement is likely more common than suspected.

Most patients with EGPA respond to steroids alone, although mepolizumab (anti-IL5) can also be considered. 

Disease severity is graded with the Five Factor Score:
1. Age >65y
2. Renal impairment at presentation
3. Cariac insufficiency
4. GI involvement
5. Absence of ENT involvement (i.e. ENT involvement is _protective_)

The higher the score, the worse the 5y prognosis (2/5 points = 2/5 5y mortality).
