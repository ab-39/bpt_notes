### What is vasculitis?
The vasculitides are a range of conditions characterised by _inflammation of the blood vessels_ that manifest clinically as palpable purpura.
- This typically results in a multi-organ disease with systemic features. 

There are also specific syndromes that can be caused by vasculitis, e.g.:
- Pulmonary-renal syndrome -> GPA or MPA
- Mononeuritis multiplex (DDx. = diabetes)
- - -
### Key Differentials
- Thrombocytopenia
- Infective endocarditis/atrial myxomatosis with emboli/mycotic aneurysm with emboli
- Septicaemia 
- Cholesterol emboli
- Amyloidosis
- - -
### Laboratory Tests for Vasculitis
- FBE, CRP, ESR
- Urinalysis and serum creatinine -> is there haematuria or renal failure?
- Platelet count, APTT and PT
- Liver function tests
- Autoantibodies - ANA, ANCA, rheumatoid factor, cryoglobulins, lupus anticoagulant, antiacardiolipin
- Complement - C3, C4
- Hepatitis B (strong association with polyarteritis nodosa), HCV, EBV, CMV
- CXR
- Arteriography - for PAN, Takayasu arteritis, and GCA
- Ultrasound - for GCA
- CT/MRI/PET
- Tissue/skin/artery/renal biopsy +/- immunofluorescence for IgA
