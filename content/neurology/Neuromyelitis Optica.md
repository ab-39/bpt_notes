# Neuromyelitis Optica
#bpt/neurology
- - -
### Overview
NMO is an immune-mediated demyelinating disorder characterised by bilateral signs and longitudinally extensive lesions spanning three or more vertebral segments, and tending to display central involvement. The presenting complaint of NMO is often bilateral optic neuritis (which differentiates it from the typically unilateral optic neuritis of [[multiple sclerosis]]). The confirmatory diagnostic test is the presence of serum autoantibodies against aquaporin 4 receptors (AQP4), and in rare cases, the CNS protein myelin oligodendrocyte glycoprotein (MOG). Acute flares of NMO are treated with glucocorticoids and/or PLEX. Long-term treatment typically involves use of monoclonal antibodies:
- Terminal complement inhibitor = eculizumab
- B-cell depletion = inebilizumab
- IL-6 antagonism = satralizumab

Azathioprine and rituximab can also be considered.
