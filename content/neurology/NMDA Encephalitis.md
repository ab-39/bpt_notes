# NMDA Encephalitis

### Overview
Anti-NMDAR encephalitis is a subtype of autoimmune encephalitis caused by antibodies against the GluN1 subunit of the NMDA receptor. It is most often associated with malignancies (particularly ovarian teratomas) and HSV-encephalitis (usually within two months). 

It has a mean age of onset of 21y, and a fourfold female predominance. 

The clinical syndrome includes:
- Adults - behavioural disturbance, seizures, reduced level of consciousness, movement disorders (particularly facial dyskinesias)
- Children - seizures, abnormal movements, irritability 

Immunomodulation is the first-line treatment - steorids, IVIg, or PLEX. If patients fail these, rituximab or cyclophosphamide can be considered. 

Approximately 5% of patients with anti-NMDAR encephalitis will go on to devleop a demyelinating disorder.

Buzzword = extreme delta brush pattern on EEG
