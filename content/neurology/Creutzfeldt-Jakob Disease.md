### Overview
CJD is a prion disorder characterised by a _highly_ rapidly progressive dementia. Diagnosis requires clinical signs (cerebrellar, extrapyramidal, extraocular, myoclonus, or akinetic mutism) **and** one of:
- EEG changes
- 14-3-3 protein in CSF (needs precautions for LP)
- CSF RT-QUIC analysis
- Ribbon sign on MRI
