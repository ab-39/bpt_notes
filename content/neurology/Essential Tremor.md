### Overview
Essential tremor is a younger onset disease that typically affects the upper limbs, and occasionally the head/face/voice. Although typically symmetrical, it can initially present unilaterally. It is most typically an action or postural tremor. 

Essential tremor usually improves with age, and can be treated with β-blockers, primidone, and gabapentin. It is ‘classically’ improved with alcohol, although this is not a recommended treatment.
