### Overview
Cerebral autosomal dominant arteriopathy with subcortical infarcts and leucoencephalopathy, or CADASIL for short, is a clinical syndrome that leads to early stroke and other neurological symptoms, including:
- Migraine with aura
- Subcortical features - e.g. pseudobulbar affect, gait dyspraxia, urinary incontinence
- Dementia
- Mood disorder
- Acute encephalopathy
- Seizures

It is driven by frameshift mutations in the _NOTCH3_ gene on chromosome 19, that encodes transmembrane receptors. 

MRI shows white matter changes, as well as lacunes following strokes, but the diagnostic test of choice is a skin biopsy looking for the deposition of granular osmiophilic material in blood vessel walls.
