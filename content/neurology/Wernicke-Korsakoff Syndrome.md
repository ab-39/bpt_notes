# Wernicke-Korsakoff Syndrome

- - -
### Overview
The end state of too much alcohol. Wernicke syndrome is characterised by a classic triad of:
1. Ataxia
2. Ophthalmoplegia with vertical nystagmus
3. Global confusion
In the presence of malnourishment. Korsakoff syndrome presents with a profound anterograde amnesia. While rare in the general population, this occurs in up to 1/8 alcholics. Prevented with parenteral thiamine but not ‘treatable’ per se.
