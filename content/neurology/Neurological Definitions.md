# Neurological Definitions
#bpt/neurology
- - -
| Term           | Meaning in Plain English                                     |
|----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Focal          | Referable to a single site in the central nervous system     |
| Encephalopathy | Altered consciousness persisting for ≥24h, including lethargy, irritation, or change in personality/behaviour |
| Tremor         | Rhythmic oscillation of a body part<br>‘Resting’ -> prominent at rest<br>‘Postural’ -> prominent on assuming a posture<br>‘Kinetic’ -> prominent on reaching for a target<br>‘Action’ -> prominent on carrying out an action |
| Dystonia       | Invountary, patterned, sustained, or repeated muscle contractions often associated with twisting movements or abnormal posture |
| Athetosis      | Slow, involuntary, distal, writhing movements of the arms and hands |
| Chorea         | Rapid, semipurposeful, graceful, dancelike, nonpatterned movements affecting proximal and distal muscle groups |
| Myoclonus      | Sudden, brief, jerklike, arrhythmic muscle twitches          |
