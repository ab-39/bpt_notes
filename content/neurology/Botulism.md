### Overview 
Botulism is an acute and potentially fatal [[neuromuscular junction disorder]] caused by the botulinum neurotoxin released by _Clostridium botulinum_ bacteria, often found colonising deep wounds or in improperly cooked food. It presents with a rapidly progressive descending flaccid paralysis, with eventual respiratory and autonomic involvement. The typical presentation is with blurred vision, diplopia, facial weakness, and dysarthria/dysphagia. Treatment is supportive, with IV botulism antitoxin. Mortality is typically low. This is a notifiable disease.
