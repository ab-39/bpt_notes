# Young-Onset Dementia
#bpt/neurology #bpt/racp_cls
- - -
### Overview
Young-onset dementia refers to dementia beginning before the age of 65y. Diagnosis is  often delayed, and associated with reduced life expectancy. The vast majority of causes remain neurodegenerative or vascular, although the presentation can be atypical. Causes typically fall under three broad umbrellas:
- [[neurology/Alzheimer Disease|Alzheimer Disease]] = characterised by short term memory difficulty; variants commoner in younger people include:
  - Logopenic aphasia (a subtype of PPA)
  - Posterior cortical atrophy (Benson’s syndrome) - presenting with visuospatial difficulties 
  - Dysexecutive Alzheimer disease 
  - Corticobasal syndrome (on a spectrum with [[Parkinson’s disease]] and related syndromes)
- [[neurology/Frontotemporal Dementia|Frontotemporal Dementia]] and related syndromes:
  - bvFTD = characterised by behavioural and personality changes with marked executive dysfunction, disinhibition, and hyperorality 
  - Non-fluent/agrammatic PPA = affecting grammar and syntax
  - Semantic PPA = affecting words, concepts, and meanings
- Parkinsonian Syndromes - less comon in the <65 age group
  - [[Parkinson’s Disease|Parkinson’s disease]]
  - Multisystem atrophy - rarely associated with dementia
  - Progressive supranuclear palsy
  - Corticobasilar degeneration
  - Dementia with Lewy bodies 

Other important causes to consider include:
- [[neurology/Huntington Disease|Huntington disease]]
- [[Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis]] and other motoneurone diseases - overlap with FTD due to C9ORF72 mutations
- Prion disorders
- Alcohol, with subsequent [[neurology/Wernicke-Korsakoff Syndrome|Wernicke-Korsakoff Syndrome]] 
- Medications
- Heavy metals
- Autoimmune causes (typically rapid in onset) - e.g. LGI1 encephalitis, anti-IGLON5 disease
- Infectious causes - e.g. [[syphilis]] 
- Brain tumours 
- Epilepsy
- Genetic causes - e.g. Down syndrome

While vascular causes of dementia in under-65’s is relatively less common, but should be suspected after a stroke, in the presence of significant vascular risk factors, or if onset is very early in life as is the case with [[CADASIL]].

### Genetics
Genes implicated in young-onset dementia include:
- Alzheimer disease -> _PSEN1_ (commonest), _PSEN2_, _APP_
  - Note that ApoE mutations confers a strong susceptibility for AD with earlier age of onset, but is also an important cause of regular-onset AD
- FTD -> C9ORF72, tau, progranulin
- Prion -> _PRNP_
- Huntington disease -> _HD_
