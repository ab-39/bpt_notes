### Comparison of MS Drugs
| Drug                                                         | Route | Mechanism                                                    | Key Points                                                   |
|--------------------------------------------------------------|-------|--------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Interferon                                                   | SC    | Unclear                                                      | Low efficacy, risk of flu-like symptoms                      |
| Glatiramer acetate                                           | SC    | Unclear - ?resembles MBP and triggers T-cell anergy          | Low efficacy, safe if trying to conceive                     |
| Teriflunomide                                                | PO    | Inhibits pyrimidine synthesis by blocking dihydroorotate dehydrogenase | Low efficacy, risk of transaminitis, cytopenias, teratogenic, and TB reactivation<br>Rapid elimination available with cholestyramine/activated charcoal |
| Dimethylfumarate                                             | PO    | Unclear - ?NRF2 pathway                                      | Medium efficacy, mild side effect profile (can be diminished with aspirin), monitor lymphocytes |
| S1PR modulators <br>(e.g. fingolimod, ozanimod, ponesimod, siponimod) | PO    | Block S1PR, preventing lymphocyte egress from lymph nodes    | Medium efficacy, needs first-dose cardiac monitoring (risk of heart block, bradycardia); macular oedema; metabolic side effect; LFTs/DILI; immunosuppression |
| Cladribine                                                   | PO    | Antagonises adenosine deaminase -> prevents DNA replication in lymphocytes -> lymphodepletion | Medium efficacy; moderately immunosuppressive                |
| Natalizumab                                                  | IV    | ⍺-4-integrin blocker, reducing lymphocyte transfer across the blood-brain barrier | High efficacy, very few side effects; increases risk of JC viral infection (screen antibodies) -> PML (subacute onset encephalitis) |
| CD20 modulators <br>(e.g. ocrelizumab, ofatumumab)           | IV/SC | Deplete B-cells                                              | High efficacy; immunosuppressive, hypogammaglobulinaemia     |
| Alemtuzumab (Campath)                                        | IV    | Depletion of lymphocytes via CD52 receptor                   | High efficacy; strongly immunosuppressive (‘liquid AIDS’); risk of secondary autoimmune disease |
