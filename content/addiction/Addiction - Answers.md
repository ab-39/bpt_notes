Alcohol is an RF for cancer development - which metabolite plays a prominent role in cancer development?

B. Acetaldehyde disrupts DNA synthesis and repair, predisposing to carcinogenic mutations.

A. Acamprosate. Best evidence, and: naltrexone is out as he is on opioids for pain; disulfiram is rare; diazepam is out because of the cirrhosis.
