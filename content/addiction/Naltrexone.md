# Naltrexone
#bpt/addiction

Long-acting µ receptor antagonist -> reduces pleasurable sensation of alcohol by blocking endorphin mediated effects.

Dose 50mg daily. Can be initiated while still drinking. 

Efficacy compared to placebo:
1. Reduced risk of heavy drinking by 83%
2. All drinking 47%

Contraindications:
1. Active opioid use (for pain management or OAT)
2. Liver disease - acute hepatitis, liver failure, or LFTS > 3x ULN
3. Caution in lactation and breastfeeding 

Common side effects usu. transient (1-2/52): nausea, headache, dizziness, fatigue. Less commonly liver injury.

Part of the ‘Sinclair Method’ = ‘pharmacological extinction’. Patient continues to drink, taking naltrexone beforehand. Blocks positive reinforcement of endorphins to ‘unlearn’ alcohol.
