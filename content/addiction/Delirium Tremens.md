# Delirium Tremens
#bpt/addiction

The most severe and feared complication of alcohol withdrawal. 
1. 48-96h after cessation
2. Autonomic hyperactivity: fever, tachycardia, HTN, sweats
3. Rapid onset, fluctuating disturbance of attention and cognition, sometimes with hallucinations

RFs:
1. Prior withdrawal seizures or FTs
2. Specific signs of chronic alcohol intake: cirrhosis, malnutrition, liver disease, low Mg
3. Development of withdrawal with a positive BAC
4. Age > 30 
5. Recent misuse of other CNS depressants
6. Concurrent illness

Management:
1. In patient
2. Diazepam
3. Can consider antipsychotics
