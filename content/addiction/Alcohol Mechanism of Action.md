# Alcohol Mechanism of Action
#bpt/addiction

Alcohol -> increases the actions of inhibitory GABA-a receptors, while antagonising excitatory NMDA receptors.

Acute intoxication initially causes disinhibition and behavioural arousal (BAC <0.1), followed by impaired judgement and reaction times (BAC 0.1-0.2), ataxia (0.2-0.3) then sedation (0.4+). 

Dependence is due to chronic ingestion leading to neuroadaptation with the brain trying to restore glutamate function by down regulating GABA-a receptors and increasing NMDA receptors -> tolerance.
