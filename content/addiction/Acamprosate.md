# Acamprosate
#bpt/addiction

Calcium N-acetyl homotaurine. Modulates NMDA and GABA receptors. 

Compared to placebo:
1. Reduces rate of any drinking
2. Increases abstinence duration by 11 days
3. No effect on heavy drinking

Contraindicated in:
1. Renal disease (Cr > 120µmol/L or eGFR < 30)
2. CPC cirrhosis
3. Caution in lactation/pregnancy - class B2/3

Common side effects: rash, diarrhoea, changes in libido, less commonly N+V, itch, erythema. 

Dose usu. 666mg TDS.
