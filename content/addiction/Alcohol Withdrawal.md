# Alcohol Withdrawal
#bpt/addiction

Most withdrawal occurs within a seven day period.

Minor Sx:
1. Tremor
2. Anxiety
3. Headache
4. Diaphoresis
5. Palpitations
6. Loss of appetite 
7. Etc. 

Serious withdrawal symptoms occur in 10% of patients:
Risk of seizures peaks at ~48h.
[[Alcoholic hallucinosis]]: 12-48h
[[Delirium tremens]]: 48-96h

Mainstay of management is benzodiazepines (usu. diazepam, oxazepam - which is a phase 1 metabolite of diazepam at 1/3rd strength - in liver failure). Can front load if anticipate high risk of seizures or DT (i.e. as much as 60mg of diazepam in 6 hours). Otherwise, use ABCD rule to initiate benzodiazepines:
1. Alcohol withdrawal diagnosed
2. BAC is falling
3. Comorbidities have ben assessed
4. Drugs (concurrently used) have been assessed
