# Alcohol Withdrawal Seizures
#bpt/addiction
- - -
Early benzodiazepine treatment typically prevents the development of withdrawal seizures. 

1. 2-5% of alcohol dependent individuals
2. Within 6-48h of cessation in 90% of cases
3. Typically GTCS
4. Often occur in clusters 
5. 1/3rd progress to delirium tremens
6. RFs: concurrent withdrawal from benzodiazepines or other sedatives; number of past withdrawal
