# Alcoholic Hallucinosis
#bpt/addiction

1. Onset 12-24h of abstinence
2. Typically resolve within 24-48h (i.e. about when DTs kick in)
3. Usually visual hallucinations (animals/insects), sometimes auditory or tactile
4. Ego dystonic hallucinations, patient is aware
5. Normal cognition and orientations
6. Normal vitals (usually)
7. RFs: genetics
