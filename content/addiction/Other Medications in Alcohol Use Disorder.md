# Other Medications in Alcohol Use Disorder
#bpt/addiction

1. Disulfiram

Aversive agent -> discourages drinking by casing an accumulation of acetaldehyde. 

Conflicting evidence.

250-500mg/day.

Not common.

2. Baclofen

Off label GABA-B agonist

Dose from 30-300mg.

Really cleared -> good in liver disease.

Effective, but note is also a muscle relaxant so comes with concomitant issues.

3. Topiramate

3rd line agent
Also off label
Start at 25mg, uptitrtte over 8 weeks to 300mg
For patients who fail conventional therapy
