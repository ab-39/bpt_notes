# Cerebral Amyloid Angiopathy
#bpt/geriatrics
- - -
### Overview
CAA arises from the same fundamental pathology as [[neurology/Alzheimer Disease|Alzheimer Disease]], but with a predominant phenotype of bleeding - usually intraparenchymal bleeds or localised subarachnoid haemorrhages, with cortical superficial siderosis. This is driven by accumulations of Aβ protein in the cortical and leptomeningeal blood vessel walls. 

While antiplatelets can often be safely continued in patients with CAA, anticoagulation typically requires a thoughtful assessment of the risk/benefit balance.
