# Male Hypogonadism and Ageing
#bpt/endocrinology #bpt/geriatrics
- - -
### Overview
Testosterone levels decline with age, but treatment is only generally indicated if:
- There are signs/symptoms consistent with testosterone deficiency
**and**
- Serum testosterone <11nmol/L

There may be a risk of polycythaemia or worsening of androgen-dependent cancers with testosterone supplementation. Additionally, exogenous testosterone can inhibit spermatogenesis.
