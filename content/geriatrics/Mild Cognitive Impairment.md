# Mild Cognitive Impairment
#bpt/geriatrics
- - -
### Overview
MCI represents an intermediate stage between the normal processes of ageing and full-blown dementia syndromes, where impairments in one or more domains of cognition are present, but not to the point where there is interference with activities of daily living. 

The rate of reversion from MCI _back_ to normal cognition is high (~40%), particularly if the patient has any of: 
- Only a single cognitive domain affected
- Normal hippocampal volume
- Comorbid depressoin
- High anticholinergic burden (anticholinergics have an OR of 1.50 for development of dementia!)
- Higher cognitive cscores
- Absence of _APOE_ 

Treatment centres around optimisation of underlying risk factors (such as blood pressure control, particularly in middle age). There is mixed evidence for cognitive training, and no evidence for cholinesterase inhibitors in MCI.
