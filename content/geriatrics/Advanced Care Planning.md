### Overview
From the National Framework:
> “A voluntary process of planning for future health and personal care whereby the person's values, beliefs and preferences are made known to guide decision-making at a future time when that person cannot make or communicate their decisions"

There is [RCT-level evidence](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20332506/) that patient’s wishes were almost _three times as likely_ to have their wishes respected when they completed an advanced care directive. They also found that values based directives were of greater use than instructional directives. 

An example of a framework for discussing advanced care planning is the ICE-REMAP framework:
- I = ideas
- C = concerns
- E = expectations
- R = reframe the situation
- E = expect empotion
- M = map out important values
- A = align with the patient and family around these values
- P = propose a plan
