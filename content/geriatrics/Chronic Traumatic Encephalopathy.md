### Overview
_Dementia pugilistica_ has been appreciated as an entity for over a century, and is characterised by the deposision of p-tau aggregates in neurons (+/- thorn-shaped astrocytes) at the depth of a cortical sulcus around small vessels, with a history of repeated head trauma.
