# Vasopressin Receptor 2
#bpt/renal #bpt/physiology

Activated by vasopressin/ADH, which is released in response to changes in plasma osmolality.

Works by up regulating transport of aquaporin 2 from intracellular vesicles to cell membranes. 

Mutations in this receptor lead to nephrogenic diabetes insipidus. 

Blocked in the distal nephron and collection duct by [[pharmacology/Tolvaptan|Tolvaptan]]:
* Prevents binding of ADH
* Reduces expression of aquaporin
* Increases water excretion
