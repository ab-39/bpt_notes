# Membranoproliferative Glomerulonephritis
#bpt/renal
- - -
### Overview
MPGN is a _histological entity_ characterised characterised by hypercellularity and thickening of the glomerular basement membrane (‘tram tracks’). Clinically, it can present with either [[nephritic syndrome]], or in severe cases, [[nephrotic syndrome]] (i.e. a mixed picture). 

MPGN is typically either mediated by immunoglobulins or complement, the first of which occurs as a primary disease in children or else secondarily to a variety of conditions, and the second of which occurs typically after exposure to a trigger causing complement dysregulation. In either case, it is associated with reduced serum complement levels.

Management involves steroids and treatment of the underlying cause.
