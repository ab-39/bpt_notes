#bpt/renal

### Overview
| Condition (Inheritance)   | Protein/Region Affected                         | Manifestations                                               | Analogous Drug/Condition                                     |
|---------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Bartter Syndrome<br>(AR)  | NKCC2 in the TAL<br>(ROMK, CCKb, Barttin)       | Hypokalaemia, metabolic alkalosis, <br>***hypocalcaemia***, <br>hypercalciuria +/- renal stones,<br>hypotension | Frusemide                                                    |
| Gitelman Syndrome<br>(AR) | NCC in the DCT                                  | Hypokalaemia,<br>metabolic alkalosis,<br>***hypercalcaemia***,<br>hypocalciuria,<br>hypotension | Thiazide diuretics                                           |
| Liddle Syndrome<br>(AD)   | ENaCs in the collecting duct (gain of function) | Hypokalaemia,<br>metabolic alkalosis,<br>hyporeninaemia,<br>hypoaldosteronaemia,<br>hypertension | Conn syndrome - **but** the actual levels of renin and aldosterone are low as this is a disorder of ENaCs |
