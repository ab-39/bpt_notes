### What is metabolic alkalosis?
Typically, metabolic alkalosis is a process that leads to the depletion of the body’s stores of acid (e.g. prolonged vomiting) or augmentation of the body’s stores of alkaline substances (e.g. milk alkaline syndrome) 
- - -
### Compensation for metabolic alkalosis 
To compensate for a metabolic alkalosis, the respiratory system is typically moved into a state of _hypoventilation_, so as to retain CO2 and attempt to normalise blood pH. This only occurs on an acute phase to a complete level:
- PaCO2 increases by 0.7mmHg for each 1mmol/L increase in [HCO3-]

pCO2 ≈ (2/3)*[HCO3]+20

Where 40 is the normal pCO2 value
