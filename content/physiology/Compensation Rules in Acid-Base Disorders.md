# Compensation Rules in Acid-Base Disorders
#bpt/physiology
- - -
### 1-2-4-5 Rule
|             | 10mmHg rise in CO2    | 10mmHg drop in CO2    |
|-------------|-----------------------|-----------------------|
| **Acute**   | 1mmol/L rise in HCO3- | 2mmol/L drop in HCO3- |
| **Chronic** | 4mmol/L rise in HCO3- | 5mmol/L drop in HCO3- |
