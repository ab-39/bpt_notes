# Respiratory Acidosis
#bpt/physiology
- - -
### What is respiratory acidosis?
Respiratory acidosis is a failure of ventilation to adequately remove CO2.
- - -
### Acute and Chronic Compensation
Acute metabolic compensation for a respiratory acidosis will involve the release of HCO3- from stores within the cells, etc., leading to:
1. 1mmol/L increase in HCO3- for every 10mmHg increase in pCO2 acutely

Chronic compensation for a respiratory acidosis is mediated by the resorption of HCO3- in the kidneys, leading to:
2. 4mmol/L increase in HCO3- for every 10mmHg increase in pCO2 chronically
