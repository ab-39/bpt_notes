# Leptospirosis
#bpt/infection
- - -
### Overview
* Zoonotic infections caused by pathogenic species in the genus Leptospira
* Spread through contact with urine from infected animals, most commonly in the tropics, with an incubation period of 2-30 (usually 7-14) days 
* Illness (can be) biphasic:
  * Acute - nonspecific febrile illness with myalgias, headaches, and ***conjunctival suffusion*** - a minority of cases progress to hepatic and renal failure (icteric leptospirosis or *Weil’s* disease)
    * Rash tends not to be a prominent feature
  * Immune phase - associated with immune-mediated complications (systemic symptoms, aseptic meningitis, uveitis)
* Diagnosis is usually with serology or PCR
* Early treatment with penicillins or ceftriaxone may limit the progress of the disease
