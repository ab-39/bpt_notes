# Nocardia
#bpt/infection
- - -
### Overview
Environmental organism - appearance = Gram-positive beaded rods:
![](Nocardia/image.png)
High propensity for causing cerebral abscesses in immunosuppressed patients, often concomitant pulmonary involvement with cavitating lesions. Treatment is with TMP-SMX. 

Similar appearance to _Actinomyces_, differentiated by the fact that _Nocardia_ is acid-fast.
