# Acute Rheumatic Fever
#bpt/infection
- - -
### Overview
Diagnosis is with Jones’ criteria: 
- 2 major + 1 minor criteria
- Recurrent: three major criteira

Major criteria:
- Carditis
- Polyarthritis
- Sydenham’s chorea (antibodies against lysoganglioside and D1/D2R)
- Erythema marginatum
- Subcutaneous nodules

Minor criteria:
- Polyarthralgia (monoarthralgia in high-risk populations)
- Fever >38.5
- ESR/CRP elevated
- Prolonged PR interval 

Do need to also have evidence of a _Strep. pyogenes_ infection:
- Positive thraot culture
- Positive streptococcal antigen test
- Or positive ASO or ADB titres 

Antibiotic prophylaxis following an episode:
- Possible ARF without cardiac involvement -> 12 months
- Probable ARF without cardiac involvement -> 5y or until age 21y (whichever is longer)
- Definite ARF without cardiac involvement -> 5y or until age 21y (whichever is longer)
- Definite ARF **with** cardiac involvement -> 10y or until age 21y (whichever is longer)
