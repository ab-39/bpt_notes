### Overview 
One particularly clinically relevant β-lactamase gene is the chromosomal gene _ampC_, which confers a unique resistance profile with a few clues to tell it apart:
- _ampC_ is usually resistant to **cefoxitin** _in vitro_, while ESBLs are not 
- _ampC_ is most commonly an inducible gene in certain specific species - the _ESCHAPPM_ organisms:
  - **_Enterobacter_**
  - **_Serratia marcescens_**
  - **_Citrobacter freundii_**
  - _Hafnia alvei_
  - _Acinetobacter_ and _Aeromonas_
  - _Proteus vulgaris_
  - _Providencia_
  - _Morganella morganii_ 
- Inducible -> may be susceptible in vitro, but will be induced with treatment in vivo
- Cefepime tends to remain active against *ampC* producers **but not** ESBLs
- _ampC_ is not inhibited by β-lactamase inhibitors such as clavulanate _in vitro_ while ESBLs are (but _in vivo_, both tend to be resistant)
  - i.e. _ampC_ producers may demonstrate sensitivity to Augmentin on testing, which is a furphy
