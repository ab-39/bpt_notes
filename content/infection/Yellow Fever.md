### Overview
Yellow fever is an illness with a broad range of severity that is caused by a flavivirus spread by the mosquito _Aedes aegypti_. It has a short incubation period (3-5 days). The presentation can range from mild (fevers, myalgias, etc.) to severe, which is classically a triphasic illness:
1. High fevers (~40ºC) with headache, myalgias, etc. Faget’s sign (bradycardia). Usually lasts 4-5 days. 
2. ‘Phase of calm’
3. Increasing fever, jaundice, bleeding -> coma and death
The mortality rate of severe disease is as high as 40%. Diagnosis is generally clinical, although the virus can be isolated from the blood in the first three days of the illness. Management is supportive. Prevention is with the yellow fever [[Vaccines|vaccine]], which is a live vaccine (it is contraindicated in patients with anaphylaxis to egg).
