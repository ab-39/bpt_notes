# Strongyloides Hyperinfection
#bpt/infection
- - -
### Overview
_Strongyloides stercoralis_ is a small worm that, once it has infected a human, lives in the small bowel. It is of clinical significant as immunosuppression can lead to _vast_ numbers of worms crossing the gut epithelium, leading to overwhelming and often fatal generalised infection (hyperinfection). Treatment is with ivermectin, and antibiotics with good Gram-negative coverage should be also used in hyperinfection to cover translocated gut flora.
