# Lyme Disease
#bpt/infection
- - -
### Overview
A tick-borne illness caused by spirochaetes of the genus _Borrellia_. Characteristic signs and symptoms include:
- Target rash
- Erythema migrans
- Fever
- Fatigue
- Headache
- Myalgia
- Lymphadenopathy
- Neuroborreliosis -> lymphocytic meningitis, cranial nerve palsies, encephalomyelitis
- Carditis -> AV bock +/- myopericarditis.
Diagnosis is usually clinical, although serology +/- CSF sampling can be used as necessary. Treatment of early disease is typically with PO amoxicillin or doxycycline, while advanced disease usually warrants a 2-4 course of IV ceftriaxone. Isolated cranial nerve palsies may be treated with doxycycline.
