# Hepatitis E
#bpt/gastroenterology #bpt/infection
- - -
### Overview
Hepatitis E is an RNA hepevirus that is functionally quite similar to [[gastroenterology/Hepatitis A|hepatitis A]], with the main difference being that some animals (rodents, swine, deer) can also serve as a reservoir. It is spread by the faecal-oral route, and causes fulminant liver failure in 1-2% of patients. The main demographic at risk are the immunocompromised and women who are pregnant, in whom the rate of [[acute liver failure]] is as high as 20%. Disease does not progress to chronic hepatitis except in rare cases involving heavily immunosuppressed patients (e.g. transplant patients). Diagnosis is with PCR or serology, and treatment is supportive.
