### Overview
#### Bullous Pemphigoid
Chronic relapsing-remitting autoimmune blistering disease characterised by multiple large, tense, pruritic vesicles or bullae. More common in the elderly. DIF shows linear deposits of IgG and/or complement C3 along the basement membrane zone, while histopathology reveals subepidermal blistering with eosinophilic-predominant infiltration. Serology shows antibodies against BP230 and/or BP180.

#### Pemphigus Vulgaris 
Autoimmune blistering disease characterised by flaccid, mucocutaneous blisters that rupture easily and form painful erosions. DIF shows reticular IgG and complement C3 deposits on keratinocytes while histopathology reveals intraepidermal acanthocytosis with only occasional infiltrates. Serology shows antibodies against desmoglein 1 and/or desmoglein 3. Initial management is with steroids with rituximab to maintain remission (PEMPHIX trial). 

#### Dermatitis Herpetiformis 
A cutaneous manifestation of [[coeliac disease]].
