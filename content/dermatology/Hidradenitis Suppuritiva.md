# Hidradenitis Suppuritiva
#bpt/dermatology
- - -
### Overview
Hidradenitis suppurtiva is a chronic inflammatory skin disorder that affects skin with _apocrine_ sweat glands - typically the axillae, groin, perianal region, buttocks, and inframammary folds. It is charactterised by deep, painful nodules and abscesses that can progress to tract formation, purulent discharge, and scarring.

Risk factors:
- Female sex
- Family history
- Obesity
- Cigarette smoking
- Mechanical stress

Management is generally with topical creams and management of comorbid conditions. If this fails, systemic antibiotics or adalimumab can be trialled.
